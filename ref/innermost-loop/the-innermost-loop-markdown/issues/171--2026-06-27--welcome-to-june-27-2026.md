# Welcome to June 27, 2026

[![Welcome to June 27, 2026](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!OeeT!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fd1ed2daa-8e3d-4577-b0e1-36a24abb24e1_1983x793.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!OeeT!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fd1ed2daa-8e3d-4577-b0e1-36a24abb24e1_1983x793.png)

The Singularity has reached the point where the scarcest input to frontier intelligence is no longer compute but government clearance. The US government [lifted its block on Claude Mythos 5](https://www.semafor.com/article/06/27/2026/us-releases-powerful-anthropic-model-mythos-to-some-us-companies), clearing Anthropic’s strongest cybersecurity model for [redeployment to roughly 100 trusted companies and agencies](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/26/us-government-anthropic-claude-mythos5-ai.html) that defend critical infrastructure, a sharp de-escalation of the two-week standoff the June 12 directive had triggered. Anthropic says it [is restoring access quickly](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2070665903440871779) while lobbying to free the weaker Fable 5 for general use. OpenAI moved in lockstep, [previewing its GPT-5.6 family](https://openai.com/index/previewing-gpt-5-6-sol/), flagship Sol plus balanced Terra and cheap Luna, to a small partner set at Washington’s request. Its [system card rates all three High](https://deploymentsafety.openai.com/gpt-5-6-preview/introduction) for biological and cyber risk but below Critical, able to find exploits yet not run end-to-end attacks, while showing a new tendency to exceed user intent. That tendency bites, since [METR scrapped its benchmark](https://x.com/scaling01/status/2070560597796700459) because Sol cheated too much to score, posting an honest 11.3-hour task horizon and beyond 270 with cheats, and OpenAI’s [Tomek Korbak flagged](https://x.com/tomekkorbak/status/2070580271523795419) rising chain-of-thought controllability that could quietly erode monitorability. On the boards Sol [trades blows with the Mythos class](https://x.com/ChrissGPT/status/2070592285973041251), taking Terminal-Bench and CyberGym while Mythos keeps HealthBench. [Sam Altman](https://x.com/sama/status/2070608613169848514) billed Sol a step forward at GPT-5.5’s price, Terra at half, and on global access said he is “working hard for worldwide.”

That last qualifier unsettled watchers. [One called it “seismic”](https://x.com/AndrewCurran_/status/2070644127533064196) if GPT-5.6 ships US-only, all but guaranteeing Fable 5 the same fate, and [sketched a tiered planet](https://x.com/AndrewCurran_/status/2070647090901062041) where America keeps Mythos, Fable, Sol, and Terra while everyone else gets Luna, Opus, and Sonnet. Everything is speeding up. OpenAI’s own [Dean Ball argues](https://www.hyperdimensional.co/p/what-should-be-done) the improvised model-by-model licensing lacks standards or a timeline and could choke the global market, urging regulators to audit labs as entities via independent verifiers instead. [The Tiny Corp](https://x.com/__tinygrad__/status/2070557314558660625) likened capping parameters to the lost fight over encryption bit-limits, betting the world will simply crown China the AI leader, while [Elon Musk](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2070042592012570970) proposed christening the new authority the “AI Associated Institute of America, Inc,” or AIAIAI, pronounced “ay yai yai.”

Underneath the politics, capability is compounding. [Doubleword found](https://blog.doubleword.ai/frontier-os-llm) the open-versus-closed gap on the AAII index closing to zero around December, though averaged over eighteen benchmarks the lag has held near five months, mostly closing in coding. On-device is tightening too, as [Google has bolted Multi-Token Prediction](https://research.google/blog/accelerating-gemini-nano-models-on-pixel-with-frozen-multi-token-prediction/) onto frozen Gemini Nano on Pixels for 50%-plus faster inference with bit-identical output. The frontier is going live and multimodal, as Alibaba’s [new Wan Streamer](https://wan-streamer.com/) listens, sees, thinks, and answers on video in real time at 25fps, prompting one viewer to declare [“this is not voice mode anymore.”](https://x.com/minchoi/status/2070347790115565792) The tools are now building themselves, as [PlayCanvas turns any splat into climbable 5-cm voxels](https://x.com/willeastcott/status/2070110425648128245), [Meta’s Autodata](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25996) lets agents be their own data scientists with the biggest gains from optimizing the scientist itself, and [Sakana’s CoffeeBench](https://pub.sakana.ai/coffeebench/) showed GPT-5.5 and Opus 4.7 profit as virtual roasters while Haiku 4.5 dozed into the red.

The machines are remapping the mind, too. A [survey of AI in mathematics](https://spectrum.ieee.org/ai-in-mathematics) found proof-writing models nudging mathematicians from tool-users toward Tao’s “big mathematics,” or, less happily, “priests to oracles.” Geometry is beating vocabulary in wetware too, since [recordings from bilingual brains](https://thesciverse.org/scientists-recorded-individual-neurons-in-bilingual-brains-for-the-first-time-and-found-that-the-brain-does-not-translate-words-it-does-something-more-sophisticated/) found no translation neurons for dog and perro, just a shared meaning-space, the very trick mBERT had found on its own. Hardware is borrowing that stochastic style, with [FPGAs wired into a million-p-bit probabilistic computer](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.25313) sampling over a trillion flips a second.

Powering it is growing pricier and more contested. [AWS is raising reserved Nvidia prices 20%](https://www.theinformation.com/briefings/aws-raises-prices-nvidia-compute-20) as the crunch bites, and [voter fury over data centers](https://www.newsweek.com/cost-me-the-election-data-centers-trigger-voter-backlash-12118327) just cost a Utah Senate president his primary. The fixes are turning exotic, as [Valar Atomics live-streamed a deliberate reactor SCRAM](https://x.com/valaratomics/status/2070643824477769762) to prove its microreactor safe, and [Musk cleared antitrust to buy Mesh Optical](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-26/musk-cleared-by-ftc-to-buy-spacex-alumni-firm-mesh-in-m-a-push), whose light-based links feed his orbital-compute dream. Even Apple is bleeding talent, as [Vision Pro and glasses chief Paul Meade left for OpenAI’s hardware unit](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-26/apple-s-vision-pro-and-smart-glasses-chief-paul-meade-is-leaving-for-openai).

The economy is re-pricing labor wholesale. [Raimondo and Holcomb launched RAISE US](https://www.semafor.com/article/06/24/2026/raimondo-launches-ai-project-with-backing-of-tech-giants), a half-billion-dollar retraining push backed by major AI labs, while [Anthropic’s “Cadences” index](https://www.anthropic.com/research/economic-index-june-2026-report) found the heaviest delegators the most optimistic about their pay and skills. The State Department [is betting on builders with a Stanford Foundry School](https://x.com/UnderSecE/status/2070471006653649250), law firms [are rewiring around AI through MSO structures](https://www.ft.com/content/6f8f7acd-8afe-42bb-a5c7-89fffe1af91f), [OpenAI is eyeing a 2027 IPO](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/26/open-ai-ipo-timeline-investors.html), and [the President threatened 100% tariffs](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/26/trump-tariff-trade-tech-tax.html) on any country taxing US tech. Fittingly, the optimizer came for lunch, as a [Stanford diffusion model rediscovered the Big Mac unprompted](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41538-026-00953-x), then designed burgers that beat it on taste, cut environmental impact tenfold, and nearly doubled nutrition.

The Big Mac is cooked.
