# Welcome to June 21, 2026

[![Welcome to June 21, 2026](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!I_kO!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F181f6eb1-796e-4151-9dcc-c7c78f9bdfcf_1983x793.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!I_kO!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F181f6eb1-796e-4151-9dcc-c7c78f9bdfcf_1983x793.png)

The Singularity is learning good taste. OpenAI’s Codex engineers teased the next leap, with Thibault Sottiaux promising that once models [improve front-end capabilities](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2068568650924409260), “That day will be something.” Teammate Victor Nunez agreed [visual taste](https://x.com/nunezvice/status/2068644690631541119) was the loudest ask. The frontier still leads, but open weights trail by inches, not miles. Vercel’s CEO, [almost shocked](https://x.com/rauchg/status/2068517095818809770) at GLM-5.2’s coding, declared “This changes things.” The model also placed [second behind Claude](https://x.com/lechmazur/status/2068428300460974279) on a debate benchmark. But capability cuts both ways. The general who led the NSA reportedly told a senator that Anthropic’s Mythos cracked nearly every [classified system it touched](https://x.com/shashj/status/2068704535124508717), “not in weeks, but in hours.” In apparent response to export controls on Fable 5 and Mythos 5, Anthropic amended its [privacy policy](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/10301952-updates-to-our-privacy-policy) to warn it may “ask you to confirm your age or identity,” via an [ID-and-selfie check](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/14328960-identity-verification-on-claude) it vows will never train a model.

Those models are muscling into discovery itself. OpenAI unveiled [LifeSciBench](https://openai.com/index/introducing-life-sci-bench/), 750 expert biology tasks, where GPT-Rosalind raised the pass rate to 36.1% from GPT-5.5’s 25.7%, strong on writing yet weak on design. For agents to cooperate they must first find each other, so Google released the open [Agentic Resource Discovery](https://developers.googleblog.com/announcing-the-agentic-resource-discovery-specification/) spec to publish and cryptographically verify AI tools and skills. The branding follows, OpenAI [swapping its ChatGPT billboards](https://x.com/ParkerOrtolani/status/2068428560075801023) for Codex ones, retiring the chatbot for the agent. Not every rollout is welcome. Kansas City will put [facial-recognition cameras](https://apnews.com/article/kansas-city-facial-recognition-ai-cameras-privacy-87847f57c94b6c2a9e22a7b3a222e703) on public buses to match riders to watch lists, with advocates protesting making residents “guinea pigs.”

Robotics is having an architectural reformation. At a Sequoia conference, Nvidia’s Jim Fan declared [“Rest in Peace, VLAs,”](https://x.com/michellelsun/status/2068678318560575499) arguing that vision-language-action models are brittle and the field is converging on physics-grounded world models that favor sample efficiency over brute-force token volume. That gospel already pays off. [Spiced self-play](https://spiced-self-play.com/) hit 99.4% safe driving on 2,500x less human data, while Ant Group and Stanford’s $1,900 [UME exoskeleton](https://ume-exo.github.io/) lets people teach robots contact-rich tasks by feel, even blindfolded. Research is hardening into product, as Sanctuary AI ran its [Physical AI](https://sanctuary.ai/news/sanctuary-ai-expands-physical-ai-strategy-to-industrial-robotics-demonstrating-production-ready-ai-performance/) on factory arms, plugging flexible wires on a live conveyor at a 99.5% rate, while GM adds [cobots](https://www.crainsdetroit.com/manufacturing-logistics/automotive/cdb-gm-cobots-rankle-uaw-20260616/) at its Detroit truck plant, irking the UAW. Soon you will just talk to them, as Elon Musk says that within [three months](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2067430722399539385) Grok will steer Tesla FSD like a backseat rider saying “drop us off right here.”

Feeding all that compute looks less ruinous than feared. An instrumental-variables study found data centers [nudged US power rates down](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.19777) from 2015 to 2024, as durable demand spreads a grid’s fixed costs wider. Tesla means to be that grid, trademarking [“MEGAPOD,”](https://x.com/bulltheoryio/status/2068569421971436011) modular hardware turning its Supercharger network into distributed AI compute, plus the blunt [“AMAZING ABUNDANCE,”](https://tmsearch.uspto.gov/search/search-results/99894747) for solar roofing, robots, and batteries. That bounty reaches the oil patch too, as the US has become the [world’s largest oil exporter](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/once-an-arab-oil-embargo-victim-us-becomes-worlds-top-oil-exporter-2026-06-11/) at 10.5 million barrels a day, after the US-Iran war throttled Saudi output and drones gutted Russian flows, a reversal for an old embargo victim.

Distance is collapsing, too. A hypersonic project named [Invictus](https://www.telegraph.co.uk/news/2026/06/20/london-to-sydney-flights-twice-as-fast-as-concorde-on-the-h/), built on a pre-cooled hydrogen engine that chills superheated air in a flash, aims to fly London to Sydney at twice Concorde’s pace, with hypersonic tests due by 2036.

Closer to home, biology is being re-permissioned. HHS asked the FDA to [loosen testosterone limits](https://nypost.com/2026/06/19/health/trump-admin-seeks-to-roll-back-testosterone-restrictions-what-to-know/) for older men, citing a 5,200-man trial that found no real rise in heart attacks or strokes, splitting clinicians. Diagnosis is decentralizing too, as an MIT researcher launches [LymeAlert](https://www.cbsnews.com/boston/news/lyme-disease-at-home-tick-test/), a 15-minute home test that reads a tick to catch the 72-hour window, with 5,000 unapproved kits shipping in August.

The economics of abundance have become a political brawl. The Vice President said the President backs [government stakes](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/macro-economic-events/26/06/60005043/jd-vance-says-trump-backs-government-stakes-in-ai-giants-but-musk-and-cuban-push-back-better-just-to-send-money-directly) in big AI firms, a sovereign-wealth play Mark Cuban waved off as “not a plan” without hundreds of billions more. Elon Musk preferred to [send money straight to people](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2068427440473452739), betting that as robots outpace the money supply, “we will desperately be fighting deflation!” But abundance has to be built before it is divided, so Open X is opening [Skill Factory #000](https://x.com/atomsnotbits/status/2068529883245056324) in Texas in July to train 10,000 workers a year. Not everyone trusts the builders, and Democrats launched the [Guardrails Alliance](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/06/18/technology/ai-super-pac-guardrails-alliance.html) super PAC to raise $15 million for AI-safety laws against a $100 million industry war chest. In the classroom the line has dissolved, as [humanizers and autotypers](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/06/18/us/ai-apps-students-cheat.html) rework AI essays and add fake typos in real time, so teachers can’t tell who wrote the homework.

To err is human, but to humanize is now a download.
