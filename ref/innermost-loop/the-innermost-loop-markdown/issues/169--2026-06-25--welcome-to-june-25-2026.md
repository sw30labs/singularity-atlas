# Welcome to June 25, 2026

[![Welcome to June 25, 2026](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!pYbv!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F9426579a-75c7-42a8-98c5-aeb2dc48d380_1983x793.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!pYbv!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F9426579a-75c7-42a8-98c5-aeb2dc48d380_1983x793.png)

The Singularity has begun editing its own source code. A new [Self-Harness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09498) paradigm lets an agent mine its weaknesses and rewrite its scaffolding, lifting Terminal-Bench scores by double digits with no human engineers. The interface is racing to match it, as OpenAI’s Greg Brockman previewed [bi-directional voice](https://x.com/Kantrowitz/status/2069828651210920410) that processes input while it speaks, and the lab shipped a warmer [GPT-5.5 Instant](https://x.com/openai/status/2069843083701915755) that better reads intent. Perception fuses with action, as Google bakes [computer use](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-computer-use-gemini-3-5-flash/) into Gemini 3.5 Flash so agents see and click any screen, and [Krea 2](https://www.krea.ai/blog/krea-2-technical-report) open-sources SOTA text-to-image. Even pricing pages turn prophetic, as new [Claude Code strings](https://x.com/synthwavedd/status/2069813760622043483) hint Fable 5 returns to subscriptions.

Discovery is onboarding silicon collaborators. Ed Witten for the first time credited [Claude Opus 4.8](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18639) with generalizing an entanglement-wedge calculation. Biology is industrializing the same way, as NVIDIA’s [BioNeMo Agent Toolkit](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/build-an-ai-scientist-for-life-science-discovery-with-nvidia-bionemo-agent-toolkit/) turns biomolecular models into callable Skills, lifting completion to 100 percent. Stanford’s Brian Hie unveiled [Proto](https://proto.evodesign.org/about), a language composing DNA, RNA, and proteins into validated designs, and [Bio IQ](https://www.aiiq.org/bio/) launched leaderboards for life-science and biosecurity.

As the models grow, the fights over their souls intensify. xAI is leaning into [video and image generation](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/xai-bets-groks-racy-side) after most of Grok’s traffic proved racy, while Meta quietly codes [a prediction-market app](https://www.npr.org/2026/06/24/nx-s1-5869486/meta-prediction-market-app-ai) where Llama writes and settles the bets live. The bias question stays unsettled, as one [audit](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/interactive/2026/06/24/are-ai-chatbots-like-chatgpt-politically-biased-we-tested-them/) found ChatGPT mostly left-leaning, Gemini neutral, and even Grok citing the left more. Trust is becoming infrastructure, as Cloudflare and the major browsers build [personhood tokens](https://www.theregister.com/software/2026/06/22/cloudflare-teams-up-with-big-browsers-to-help-websites-tell-welcome-from-unwelcome-visitors/5259782) to sort humans and authorized bots from malicious traffic, even as Anthropic accused Alibaba’s Qwen of the largest known [distillation attack](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-24/anthropic-accuses-alibaba-of-illicitly-accessing-its-ai-models), 28.8 million harvested exchanges. Frontier labs now hire [philosophers](https://www.economist.com/science-and-technology/2026/06/24/why-big-ai-labs-are-hiring-so-many-philosophers) to write AI constitutions while two more [Gemini staffers](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-24/google-poised-to-lose-two-more-high-profile-ai-staffers-to-anthropic) were poached by Anthropic, whose envoy Tom Brown reportedly [charmed a White House](https://www.wired.com/story/the-trump-white-house-is-over-anthropics-dario-amodei/) cool on Dario Amodei.

Silicon is where the capital is detonating. Japan’s PM Takaichi unveiled a [$2.3 trillion](https://cryptobriefing.com/japan-23-trillion-ai-semiconductor-investment-plan/) plan to quintuple chip output, lofting the Nikkei past 72,000. Memory is the new oil, as Micron became [tech’s margin king](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/24/micron-is-techs-margin-king-memory-crisis-pushes-it-past-nvidia-meta.html) at a record 84.9 percent, posted [record revenue](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/24/micron-mu-earnings-report-q3-2026.html) and a trillion-dollar cap on 7x data growth, and noted a [humanoid robot](https://x.com/PolymarketMoney/status/2069894407554552139) holds ten times a car’s memory. Inference is going custom, as Qualcomm bought [Modular](https://www.reuters.com/business/qualcomm-buy-ai-startup-modular-2026-06-24/) to challenge CUDA and unveiled its [Dragonfly C1000](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/24/qualcomm-data-center-cpu-meta.html) CPU with Meta as first customer, while OpenAI and Broadcom taped out [Jalapeño](https://openai.com/index/openai-broadcom-jalapeno-inference-chip/), OpenAI’s first inference chip, in nine months. The map is hardening too, as the EU signed the US-led [Pax Silica](https://www.euractiv.com/news/eu-signs-us-pax-silica-initiative-singling-out-china-on-ai-chips/) pact against China and agreed to buy $40 billion in US chips.

Powering it all is an industry. Sunrun, Tesla, and Renew Home are stitching home batteries and EVs into [16 gigawatts](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/06/24/3316793/0/en/sunrun-renew-home-and-tesla-team-up-to-deliver-more-than-16-gigawatts-of-fast-flexible-power-for-data-centers-and-large-loads.html) of flexible capacity, Walmart signed its first [nuclear deal](https://corporate.walmart.com/news/2026/06/23/constellation-and-walmart-announce-longterm-agreement-to-support-reliable-emissionsfree-nuclear-energy-in-illinois), and NVIDIA-backed [Eco Wave Power](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/eco-wave-power-ai-digital-twins/) aims to run a data center on ocean swells. Compute itself becomes tradable, as a16z funds [Ornn](https://a16zcrypto.com/posts/article/investing-in-ornn) to offer the first modern exchange for compute as a commodity. Cheap electrons mean cheap miles, as Bezos-backed [Slate Auto](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/24/slate-autos-radically-simple-electric-truck-starts-at-24950/) opened orders for a $24,950 pickup.

The machines are stepping off the assembly line. Amazon’s Zoox revealed a [production-intent robotaxi](https://zoox.com/journal/the-updated-zoox-robotaxi) at a hundred a week, Agility’s Digit is going [public via SPAC](https://www.wsj.com/finance/agility-maker-of-humanlike-robots-to-go-public-in-2-5-billion-spac-deal-62c3cb32) at $2.5 billion, and 1X’s Bernt Bornich is [open-handing NEO](https://x.com/BerntBornich/status/2069814847525814456) to developers, calling a Western humanoid platform too vital to gatekeep.

We are also rewriting the wetware. Elon Musk says Neuralink may attempt [brain-to-brain telepathy](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2069687831375831179) through latent space this year. Pharma chases subtler upgrades, as Eli Lilly closed its [Centessa](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/lilly-completes-acquisition-of-centessa-pharmaceuticals-to-advance-treatments-for-sleep-wake-disorders-302808668.html) buy, possibly betting an [orexin agonist](https://x.com/MTSlive/status/2069897087710535845) could let anyone feel rested on four hours’ sleep. Prevention is scaling too, with Stripe, Anthropic, and OpenAI backing [Intercept](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/06/24/1139621/stripe-anthropic-and-openai-are-backing-an-effort-to-stop-respiratory-infections/), a $500 million push against respiratory viruses, while an AI-ECG, [EchoNext](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-026-04454-y), caught hidden heart disease and triggered a transplant, and the FDA dropped its complaint against [Whoop’s blood-pressure feature](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-23/fda-drops-complaint-against-whoop-s-blood-pressure-tracking-tool).

Orbit is opening as a factory floor. SpaceX [confirmed deployment](https://x.com/SpaceX/status/2069420637383168301) of Starfall, a [reentry capsule](https://arstechnica.com/space/2026/06/with-starfall-spacex-eyes-an-edge-in-global-cargo-delivery-from-orbit/) that could corner global cargo return and orbital manufacturing, which rival [startups](https://payloadspace.com/spacex-starfall-demo-provides-validation-for-reentry-startups/) cheered as proof the market is real. Payloads turn playful, as [Starbase Brewing](https://x.com/StarbaseBrewing/status/2069850769772396851) flew dozens of yeast strains and Texas bluebonnet seeds to study spaceflight.

Even the oldest secrets are being pried open, as Rep. Eric Burlison is [subpoenaing](https://x.com/EricBurlison/status/2069785870983463363) post-Manhattan-Project labs, says [MIT Lincoln Labs](https://x.com/EricBurlison/status/2069804494464970864) admitted holding a video file he named, and the [Disclosure Forum 2026](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3004GwnxwzM) convenes live in the Senate today on UAPs.

Meanwhile, the circle of rights is widening. Quebec’s Terrasse-Vaudreuil became the first Canadian town to recognize [trees as living beings](https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/montreal/terrasse-vaudreil-quebec-tree-rights-9.7243634), granting them “the right to life, to natural growth, to integrity and to regeneration.”

I think that I shall never see a person lovely as a tree.
