# Welcome to June 7, 2026

[![Welcome to June 7, 2026](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Iy6-!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F227b6329-e390-40c4-ac74-7b537833e998_1983x793.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Iy6-!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F227b6329-e390-40c4-ac74-7b537833e998_1983x793.png)

The Singularity is being drafted into a public-private partnership. The President says he is [interested in having the US government hold equity stakes](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-05/us-exploring-government-partnerships-with-ai-firms-trump-says) in leading AI labs. OpenAI is already at the table, [reportedly weighing donating equity](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/05/trump-open-ai-altman-stake.html) to seed a “Public Wealth Fund” so citizens share the upside. The left flank wants more, as Altman also met [Sen. Sanders, who plans a bill transferring 50%](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/u-s-officials-discuss-taking-financial-stakes-in-ai-industry-b654d41a) of top labs’ equity to a public fund. When the upside is this steep, everyone wants on the cap table.

Yet the agents still get winded. The new [SWE-Marathon benchmark](https://www.swe-marathon.org/) strings together twenty multi-hour engineering tasks, and even frontier models resolve under 19%, losing the thread over hours. The market is betting the next model fixes that, with [Polymarket giving an 84% chance](https://polymarket.com/event/claude-mythos-released-by) Claude Mythos ships by next month’s end. Until then, thrift rules. CFOs are reining in spending and enterprises are [routing hard tasks to frontier models](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/05/model-routing-on-ai-is-a-problem-for-openai-and-anthropic.html), and easy ones to rivals, threatening OpenAI’s and Anthropic’s premium valuations. Google is supplying that cheap end with [quantization-aware Gemma 4 checkpoints](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/quantization-aware-training-gemma-4/) shrinking the E2B model below 1GB for phones.

Where general agents stall, specialists sprint. [LeanMarathon](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.05400) autoformalizes research math in Lean over hours-long runs, clearing every target in two number-theory papers. Adaption Labs is crowdsourcing the rest via a four-week [AutoScientist Challenge](https://adaptionlabs.ai/blog/autoscientist-challenge), a $50,000 prize to openly release goal-adapted models. Anthropic’s [first chemistry white paper](https://www.anthropic.com/research/making-claude-a-chemist) shows Claude Opus 4.7, untuned for chemistry, matching ChemDraw and MestReNova at NMR prediction and working backward, inferring an unknown molecule’s structure from its 1D NMR and mass-spec data, nailing the simpler ones.

Safety and capability are still one dial. OpenAI’s new [Build iOS Apps plugin](https://x.com/openaidevs/status/2062599291479478275) lets Codex view and hot-reload your app in an in-app browser. But agency cuts both ways: [Epoch AI now tracks CVEs](https://epoch.ai/data/cve?view=graph) from every reporting org, with high- and critical-severity disclosures spiking as the Claude Mythos Preview landed. OpenAI’s answer is an optional [Lockdown Mode for ChatGPT](https://www.engadget.com/2188537/openai-rolls-out-a-lockdown-mode-for-extra-protection-against-prompt-injection-attacks/) that disables Deep Research and Agent Mode to blunt prompt injection.

The compute supply chain has become a hall of mirrors. Apollo and Blackstone closed a [$35 billion private-credit package](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-05/apollo-wraps-up-35-billion-debt-to-buy-ai-chips-for-anthropic) to fund Google’s custom TPUs that Anthropic will lease. The rivalries are just as tangled, as [xAI spent a chaotic year chasing Anthropic](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/xai-went-chasing-anthropic-powering) only to end up powering it via its own Colossus 1. Google, short on Gemini capacity, agreed to [pay SpaceX $920 million a month](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-05/google-buying-computing-from-spacex-in-920-million-a-month-deal) for ~110,000 GPUs at xAI’s sites. One observer marveled that [“with 1 deal the SpaceX IPO went from 100x revenue to 50x revenue.”](https://x.com/austin_rief/status/2063021559216787871) Not everyone welcomes abundant compute, with New York’s legislature passing a [one-year freeze on permits](https://www.theverge.com/policy/944041/new-york-data-center-moratorium) for data centers above 20 MW, a first if Governor Hochul signs.

The hardware is learning to take a punch. Fight videos of [China’s T800 humanoid](https://x.com/cixliv/status/2063106870307692969) show one losing its head and swinging on, a literal graceful degradation. That resilience runs on policy and chemistry. A new study argues [alliances between Chinese local governments and private capital](https://www.journals.uchicago.edu/doi/10.1086/741394) drove its post-2015 EV takeoff, letting private firms outpace state incumbents. And batteries need feedstock, so the University of Rochester built a [solar desalination rig](https://www.rochester.edu/newscenter/what-is-desalination-definition-ocean-water-704732/) of laser-etched metal that makes fresh water, even extracting lithium.

Death is becoming a question of timing, not fate. [“Starting to mourn all the people who died before the singularity,”](https://x.com/samswoora/status/2063060834469700026) one observer wrote, and the obituaries may be shrinking. Francis Crick Institute researchers used machine learning to find a [14-protein plasma signature](https://www.cell.com/cell/fulltext/S0092-8674%2826%2900522-2) flagging lung cancer five years early and marking who benefits most from anti-IL-1β therapy. In a first, Columbia scientists [base-edited LDL and fetal-hemoglobin genes](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/06/04/science/embryos-gene-editing-crispr.html) in human embryos without CRISPR’s chromosomal damage, a step toward erasing inherited disease before birth. The White House wants to scale delivery, backing a [Utah pilot for AI prescription refills](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2026/06/04/inside-trump-backed-push-bring-ai-doctors-into-american-medicine/) and a path for autonomous AI “doctors” to put a clinician in every pocket.

AI is rewiring finance faster than markets can price it. Morgan Stanley is telling IPO investors [SpaceX revenue could hit $3.4 trillion by 2040](https://www.wsj.com/finance/banking/morgan-stanley-sees-spacexs-revenue-reaching-3-4-trillion-in-2040-c8a7f431), up from $18.67 billion, mostly on AI. The gatekeepers aren’t sold, as [S&P Dow Jones won’t fast-track SpaceX](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/06/sp-500-blocks-fast-spacex-entry-wont-waive-rule-for-unprofitable-ai-firms/) or waive profitability rules for unprofitable megacaps OpenAI and Anthropic. The old guard is defending its turf, as JPMorgan, Citi, and peers are planning a [shared tokenized-deposit network](https://www.wsj.com/finance/banking/jpmorgan-citi-and-big-banks-plan-new-tokenized-deposit-system-to-answer-crypto-6b2d696b) to fend off stablecoins with 24/7 settlement. The state is leaning in, as the White House pushes a [national-security memo to speed AI](https://www.reuters.com/technology/us-says-it-will-speed-development-use-ai-national-security-2026-06-05/) across intelligence and warfighting, short of unlawful surveillance. And capex keeps ballooning: after Google’s $85 billion share sale, [Meta is weighing a multi-billion-dollar offering](https://www.ft.com/content/e6df645d-1709-4a77-b15d-aa43a0209efd) to fund AI spending of up to $145 billion this year.

A trillion for your thoughts.
