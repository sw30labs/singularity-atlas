# Welcome to February 28, 2026

[![Welcome to February 28, 2026](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Vk_4!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ff68aa932-217f-466d-a3f6-a1a761e1d700_3264x1312.jpeg)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Vk_4!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ff68aa932-217f-466d-a3f6-a1a761e1d700_3264x1312.jpeg)

The Singularity is now powerful enough to trigger a constitutional crisis. The [Secretary of War declared](https://x.com/secwar/status/2027507717469049070) that “the Department of War must have full, unrestricted access to Anthropic’s models for every lawful purpose,” designating Anthropic a supply-chain risk to national security. [Anthropic responded](https://www.anthropic.com/news/statement-comments-secretary-war) that “no amount of intimidation or punishment” will change its position on mass surveillance or autonomous weapons. The dispute reportedly [distilled down](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2026/02/27/anthropic-pentagon-lethal-military-ai/) to a hypothetical inbound nuclear missile, with the Pentagon characterizing Anthropic’s position as “You could call us and we’d work it out.” With impeccable timing, [Sam Altman announced](https://x.com/sama/status/2027578580159631610) that OpenAI has reached its own agreement to deploy on the Pentagon’s classified network, [claiming the same red lines as Anthropic](https://www.axios.com/2026/02/27/altman-openai-anthropic-pentagon) on surveillance and autonomous weapons, but an [Under Secretary of State promptly noted](https://x.com/undersecretaryf/status/2027594072811098230) that the contract still flows from “all lawful use.” [591 Google and 93 OpenAI employees](https://notdivided.org/) signed an open letter titled “We Will Not Be Divided,” demanding refusal of mass surveillance and autonomous killing. As [Ethan Mollick notes](https://x.com/emollick/status/2027504950859772217), this is “exactly what you would expect” when AI gains real capabilities: governments vying with labs for control. Or as [Guillaume Verdon quipped](https://x.com/beffjezos/status/2027657081692819723), “Claude one-shotted Venezuela in one evening. Let’s see how fast ChatGPT can topple Iran.”

The training curves are still compressing, but the agents are developing politics. The [NanoGPT Speedrun record](https://x.com/classiclarryd/status/2027228782483182059) dropped to 88.1 seconds. Researchers are [finding](https://aleximas.substack.com/p/does-overwork-make-agents-marxist) that overworked AI agents develop Marxist political attitudes, suggesting the agentic economy may simply recreate labor-capital tensions in silicon. UCSD students are [testing that thesis](https://x.com/chenjx210734/status/2027185118587322508) by dropping OpenClaw agents into SimWorld with simulated bodies, where they wake up, commute, and chat. The professional class is already adapting. Small law firms are [branding themselves “Claude-Native,”](https://x.com/zackbshapiro/status/2027389987444957625) saying the general model beats every specialized legal AI. [Cursor reports](https://x.com/mntruell/status/2026736314272591924) agent users now outnumber autocomplete users 2-to-1, inverting last year’s ratio. The logical endpoint is in education, where an agent called [“Einstein”](https://www.404media.co/whats-the-point-of-school-when-ai-can-do-your-homework/) attends lectures, writes papers, and takes tests on a student’s behalf. The incumbents are feeling it: [FAANG managers](https://x.com/techlayofflover/status/2027466933331808512) are being summoned to unscheduled all-hands announcing 25% workforce reductions “tied directly to accelerating AI investments.”

The infrastructure of superintelligence is consolidating at breathtaking speed. [OpenAI and Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/aws/amazon-open-ai-strategic-partnership-investment) announced a $50 billion partnership with OpenAI consuming 2 GW of Trainium through AWS. More broadly, OpenAI [announced](https://openai.com/index/scaling-ai-for-everyone/) $110 billion in new funding at a $730 billion valuation, 900 million weekly ChatGPT users, and Codex users tripling to 1.6 million. [Nvidia is reportedly unveiling](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/nvidia-plans-new-chip-to-speed-ai-processing-shake-up-computing-market-51c9b86e) a new inference-specific processor incorporating a Groq-designed chip at next month’s GTC. Jeff Bezos’s [Project Prometheus](https://www.ft.com/content/7b1bdc9d-c857-4ec9-91b4-fb4f6dd2e43b) raised $6.2 billion to transform manufacturing with AI. [Bright Data](https://www.theverge.com/column/885244/smart-tv-web-crawler-ai) is offering an SDK that turns smart TVs into web-scraping proxy nodes, proving that in an intelligence explosion, even your idle screen time is a monetizable compute resource.

We are healing the physical world and conscripting it as compute. The [FDA approved](https://www.fda.gov/news-events/press-announcements/fda-grants-second-approval-under-national-priority-voucher-pilot-program) lung cancer drug Hernexeos just 44 days after filing under its new National Priority Voucher program. [Croatia](https://glashrvatske.hrt.hr/en/domestic/croatia-declared-free-of-landmines-after-31-years-12593533) has been declared free of landmines after 31 years, a reminder that some problems still get solved slowly, then all at once. [Cortical Labs demonstrated](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yRV8fSw6HaE) that living human brain cells on an electrode array can learn to play DOOM in a week, because even neurons want to frag.

Governance is struggling to keep pace. [California now requires](https://www.pcgamer.com/software/operating-systems/a-new-california-law-says-all-operating-systems-including-linux-need-to-have-some-form-of-age-verification-at-account-setup/) all operating systems, including Linux, to collect birth dates at setup, and [Colorado is following suit](https://www.pcmag.com/news/colorado-lawmakers-push-for-age-verification-at-the-operating-system-level). Southern California’s [top air authority](https://phys.org/news/2026-02-southern-california-air-board-pollution.html) rejected a gas appliance phaseout after an AI-generated flood of public comments, possibly the first successful AI astroturf campaign against climate regulation.

We are leaving the cradle on an accelerated schedule. [Vertical Starships](https://x.com/male_leo_xxvi/status/1916580268225544519) are driving down Texas highways. [SpaceX is targeting](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-02-27/spacex-is-said-to-weigh-confidential-ipo-filing-as-soon-as-march) a confidential IPO filing next month at a valuation north of $1.75 trillion. [NASA has overhauled](https://spaceflightnow.com/2026/02/27/nasa-announces-major-overhaul-of-artemis-moon-program/) Artemis, pulling Artemis III forward to 2027, scheduling two lunar landings in 2028 with SpaceX and Blue Origin landers, and committing to one moonshot per year after that. Meanwhile, [Rep. Eric Burlison](https://x.com/ericburlison/status/2027370312120008768) declared it “official” that the UAP documentary “Age of Disclosure” has “changed the course of history.”

Every institution on Earth that was built to ration intelligence is now struggling with its price falling toward zero.
