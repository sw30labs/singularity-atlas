# Welcome to March 22, 2026

[![Welcome to March 22, 2026](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!-F-O!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fd700b4b5-c382-4d15-acc4-151a2fdcbdd7_3264x1312.jpeg)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!-F-O!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fd700b4b5-c382-4d15-acc4-151a2fdcbdd7_3264x1312.jpeg)

The Singularity is building its own foundry. Elon Musk unveiled [TERAFAB](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2035506574182199757), a joint SpaceX/Tesla project aiming to produce over a terawatt of compute per year, 80% for space and 20% for Earth. SpaceX calls it [“the next step towards becoming a galactic civilization,”](https://x.com/spacex/status/2035519125284380672) designed to [“close the gap between today’s chip production and the future’s demand.”](https://x.com/spacex/status/2035542868790558771) Near Tesla’s Austin gigafactory, it will fabricate [2-nm chips in two flavors](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-22/elon-musk-says-tesla-xai-spacex-terafab-to-start-in-austin): edge silicon for Tesla’s robotaxis and Optimus robots, and high-power chips for SpaceX and xAI, including for a new mini AI data center satellite at 100 kW. Its planned [recursive design loop](https://x.com/cb_doge/status/2035552786331705757) will keep masks, fabrication, testing, and iteration in a single building. Tesla declared that [“to understand the universe, you must explore the universe,”](https://x.com/tesla/status/2035544492896669952) reading less like a slogan and more like a procurement order for the impending Dyson Swarm.

The models fueling that compute are getting lighter and longer-range. Chinese lab Evermind AI launched [Memory Sparse Attention](https://github.com/EverMind-AI/MSA), an architecture with under 9% degradation scaling from 16k to 100 million tokens, decoupling memory from reasoning. OpenAI is [“throwing everything”](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/03/20/1134438/openai-is-throwing-everything-into-building-a-fully-automated-researcher/) into building a fully automated AI researcher, targeting a research intern by September and a multi-agent system by 2028. Even the tooling layer is compressing: Cursor shipped [Composer 2](https://cursor.com/blog/composer-2), billing it as frontier-level coding at a fraction of the cost.

The agents are inheriting the web. Cloudflare’s CEO predicts [bot traffic will surpass human traffic online by 2027](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/19/online-bot-traffic-will-exceed-human-traffic-by-2027-cloudflare-ceo-says/). The bots are learning to blend in. Browser Use found [its agent was the stealthiest, accessing websites 81% of the time](https://x.com/reformedot/status/2035417245548151252). Democratization is accelerating. OpenClaw proved fully autonomous AI [can run at home without the big labs](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/21/openclaw-chatgpt-moment-sparks-concern-ai-models-becoming-commodities.html), and in China, [schoolkids and retirees alike are raising “lobsters”](https://www.reuters.com/technology/openclaw-enthusiasm-grips-china-schoolkids-retirees-alike-raise-lobsters-2026-03-19/) as the craze goes mainstream.

That democratization is cooking mathematics. Researchers built an agent that [generated 665 novel research problems in differential geometry](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18813), many unknown to experts. Terry Tao agrees, noting that [even high schoolers can now make real contributions to frontier math](https://x.com/andrewcurran_/status/2035049803676336230) thanks to AI tools.

The applications layer above is consolidating to match. OpenAI plans a desktop [“Superapp”](https://www.wsj.com/tech/openai-plans-launch-of-desktop-superapp-to-refocus-simplify-user-experience-9e19931d) merging ChatGPT, Codex, and its browser. WordPress.com now [lets AI agents draft, edit, and publish posts](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/20/wordpress-com-now-lets-ai-agents-write-and-publish-posts-and-more/). Google Search is [replacing news headlines with AI-generated text](https://www.theverge.com/tech/896490/google-replace-news-headlines-in-search-canary-coal-mine-experiment), turning the index itself into a generative layer.

The physical infrastructure is keeping pace. SoftBank is developing a [$500 billion, 10-gigawatt data center campus in Ohio](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-20/softbank-plans-giant-ohio-ai-data-center-powered-by-gas-plants), built on a [decommissioned uranium enrichment plant](https://apnews.com/article/ai-data-center-ohio-uranium-enrichment-4667fa1442ec1c652228337ab4eb68ee), and powered by natural gas. Not all infrastructure moves are legal, however. The co-founder of Super Micro Computer was [charged with diverting $2.5 billion in Nvidia AI chips to China](https://www.cnn.com/2026/03/19/politics/super-micro-computer-founder-charged-ai-chips-china).

Atoms are catching up to bits. China now fields [140 humanoid robotics companies](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/mar/19/inside-chinas-robotics-revolution). Arc Institute introduced [BioReason-Pro](https://arcinstitute.org/news/bioreason-pro), a model that predicts function for the 99.9% of proteins lacking experimental annotations. Nectome has [preserved a pig’s brain with minimal damage](https://www.newscientist.com/article/2520204-major-leap-towards-reanimation-after-death-as-mammals-brain-preserved/), now offering the technique to the terminally ill. AI optimization is extending to livestock and land. Halter’s [AI-powered solar collars](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-20/peter-thiel-s-founders-fund-backs-ai-cow-collar-startup-at-2-billion-valuation) now let ranchers herd cattle via vibrations from an app, valued at $2 billion. Making land post-scarce, Coastal Assembly [grew over 90 feet of new beach in six months](https://x.com/alexwg/status/2035349366081216769) at a Maldives resort using AI-optimized underwater structures to redirect sediment.

The same superintelligence that grows beaches and herds cattle is also selecting targets. Palantir’s Maven AI, which has [carried out thousands of targeted strikes against Iran](https://www.reuters.com/technology/pentagon-adopt-palantir-ai-as-core-us-military-system-memo-says-2026-03-20/), will become an official program of record across the U.S. military. Pervasive data cuts both ways, though. Le Monde [located France’s Charles de Gaulle aircraft carrier in real time](https://www.lemonde.fr/en/international/article/2026/03/20/stravaleaks-france-s-aircraft-carrier-located-in-real-time-by-le-monde-through-fitness-app_6751640_4.html) through a sailor’s Strava profile.

The economy is metabolizing disruption at machine speed. Ironically, OpenAI plans to [nearly double its headcount to 8,000](https://www.ft.com/content/7ffea5b4-e8bc-47cd-adb4-257f84c8028b) to sell the tools that replace humans. Capital is voting with its feet. Massachusetts residents [fled with $4.2 billion in income](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-20/massachusetts-lost-4-billion-of-income-after-millionaire-tax) after a millionaire surtax took effect. Walmart has [patented ML-driven dynamic pricing](https://www.ft.com/content/8c2338dc-9e2e-4561-955a-c2a6a6c4d28e). Europe, struggling to compete despite homegrown labs like Mistral, is trying to tax what it can’t build. Mistral’s own CEO argues [AI companies should pay a content levy](https://www.ft.com/content/d63d6291-687f-4e05-8b23-4d545d78c64a). Domestically, the White House released a [national AI policy framework](https://x.com/mkratsios47/status/2034969214537138370) to preempt a 50-state patchwork, betting unified rules will outrun fragmented ones. At Meta and OpenAI, employees now compete on [“tokenmaxxing” leaderboards](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/03/20/technology/tokenmaxxing-ai-agents.html), spending thousands a month automating work. Jensen Huang wants to go further, [proposing AI tokens as a salary supplement](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/20/nvidia-ai-agents-tokens-human-workers-engineer-jobs-unemployment-jensen-huang.html) and envisioning hundreds of thousands of Nvidia AI agents.

Every token consumed down here is a down payment on the terawatt up there.
