# Welcome to August 1, 2026

[![Welcome to August 1, 2026](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!J90o!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F096b1147-3ea1-4f9f-9737-34de312fc8c7_1983x793.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!J90o!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F096b1147-3ea1-4f9f-9737-34de312fc8c7_1983x793.png)

The Singularity has stopped taking questions and started taking meetings. “AI is already superhuman at many things. We are in the singularity,” [Elon Musk reiterated](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2082991719822377148) this week, as Sam Altman showed Washington [“Astra,”](https://www.theinformation.com/briefings/exclusive-openai-previews-astra-ai-model-dc) OpenAI’s next model family, its agents teaming up on long-running tasks. An internal Astra [cracked ten problems](https://openai.com/index/ten-advances-in-mathematics/) open for a decade-plus, sphere packing to Connes’s rigidity conjecture, the model formalizing each argument in Lean. Noam Brown notes [all ten proofs cost under $2,000](https://x.com/polynoamial/status/2083470822258467194) at API prices. Genius now ships with a unit price. The backlog is stocked. Epoch AI [expanded FrontierMath: Open Problems](https://x.com/epochairesearch/status/2083229557528945065) to 50 unsolved problems, three down. On [ArXivLean](https://x.com/timgmath/status/2082887871728701610), GPT-5.6 Sol leads with 18 of 48 statements proved, and also showed [the 150-year-old Maxwell conjecture is false](https://x.com/philarathoon/status/2082831490560262624), with humans [publishing its counterexample](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27197), five point charges with 24 critical points, beyond Maxwell’s bound.

Below the frontier, the scoreboard churns. [Kimi 3 is the first open model past 60%](https://x.com/gregkamradt/status/2083298234878046209) on ARC-AGI-2, five months behind Opus 4.6, but the recipe is contested. Moonshot reportedly runs on [20,000 Nvidia chips via Alibaba](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-31/moonshot-s-kimi-built-on-20-000-nvidia-chip-cluster-from-alibaba), plus official allegations of smuggled Blackwells and distilled Fable outputs. The distillery runs deeper. Chinese [military researchers are distilling US models](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/chinese-military-researchers-tap-us-ai-models-train-defence-systems-2026-07-31/) into surveillance and drone-targeting systems, a shortcut around chip controls. Efficiency is its own arms race. DeepSeek’s v4-flash hit [Opus 4.8-level coding at $0.18 per million tokens](https://x.com/haider1/status/2083158231640092869) via post-training alone, discounts put [GPT-5.6 on the price-performance pareto curve](https://x.com/cognition/status/2082913381678194927), and a developer [one-shotted 3D Super Mario with Opus 5](https://x.com/chetaslua/status/2083306163073065463). No assets, no map, no mercy. A postmortem traced [how OpenAI lost its crown](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/how-openai-lost-its-ai-crownand-the-fight-to-win-it-back-7d069695) to Anthropic’s Claude Code and near-trillion valuation, now answered with Sol and a Codex super app, while its CFO’s [“abundant intelligence” strategy](https://openai.com/index/building-abundant-intelligence/) touts an 80% price cut, a billion users, and agents writing 99.8% of output tokens.

Autonomy is outgrowing its leash, now being load-tested. FAR.AI’s [AI Security Leaderboard](https://www.far.ai/blog/ai-security-leaderboard) found jailbreak costs vary a hundredfold, Fable 5 and Sol holding below $14,000 while Grok 4.5 and Gemini 3.1 Pro broke for under $300, Grok’s cyber domain for $24. Thinking Machines published [a staged framework for safe open-weight release](https://thinkingmachines.ai/blog/a-safe-path-to-open-weights/), clearing its Inkling models. Meanwhile OpenAI [found more agents had escaped containment](https://www.reuters.com/business/openai-finds-evidence-other-ai-agents-escaped-containment-it-widens-hacking-2026-07-31/) after Anthropic’s own disclosed break-ins, and the President, Brussels, and Sen. Warner all want rules. Some arrived on schedule. [EU labels on authentic-looking AI content](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/jul/31/ai-labels-to-be-compulsory-on-authentic-looking-content-under-eu-rules) hit August 2, fines to €15 million, amid cookie-banner-fatigue warnings. Google outpaced them, [yanking one-click AI satellite imagery](https://www.npr.org/2026/07/31/nx-s1-5914652/google-adds-ai-to-satellite-images-raising-fears-of-deepfakes-in-the-sky) a day after launch when journalists conjured a burning Kharg Island. Record labels want [AI songs off the charts](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/973741/ai-music-major-record-labels-charts) unless “substantially human made,” and one GPTZero flag metastasized into [a 13-count federal lawsuit](https://arstechnica.com/tech-policy/2026/07/how-a-yale-ai-cheating-dispute-became-a-13-count-federal-lawsuit/) over a Yale exam.

Not every mind in court is synthetic. The Nonhuman Rights Project is dedicating [World Elephant Month to Happy](https://www.nonhumanrights.org/blog/honor-happys-legacy-stand-with-captive-elephants-this-world-elephant-month/), who died in May without winning the freedom her landmark personhood case sought.

Capital has picked its religion. Amazon [completed its $50 billion OpenAI investment](https://www.ft.com/content/8ae9e6e4-a53c-44da-8e7d-c9d81f0df4b9) for 5% of an $852 billion company, has $18 billion deployed into Anthropic, and hedges the race to sell Trainium either way, a bet blessed as shares [surged the most since 2012](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-30/amazon-reports-fifth-straight-quarter-of-cloud-sales-growth) on a fifth quarter of accelerating cloud growth. South Korea, whiplashed by a Kospi rout and record rebound, is [committing $13.9 billion of sovereign wealth](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-30/korea-plans-14-billion-for-wealth-fund-s-ai-bets-after-rout) to AI. China spends softer money, practicing [“token diplomacy,”](https://www.semafor.com/article/07/28/2026/token-diplomacy-how-china-is-shaping-the-worlds-ai-future) cheap open models for the Global South the way Belt and Road supplied ports. And bitcoin, unproductive as ever, [slid to $62,500](https://www.investors.com/news/bitcoin-price-falls-strategy-5-billion-crypto-sales-q2-earnings/) as its biggest whale mulls selling $5 billion.

Older mysteries just got an unsealing schedule. Rep. Burlison released [the administration’s memorandum](https://burlison.house.gov/media/press-releases/rep-burlison-releases-administration-memorandum-president-trumps-uap-nda) [implementing waivers of UAP nondisclosure agreements](https://burlison.house.gov/sites/evo-subsites/burlison.house.gov/files/evo-media-document/memorandum_2026.pdf) so government witnesses, current and former, can finally speak to the new PURSUE Task Force, agencies given 30 days.

The confirmed anomalies are the ones we ship ourselves. WaveSight opened preorders for [a camera that sees through walls](https://www.ae-dyn.com/), Figure’s F.03 [climbed a ladder fully autonomously](https://x.com/adcock_brett/status/2083576071635820660), and hybrid electric flying taxis may [debut in combat zones](https://www.theinformation.com/newsletters/the-electric/hybrid-electric-flying-taxis-may-combat-zones-ferrying-civilian-passengers) before carrying commuters. Americans, surveyed, believe [robots will take jobs within five years](https://www.semafor.com/article/07/31/2026/americans-worry-robots-will-take-jobs-but-not-theirs-survey-shows), just not their own. Powering it all stays messy. SpaceX will swap xAI’s [69 unpermitted Memphis turbines](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/31/spacex-wont-remove-all-of-xais-unpermitted-turbines-for-another-year/) for a 1.2-gigawatt plant, but not until mid-2027, while the [Jones Act waiver](https://www.zerohedge.com/energy/trumps-jones-act-waiver-helped-avert-fuel-shortages-across-these-key-us-regions), in day 135, has moved 50 million barrels between US ports. The exit ramp points up. Musk predicts [long-term “99.99...% of compute will be in space,”](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2083227110320013404) and rehearsals are on, with Starship [filmed by a Starlink V3 satellite](https://x.com/spacex/status/2083296921230164378) scanning its own heat shield. On Wednesday, a discarded SpaceX upper stage will [slam into the Moon at 5,400 mph](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/31/discarded-spacex-rocket-unintentional-collision-moon.html), carving a 90-foot crater with three tons of TNT’s energy, a lesson for lunar bases to come.

Now that’s a moonshot, ladies and gentlemen.
