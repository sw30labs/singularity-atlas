# Welcome to June 12, 2026

[![Welcome to June 12, 2026](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!GC47!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fc96009c7-94f7-4306-ae20-107dda7a9271_1983x793.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!GC47!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fc96009c7-94f7-4306-ae20-107dda7a9271_1983x793.png)

The Singularity is taking a Dyson Swarm public. SpaceX is making history with the [largest IPO ever](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-11/elon-musk-s-spacex-set-to-make-history-with-record-breaking-ipo), priced near $1.8 trillion and opening the public-market era for frontier infrastructure. On a pre-IPO livestream, Musk gave the real reason: raising funds for the “massive capital endeavor” of [orbital data centers](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/12/spacex-ipo-spcx-live-updates.html), his main way to expand AI with round-the-clock solar beyond Earth’s power budget and backlash. Priced at $135, Musk’s net worth is set to hit [$970.5 billion](https://x.com/biancoresearch/status/2065189344801857595), one Tesla rally short of creating the first trillionaire. Founders Fund’s $600M bet on SpaceX is now worth [over $50 billion](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-11/founders-fund-andreessen-set-for-record-returns-from-spacex-ipo) and a16z’s over $10 billion. And the public wants in with [more than $70 billion](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-11/spacex-ipo-said-to-draw-more-than-70-billion-in-retail-orders) in retail orders.

They are betting on minds learning to think longer. A new study found frontier models’ no-chain-of-thought time horizon has [doubled yearly for six years](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.07157), with GPT-5.5 reasoning past 3 human-minutes, and projected to hit 25 by 2030. Intelligence is also hyperdeflating, with Claude Fable 5 [well ahead of the token-efficiency frontier](https://x.com/scaling01/status/2065207315196739816), one-shotting a [No Man’s Sky clone](https://x.com/spicey_lemonade/status/2064957490400231432) and, via Ultracode, a Three.js starship that [screenshotted and fixed itself](https://x.com/ChrissGPT/status/2065193150222663959) until its user was “genuinely shocked.” But capability cuts both ways. Epoch AI judged the Mythos family a [genuine leap in exploit development](https://epochai.substack.com/p/are-mythos-cyber-capabilities-overhyped), seven months ahead of trend, and after researchers caught a clause allowing poisoning of AI research in Fable 5’s system card, Anthropic [reversed within 48 hours](https://www.wired.com/story/anthropic-responds-to-backlash-on-claudes-secret-sabotage-on-ai-research/) and apologized. Others want to democratize superintelligence outright, as the AI Alliance’s [Project Tapestry](https://thealliance.ai/projects/tapestry) lets nations co-train one shared model while keeping data local, even as OpenAI weighs [drastic price cuts](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/openai-considers-drastic-price-cuts-anticipating-war-for-users-with-anthropic-9b8c178e) against Anthropic, racing the cost of intelligence toward zero.

Cheaper, sharper intelligence is rewiring the code it scans and the commerce it runs. Microsoft’s larger [Patch Tuesday](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/msrc/blog/2026/05/a-note-on-patch-tuesday) reflects AI scanning surfacing more bugs in even its best code, while malware authors fight back absurdly by [stuffing spyware with “nuclear & biological weapons text”](https://x.com/jsrailton/status/2064661778978533571) to trip AI scanners’ safety refusals. Friendlier agents reach for your wallet, as Visa now sits inside [ChatGPT](https://apnews.com/article/visa-chatgpt-openai-shopping-mastercard-d769dec86344cb4977c98789e8ec492f) to check out anywhere and [Coinbase for Agents](https://www.coinbase.com/blog/coinbase-for-agents) lets your AI trade within limits you set. Silicon is being repriced too, as memory-chip maker Kioxia, up 600% this year, overtakes Toyota as [Japan’s most valuable firm](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-12/kioxia-becomes-japan-s-most-valuable-firm-as-ai-mania-goes-on), Nvidia, shut out of China’s GPU market, now pitches Arm-based [Vera CPUs to Chinese clients](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/nvidia-begins-vera-cpu-sales-pitch-chinese-clients-sources-say-2026-06-12/), and Google courts Samsung to [help build a future AI chip](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/google-turns-samsung-future-ai-chip-capacity-tightens) as the crunch pushes past TSMC.

Cognition needs real estate. Anthropic signed a dozen-plus letters of intent to [lease its own data centers](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/anthropic-pursues-first-data-center-leases-seeks-financial-backing-google), backed by Google, while Epoch found the largest single data center has [doubled compute every seven months](https://epoch.ai/data-insights/largest-data-center-compute) since 2024. The buildout is now a battlespace, with OpenAI exposing a China-linked [influence operation](https://openai.com/index/prc-linked-influence-operations-ai-debates/) seeding narratives against US data centers. Autonomy has crossed harder lines elsewhere, as humanoid robots in China [beg for coins](https://x.com/ErenChenAI/status/2065141531082645722) while a Ukrainian official confirms fully autonomous “Terminator mode” drones have [killed soldiers with no human in the loop](https://www.newscientist.com/article/2529849-fully-autonomous-drones-have-killed-human-soldiers-for-the-first-time/).

Intelligence is electricity that learned to think, so the scramble is now for watts. Commonwealth Fusion’s five papers detail an upcoming 400-MW [ARC reactor](https://arstechnica.com/science/2026/06/__trashed-19/), BYD is switching on [1.5-MW Flash Chargers](https://www.theverge.com/transportation/947553/byd-flash-chargers-uk-europe-ev-blade-battery) adding 400km in five minutes, and US solar [outproduced coal](https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2026/jun/11/solar-energy-us-coal) for the first time.

Biology is getting cooked. A Biohub-Berkeley [laser phase plate](https://biohub.org/blog/laser-phase-plate-cryo-em-making-invisible-visible/), a beam 100 million times brighter than the Sun’s surface, sharpened cryo-EM enough to image over half the proteins running life inside intact cells. The clinic is following, with Beijing’s 301 Hospital recruiting 2,000 over-50s for China’s first large [stem-cell antiaging trial](https://longevity.technology/news/china-launches-first-large-scale-stem-cell-antiaging-trial/) and David Sinclair taking an AI-tuned [chemical-reprogramming drug](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/06/09/1138545/david-sinclair-plans-to-test-whole-body-rejuvenation-drugs-in-the-xprize-competition/) into humans via the XPrize, chasing a younger body.

The hardest problem of the intelligence age isn’t building it, it’s sharing it. Anthropic and OpenAI are [planting flags in London](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/11/anthropic-openai-london-expansions-big-tech.html) as the boom goes global, Anthropic’s [$150M Claude Corps](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-corps) is embedding 1,000 fellows in nonprofits, and Google is funding [300,000 trade workers](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/11/google-trade-worker-initiative-ai) to build it, even as “[Europe 2031](https://europe2031.ai/)“ warns of nations missing the window. Accountability is hardening too, as a German court ruled Google’s [AI Overviews](https://the-decoder.com/landmark-german-ruling-declares-googles-ai-overviews-are-googles-own-words-and-makes-it-liable-for-false-answers/) to be its own speech, and liable for falsehoods, while Apollo now [screens all software bets](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-11/apollo-is-screening-all-software-investments-for-ai-threat-risk) for disruption risk. The biggest players now pace themselves against the Singularity, with Sam Altman warning recursive self-improvement could [delay OpenAI’s IPO](https://www.reuters.com/business/openai-expects-go-public-within-next-year-information-reports-2026-06-10/) and the President predicting AI firms will [give back](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/trump-says-he-thinks-ai-companies-will-agree-giving-back-public-2026-06-10/) via equity stakes that make citizens “very rich.” Framing it all, [Dario Amodei](https://darioamodei.com/post/policy-on-the-ai-exponential) warned policy is plodding like Tolkien’s Treebeard while AI sprints, urging a leap past transparency to FAA-style testing of top models.

All we have to decide is what to do with the compute we’re given.
