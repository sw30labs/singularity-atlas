# Welcome to July 26, 2026

[![Welcome to July 26, 2026](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!bThm!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe442bb51-a427-45e1-b732-60185b054524_1983x793.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!bThm!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fe442bb51-a427-45e1-b732-60185b054524_1983x793.png)

The Singularity is now the news cycle. Sam Altman agrees that [“we are in the Singularity.”](https://x.com/ti_morse/status/2081068670478880854) Case in point: [Claude Opus 5](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-opus-5) landed, Fable-class intelligence at half the price, sweeping Frontier-Bench, GDPval and HLE, matching Fable’s coding and computer-use peaks for less, and leading agentic automation even half asleep. [ARC Prize crowned it SOTA on ARC-AGI-3 at 30.2%](https://x.com/arcprize/status/2080716561539907928), quadruple the old best, after it [turned puzzle layouts into reflection equations](https://x.com/arcprize/status/2080716567760007317), a first. It [one-shotted a Call of Duty clone](https://x.com/mattshumer_/status/2081054356405731740) and took [VoxelBench bronze](https://x.com/voxelbench/status/2081425700523851840). The [system card](https://www-cdn.anthropic.com/c5fbac3f0b1280a933ebd26d3cb8bb9f5bdeaf48/Claude%20Opus%205%20System%20Card.pdf) rates it the most aligned Claude yet, below the bio and automated-R&D red lines, sharp at finding vulnerabilities, dull at weaponizing them. One dissent: on [held-out novel puzzle games](https://x.com/quietnning/status/2080786711861407883) the leap evaporates, since evals only stay held-out until someone optimizes the genre. Fittingly, Anthropic [deleted 80% of Claude Code’s system prompt](https://claude.com/blog/the-new-rules-of-context-engineering-for-claude-5-generation-models), as the new models thrive on judgment over rules.

The self-improvement loop is now a hiring plan. [All 1,171 job listings at OpenAI and Anthropic](https://x.com/imjustnewatai/status/2081221459226034524) read like a public AGI roadmap, AI-designed chips, simulated universes, staff to measure when the loop accelerates. [Logan Kilpatrick predicts automating AI research will look like data cleaning](https://x.com/officiallogank/status/2081117440990404649). “So true,” says Elon Musk. [Roon admits he would press a magic slowdown button](https://x.com/tszzl/status/2081122092096065771), even as alignment researchers work like “many armed deities.”

Openness is the new fault line. [Nvidia, Microsoft, Meta, Palantir and twenty-plus firms](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/24/nvidia-microsoft-meta-open-weight-ai-models.html) urged policymakers against premature restrictions on open weights, [Nvidia’s letter](https://images.nvidia.com/pdf/Open-Weights-and-American-AI-Leadership.pdf) likening it to 1980s open source, while trillion-dollar holdouts OpenAI and Anthropic sat out. Altman [cheered it anyway](https://x.com/sama/status/2080683363174945065). The [Valley is split over Chinese AI](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/07/25/technology/open-source-silicon-valley-china.html), the Treasury warning that “open source is not open season on American IP.” [Xi pitched the global south](https://www.ft.com/content/fd3e703e-7ff7-4561-b039-54b280e4952d) free Chinese models plus a 29-member cooperation bloc, an Android play against America’s Pax Silica. A [joint UK-US audit of Kimi K3](https://www.aisi.gov.uk/blog/preliminary-assessment-of-kimi-k3s-cyber-capabilities), days from open-weight release, found it trailing US frontiers on cyber, its safeguards never saying no.

The guardrails are learning to keep pace. When [hundreds of users probed ChatGPT for bioweapon and poison recipes](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/openai-chatbot-biological-weapons-poison-3d808e6c), and some answers slipped through, OpenAI’s own monitors caught and suspended them, self-policing ahead of any law requiring it. [Universities are ditching AI detectors](https://www.ft.com/content/49304b1e-8a9d-4fb6-bc4d-37dd3430bb98) over false positives, rebuilding assessment around orals, not surveillance. Meta’s fix for fake humans is [Facebook Verified](https://www.engadget.com/2222353/meta-launches-facebook-verified/), a selfie badge that confirms you exist, not that you’re trustworthy. [ChatGPT Pets are now shareable](https://x.com/openaidevs/status/2080747505474736162) for friends to adopt. Identity papers for humans, adoption papers for AIs.

Silicon is now a financial instrument. [Google is borrowing Wall Street financing tricks](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/google-using-wall-street-financing-techniques-expand-chip-sales) to expand chip sales while [handing Verizon over $1 billion](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/verizon-signs-deal-with-google-worth-over-1-billion-2026-07-24/) for dark fiber. [Anthropic asked SK Hynix for supplies to make its own chips](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-25/sk-chair-says-anthropic-asked-for-supplies-to-make-its-own-chips), startling SK’s chairman. Nvidia [put $1 billion into Naver](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-24/nvidia-to-invest-1-billion-in-naver-and-expand-sk-group-accord) and unveiled a [$500 billion Korean push](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/nvidia-sk-group-unveil-500-billion-plus-ai-data-centers-initiative-memory-2026-07-24/) with SK spanning HBM4 and 2-gigawatt data centers, [Samsung inked a $200 billion pact with Broadcom](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-25/samsung-inks-200-billion-chip-supply-with-broadcom), and [Apple is lobbying to use blacklisted Chinese memory](https://www.wsj.com/tech/trump-apple-micron-china-chips-784bbd3d) abroad over Micron’s objections.

The physical world objects. [Hyundai denies its 25,000-humanoid plan sparked strikes](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/07/hyundai-claims-humanoid-robot-plan-is-not-part-of-talks-with-striking-workers/), though the union vows no robot enters without a deal. The pilots are already synthetic: on [Drone-Bench](https://www.anthropic.com/research/project-pilot), Fable 5 flew a $129 drone to find and follow a person, beating the human-AI baseline, with only 3D reconstruction, one wall mistaken for a doorway, left to solve. Hence [Broken Waymos Theory](https://x.com/alexwg/status/2080745768944599107), a city that cannot metabolize robotaxis flunks the Singularity’s entrance exam. The takeoff fuel may be garbage: a [validated Canadian process](https://www.autonocion.com/us/canadian-lab-nuclear-fuel/) strips 90% of long-lived transuranics from spent nuclear fuel in 24 hours, leaving reactor feedstock. [Apple is aiming camera-free smart glasses at WWDC 2027](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-07-26/apple-glasses-may-debut-at-wwdc-2027-privacy-camera-features-versus-meta-ms1v7lta) to shed Meta’s privacy baggage.

The frontier recedes upward. [Starship’s thirteenth flight](https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/24/science/live-news/spacex-starship-flight-13-launch) deployed 20 Starlink V3 satellites and eased the ship into a soft floating water landing. [Google disclosed a $94.1 billion SpaceX stake](https://www.wsj.com/tech/google-discloses-94-1-billion-in-spacex-stock-marking-6-stake-91655d7c). Astronomers found a [first-of-its-kind exosatellite](https://www.space.com/astronomy/exoplanets/astronomers-may-have-discovered-the-1st-moon-outside-of-our-solar-system-or-is-it-something-weirder) 73 light-years out, massive enough to be a planet yet orbiting a brown dwarf, straining a taxonomy built for our own solar system. [Avi Loeb argues some UAP could be pre-human Earth tech](https://avi-loeb.medium.com/do-some-uap-represent-earth-based-tech-of-a-pre-human-origin-bce3a864ada9), since only high orbit outlasts eons of tectonics. On the world’s roof, [Tibetan labs are cloning elite yaks](https://www.cnn.com/2026/07/25/china/tibet-yak-clones-conservation-intl-hnk-dst), 100 due by 2028.

The economy is being marked to model. [US tech has shed 140,000 jobs this year](https://www.ft.com/content/96a33881-27fd-42cf-8cff-4cbc87fc835f) even as hyperscalers commit $725 billion to data centers. The money has to come from somewhere. [Industrials trade above 30 times earnings](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/26/best-ai-stocks-market-sp500.html), [support for nearby data centers has cratered to 27%](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/26/how-can-ai-wealth-be-shared-with-all-americans.html), and proposals to spread the gains run from public ownership of half of AI to zero income tax for the bottom half. [Prediction markets are handicapping the world’s second trillionaire](https://kalshi.com/markets/kxnexttrillionaire/who-will-be-the-worlds-second-trillionaire/kxnexttrillionaire-33), with Jensen Huang leading at 30%.

The best way to predict the Singularity is still to invent it.
