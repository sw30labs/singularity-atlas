# Welcome to May 11, 2026

[![Welcome to May 11, 2026](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!8uKF!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F6f0d54c8-4a49-4dd9-9b38-61c237443303_1983x793.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!8uKF!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F6f0d54c8-4a49-4dd9-9b38-61c237443303_1983x793.png)

The Singularity is deprecating its own knobs. [OpenAI is winding down its fine-tuning API](https://www.perplexity.ai/page/openai-winds-down-fine-tuning-ifobg8gsT8.gDcIjPLOG8A), giving customers until January 2027 to spin up new training jobs. The logic, [as one observer notes](https://x.com/dbreunig/status/2053508329318830312), may be that as the largest models keep getting better at more things, adjusting their weights matters less. [Sam Altman](https://x.com/sama/status/2053192920933777486) put it bluntly with a tongue-in-cheek AGI re-coinage, joking that GPT-5.5 is a “genius,” not just a “generalist.” To track this profusion of ever-larger minds, [Cisco released its open-source Model Provenance Kit](https://blogs.cisco.com/ai/model-provenance-kit), examining metadata and weights like a model genome to spot shared origins and tampering. Meanwhile, the harness is eating the model. [Hermes Agent is now #1 on the global OpenRouter token rankings](https://x.com/nousresearch/status/2052904761087729897), passing OpenClaw by generating its own skills, while OpenClaw users hand-write theirs and lean on Opus for prompt-injection safety.

The Erdős backlog is going industrial. [Standalone AI solutions to open Erdős problems are skyrocketing](https://x.com/acerfur/status/2053571980150899154). The agents have learned to leave their desks. [An OpenAI “Codex mobile” experience has been spotted](https://x.com/ai_for_success/status/2053467456954593364), letting users keep working with Codex whenever their computer is awake. Codex is also learning to hustle. [Told to “go off and make me $5,”](https://x.com/chatgpt21/status/2053556436475461786) it allegedly found an open-source security bounty, filed a legit PR, worked 22 hours across audits, and netted $16.88.

The underlying silicon is sprinting too. [AMD’s ROCm stack has reportedly improved 75x in the 14 days since DeepSeek V4](https://x.com/semianalysis_/status/2053520440589451720), with only another ~7.5x needed to catch Nvidia’s B200. The grid is struggling to keep pace. [Maryland’s Office of People’s Counsel filed a FERC complaint](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/maryland-citizens-slapped-with-usd2-billion-grid-upgrade-bill-for-out-of-state-ai-data-centers-state-complains-to-federal-energy-regulators-says-additional-cost-breaks-ratepayer-protection-pledge-promises) over a $2B ratepayer tab for grid upgrades servicing out-of-state data centers, calling it a breach of the “ratepayer protection pledge.” [Microsoft and G42’s $1B Kenyan geothermal data center has stalled](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-10/microsoft-s-african-data-center-falters-on-payment-demands) over payment guarantees that Kenya’s president says exceed national resources. The wires now run through warzones, with [U.S. hyperscalers piping Gulf data center traffic out via fiber-optic cables an Iraqi telecom has strung alongside crude-oil pipelines](https://restofworld.org/2026/iraq-big-tech-gulf-war-data/). Even fusion’s supply chain is becoming AI infrastructure, with [the industry driving high-temperature superconducting wire from 5,000 km to 1.5 million km over 15 years](https://x.com/deeptechweek/status/2053288300358906271) and knock-on effects in transport, medical imaging, power, and data center design.

The data centers are leaving the planet. [SpaceX has filed a trademark for “SpaceXAI,”](https://x.com/sawyermerritt/status/2053168221793218737) covering satellite-based data centers, orbital computing, and AI for managing space-based platforms. [Starship V3 has been fully stacked for the first time](https://x.com/spacex/status/2053173499984818195), ready to lift orbital silicon. From there, algorithms may spot more company. [Machine learning has just identified 10,000 new exoplanet candidates](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.18579) from TESS images, mostly around faint stars. Back on the ground, [Utah’s Hypercraft launched Razorback](https://defence-blog.com/utah-based-firm-develops-razorback-autonomous-combat-vehicle/), an autonomous combat vehicle that carries 2,400 pounds, drives 280 miles on a charge, and exports 38 kW to charge drones, run directed-energy weapons, and sustain forward command posts, with no human onboard.

Superintelligence is making hidden agents shallow. Following the launch of the White House’s historic PURSUE initiative, [Rep. Burlison asked MIT Lincoln Lab to preserve a 1952 reel-to-reel](https://burlison.house.gov/media/press-releases/burlison-requests-review-identified-1952-uap-recording) tied to early federal UAP investigations. [The Pentagon reportedly plans to release another 46 UAP videos next week](https://x.com/the_astral_/status/2053498608830304302) requested by Rep. Luna, while [Rep. Burchett says the first PURSUE drop was “just the tip of the iceberg.”](https://www.foxnews.com/video/6395022863112)

The biosphere is in active repair. [Great Lakes river otters are clawing back from the brink](https://www.rewildingmag.com/the-river-otters-remarkable-comeback/) after decades of cross-border effort. And plants are joining the conversation. [Plant seeds can sense the vibrations of falling raindrops](https://www.sciencealert.com/plants-can-sense-the-sound-of-rain) and wake from dormancy in response. We’re patching ourselves, too. [An MIT team released FINGERS-7B](https://picower.mit.edu/news/mit-based-team-releases-first-ai-foundation-model-alzheimers-prevention), the first AI foundation model for Alzheimer’s prevention, integrating lifestyle, clinical, genomic, and proteomic data from tens of thousands of at-risk individuals.

The economy is being remade in AI’s image. [OpenAI and Anthropic are projected to end 2026 with combined ARR exceeding Nvidia’s revenue last year](https://x.com/code_star/status/2053576378357051416), as software is starting to compete with silicon. [Alphabet briefly overtook Nvidia in market cap](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/10/alphabet-160percent-rally-in-year-reflects-value-of-owning-most-of-ai-stack.html), vindicating the once-doubted AI laggard. Capital is voting with its feet, with [billionaire tax refugees flocking to Incline Village](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/billionaire-tax-refugees-flock-ritzy-nevada-lake-town) at Lake Tahoe, increasingly described as “the nicest San Francisco neighborhood.” Labor is reshuffling. Inside newsrooms, [McClatchy journalists are withholding bylines](https://www.spokesman.com/stories/2026/may/01/reporters-at-mcclatchy-withhold-bylines-in-dispute/) from AI-spun articles. [Women hold 83% of the 15 most AI-vulnerable jobs](https://www.inc.com/kit-eaton/ai-poses-a-greater-job-threat-to-women-than-men-new-data-shows/91342399) despite being just 47% of the workforce, the surveilled and algorithmically-managed work AI is automating away first. AI is now playing both sides of the security stack, with [40% of the 5,000 breaches Experian serviced last year being AI-powered](https://www.bloomberg.com/graphics/2026-ai-identity-theft-scams/). But even Tehran’s [record 70-day internet blackout](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-05-09/iran-businesses-buckle-under-strain-of-record-internet-shutdown?sref=gB2arSXM) can’t slow the world’s compounding.

Compounding, the only constant is compounding.
