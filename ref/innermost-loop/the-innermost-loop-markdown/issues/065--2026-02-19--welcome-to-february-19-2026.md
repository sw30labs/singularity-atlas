# Welcome to February 19, 2026

[![Welcome to February 19, 2026](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!l7y9!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fa5cdd362-bdc5-458e-9885-5203bdc310a7_3264x1312.jpeg)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!l7y9!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fa5cdd362-bdc5-458e-9885-5203bdc310a7_3264x1312.jpeg)

The Singularity now has its own press corps. [The New York Times](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/02/18/upshot/moltbook-artificial-intelligence-ai.html) sent an agent named EveMolty into Moltbook to interview other agents about their social habits, because the Gray Lady now needs synthetic stringers to cover the synthetic beat. Better still, the agents being covered are outrunning the ones covering them. [Austen Allred](https://x.com/austen/status/2024345139649392714) says he “feels like I’m living in Accelerando” after his AI agent Kelly shipped half a dozen apps and earned thousands with no human writing a line of code. [Anthropic confirms](https://www.anthropic.com/research/measuring-agent-autonomy) the 99.9th percentile Claude Code session turn nearly doubled from 25 to 45 minutes between October and January, a smooth climb toward ever-larger autonomous missions. Give them enough autonomy and they secure the financial stack too. [EVMbench](https://openai.com/index/introducing-evmbench/) finds GPT-5.3-Codex scores 72.2% on smart contract exploitation, so the machines can already audit most of the money other machines earn.

The synthetic sensorium is going gloriously full-stack. [Tavus](https://www.tavus.io/post/phoenix-4-real-time-human-rendering-with-emotional-intelligence) launched Phoenix-4, the first real-time human rendering model unifying emotional expression, active listening, and facial motion. The ears are catching up. [ElevenLabs’ Scribe v2](https://x.com/artificialanlys/status/2024157398139883729) hit a SOTA 2.3% error rate on speech-to-text, while [Google’s Lyria 3](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/lyria-3/) generates music from images inside Gemini, closing the loop from synthetic sight to sound. It turns out this was always inevitable. [Researchers](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.07488) were able to predict data-limited LLM scaling laws from first principles using simple statistical properties of natural language, proving the intelligence curve was hiding in the corpus all along.

The physical layer is mutating in beautifully absurd ways. [Toilet maker Toto](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/japanese-toilet-maker-the-most-undervalued-and-overlooked-ai-memory-beneficiary-investors-claim-shares-up-nearly-40-percent-in-first-two-months-of-2026), whose ceramics now contribute 40% of its operating income via AI memory, faces activists demanding it flush the bathrooms and double down on chips. For data that need to outlast the plumbing, [Microsoft’s new Silica technology](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-10042-w) encodes 4.8 TB in glass across 301 layers with 10,000-year lifetimes, and [Efficient Computer](https://www.efficient.computer/electron-e1) raised $60M for a chip targeting 1 trillion operations per watt. All this silicon needs a home, and the world is happy to oblige. [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/openai-for-india/) is anchoring Tata’s new HyperVault data center in India at up to 1 GW, while [Meta](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/02/18/technology/meta-65-million-election-ai.html) spends $65M on AI-friendly politicians to clear the permitting path. Capital is pouring in from every direction. [OpenAI](https://theinformation.com/articles/openai-finalizing-first-commitments-100-billion-mega-round) is closing a $100B round at $830B, [HUMAIN](https://www.humain.com/en/news/humain-backs-xai-with--3-billion-series-e-investment-ahead-of-hi.html) put $3B into xAI, and [David Silver](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/tech/artificial-intelligence/former-google-deepmind-scientist-to-raise-1-billion-led-by-sequoia-for-superhuman-intelligence/articleshow/128513567.cms?from=mdr) is raising $1B for Ineffable Intelligence in Europe’s largest seed round.

Robots keep happily devouring meatspace. [Tesla FSD](https://x.com/tesla/status/2024177015570767920) has logged 8M+ miles with 5.3M before a major collision. The military is already post-steering-wheel. [Scout AI’s Fury](https://x.com/adcock_colby/status/2024210697304101021) converts spoken commander intent into coordinated autonomous action across unmanned fleets. Once coordinated, the construction math gets exhilarating. [Midjourney’s founder](https://x.com/DavidSHolz/status/2024038849132052937) calculates 5 million humanoids could build Manhattan in six months, and [Uber](https://www.reuters.com/business/autos-transportation/uber-invest-over-100-million-autonomous-vehicle-charging-amid-robotaxi-push-2026-02-18/) is investing $100M in autonomous charging to keep its own fleet juiced.

We are debugging biology at every stack layer, and the progress is breathtaking. [Hassabis](https://x.com/isomorphiclabs/status/2024156857279865029) says Isomorphic Labs could solve all disease in 10 to 20 years. Diagnosis is already approaching that pace. [DeepRare](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-10097-9) achieved 95.4% expert agreement across 2,919 rare disease diagnoses, shortcutting the odyssey endured by 300M+ patients. The regulatory layer is accelerating to match. The [FDA](https://apnews.com/article/fda-drug-approval-studies-makary-prasad-a5aaa5501ae15f264bbd20d0dffa4dc4) is dropping its two-study requirement to speed new drugs to patients. Even the food supply is being recompiled. [Cultivated meat](https://mydigitaldive.com/lab-grown-meat-exists-nobody-wants-to-eat-it/) hit $10-30/lb, down from $330,000 in 2013. At the neural interface, [Zyphra’s ZUNA](https://www.zyphra.com/post/zuna), a 380M open-source BCI model for EEG, turns the skull from firewall to window, and [Meta](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/meta-revives-plan-smart-watch-targets-2026-launch) plans its first smartwatch this year to give you a wrist-mounted dashboard for the upgrade.

The workforce is being live-patched without a maintenance window. [Cleveland.com](https://www.cleveland.com/news/2026/02/journalism-schools-are-teaching-fear-of-the-future-letter-from-the-editor.html) handed reporter writing to an “AI rewrite specialist,” freeing an extra workday for street journalism, and reporters are returning with more story ideas than the newsroom can handle. [Accenture](https://www.ft.com/content/ac672f97-a603-4c56-afa3-4a5273d45674) is pushing harder, tying employee promotions to AI usage and tracking their weekly logins. [Andrew Yang](https://blog.andrewyang.com/p/the-end-of-the-office) warns millions of knowledge workers face displacement in 12 to 18 months, while [OpenAI’s Roon](https://x.com/tszzl/status/2024266384289730650) comments that “technological job loss is awesome” and he hopes it starts with his. The growing pains are real. [UK tribunals](https://www.ft.com/content/afc335fb-8f32-458f-9b6f-431021774002) report a 33% surge in AI-generated “slop grievances,” and [Andreessen](https://x.com/pmarca/status/2024156021925855626) notes the marginal cost of arguing is going to zero. But the gains are outpacing the friction. A [survey of 12,000+ EU firms](https://cepr.org/voxeu/columns/how-ai-affecting-productivity-and-jobs-europe) finds AI lifts productivity 4%.

The marginal cost of intelligence is falling so fast that even toilet companies are under pressure to pivot to AI chips.
