# Welcome to July 5, 2026

[![Welcome to July 5, 2026](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!85b7!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F6ae6d9bb-25b8-4f79-96f5-67cb26e68fae_1983x793.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!85b7!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F6ae6d9bb-25b8-4f79-96f5-67cb26e68fae_1983x793.png)

The Singularity is discovering that intelligence, pushed far enough, bends back to face itself. Jürgen Schmidhuber [argues](https://x.com/scaling01/status/2073485177188241787) that the one constant across training and rollouts is the model itself, so a mind should learn to model itself to better predict its next token. If self-knowledge is the frontier, orchestration is the near ground, and a hobbyist [reverse-engineered](https://x.com/harshalsinghcn/status/2073500169707172338) Sakana’s Fugu into “tinyrouter,” a ~10k-parameter conductor that beat every model on MMLU by handing each question to the right specialist. Raw scale still matters, as Google’s rumored [Gemini 3.5 Pro](https://x.com/astropol0/status/2073496819301335296) may ship a 2-million-token context, doubling Anthropic’s Opus and Fable. On Fable itself, Google’s Andy Coenen [credits](https://x.com/_coenen/status/2073475535934574857) three ingredients, sheer scale, relentless long-horizon agentic RL, and a constitution that turns thousands of decisions into one legible compass. Anthropic’s [prompting guide](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/build-with-claude/prompt-engineering/prompting-claude-fable-5) confirms that recipe, promising longer autonomous runs and classifiers that reroute cyber and bio requests to Opus 4.8, while Simon Willison [relays](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/3/judgement/) that letting Fable exercise judgement beats dictating rules and conserves tokens before a price hike.

That judgement is now aimed at discovery. A new [framework](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.01233) measures how far machine research ideas drift from human taste, finding that LLMs cluster around bridge-and-synthesis gaps while humans roam wider. Alibaba’s [Elements Claw](https://www.scmp.com/tech/big-tech/article/3359335/alibabas-elements-claw-ai-agent-unearths-four-new-superconductors) put that narrowness to work, screening 2.4 million crystals in 28 GPU-hours and surfacing four verified superconductors. The application layer is molting just as fast. One builder [declares](https://x.com/skeptrune/status/2073564586297672151) that Fable managing sub-agents made hand-coding worthless, leaving accountability as “the only value left in us meatbags.” Whimsy trails utility, as Fable turned an e-ink tablet into [Tom Riddle’s diary](https://x.com/maximerivest/status/2073544461473169432) where prompts fade and a spirit replies, and Google imagined the [Founding Fathers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q3RjZY-rSsc) co-editing the Declaration with AI. Fittingly, the human-microtask era it replaced is closing, as Amazon [winds down](https://www.theregister.com/off-prem/2026/07/03/amazons-mechanical-turk-to-stop-accepting-new-customers-and-not-even-ai-can-save-it/5266274) Mechanical Turk, the 2005 marketplace that survived by labeling AI’s training data.

Making minds this cheap requires making silicon cheaper. Sam Zeloof [renamed](https://x.com/szeloof/status/2073527770525614233) Atomic Semi to [Fab2](https://fab2.com/), a “fab fab” spanning an Austin chip fab, a Lockhart fab fab, and a San Francisco garage, all wired to a browser EDA tool. To debug that silicon, ETH Zurich [floated](https://phys.org/news/2026-07-ion-3d-electromagnetic-fields-chips.html) a single trapped ion in a Penning trap to map chip fields in 3D down to 10 nanovolts per meter. Nations are bidding for the buildout, as Macron and Modi personally [court CEOs](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/04/macron-modi-ai-infrastructure-tech-ceos.html), landing 75 billion euros from SoftBank for France and a record $48 billion from Amazon for India.

The compute is growing legs. Nous Research’s Hermes now [inhabits](https://x.com/dodoreach/status/2072444986931487008) a robodog that sees, hears, and talks, while an F-35B [stole](https://x.com/JayTC53/status/2073527472583229441) the National Mall’s America 250 show in full hover mode. Tesla, meanwhile, may soon [demand facial recognition](https://www.zerohedge.com/technology/tesla-about-use-facial-recognition-activating-full-self-driving) before Full Self-Driving engages, gamified with streak days. Beneath the software, the hardware quietly overdelivers, as EV batteries [outlast](https://www.wsj.com/business/autos/ev-batteries-are-defying-expectations-after-hundreds-of-thousands-of-miles-8579de13) their doubters past hundreds of thousands of miles, dissolving the anxiety that once kept buyers away.

America is bolting together the first ribs of a Dyson Swarm, and Europe has flinched first. An ESO [study](https://www.eso.org/public/news/eso2607/) warns that 1.7 million proposed satellites, including orbital data centers and sunlight mirrors, could blind astronomy, urging a 100,000 cap.

The same age teaching silicon to model itself is racing to keep the biological self from unraveling. An annual [pipeline report](https://alz-journals.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/trc2.70251) now counts 158 Alzheimer’s drugs across 192 trials, revealing a decade-long pivot from amyloid toward inflammation and immunity, while a Loma Linda [study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/42002260/) links a five-eggs-a-week diet to a 27% lower risk of the disease.

History is being re-read with the same instruments. Researchers found that the [hobbits](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/sciadv.aeb7219) of Flores ate what the dragons left behind and never mastered fire, while triple-DNA forensics gave a name back to a Continental soldier in a shallow 1780 Camden grave, [John Pumphrey](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/revolutionary-war-soldier-identified-john-doe-technology-camden/), who enlisted at thirteen, just before America’s 250th. Older secrets are surfacing too, as the President told his new intelligence chief to [“declassify almost everything,”](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/07/01/politics/bill-pulte-intelligence-director-trump) his UAP advisors [accused](https://nypost.com/2026/07/04/us-news/trump-ufo-advisors-accuse-private-corporations-of-operating-alien-craft-retrieval-programs/) defense contractors of running craft-retrieval programs, and whistleblowers launched [Vanguard](https://x.com/vanguardenterpr/status/2073490277025640452) to shield those who talk. Even birds are being decoded, as Berkeley’s Julie Elie won the [Coller-Dolittle Prize](https://www.freepressjournal.in/education/breaking-the-bird-barrier-scientist-decodes-zebra-finch-language) for translating eleven zebra-finch calls.

Ownership is being rewritten last. The Treasury [launched](https://home.treasury.gov/news/press-releases/sb0554) 530A accounts, an early move toward Universal Basic Equity that seeds every eligible child with a $1,000 stake in capital markets, not a cash handout, [tracked](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/01/trump-accounts-launch-july-4.html) through a Robinhood app. The nation marked its 250th with a world-record [851,000 fireworks](https://nypost.com/2026/06/16/us-news/trumps-july-4-bash-to-include-record-setting-851k-fireworks/), even as networked war [blurs](https://www.msn.com/en-us/news/world/wars-are-blurring-lines-between-corporate-and-national-security/ar-AA27c657) the line between corporate and national security and turns data centers into targets. Marc Andreessen [expects](https://x.com/pmarca/status/2073574350645792791) America to reach 90% of global market cap, and wealthy families are [pulling kids](https://www.wsj.com/us-news/education/alternative-education-wealthy-families-ai-cd7922b3) toward schools like [Forge Prep](https://forgeprep.org/), built for 2040, not 1940.

It takes a startup to raise a child.
