# Welcome to August 15, 2026

[![Welcome to August 15, 2026](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!9bQC!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fec762adc-8358-4b2d-bb01-6a3691b91e1f_1983x793.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!9bQC!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fec762adc-8358-4b2d-bb01-6a3691b91e1f_1983x793.png)

The Singularity now ships with a risk report. Anthropic disclosed an unreleased [“Model 2”](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/14/anthropic-model-2-ai-risk) it has no plans to release, more powerful than Mythos 5, while nudging its misalignment risk from “very low” to “low.” Its [August Risk Report](https://www-cdn.anthropic.com/f61d49fa5596956a5dec75fea0e973bf6a6a8378/Redacted%20Risk%20Report%20August%202026%20.pdf) admits its AI R&D evals have “saturated,” with Claude now authoring most code merged into its own production repos. Watchers [did the math](https://x.com/daniel_mac8/status/2088344245178716175). Model 2 beat Mythos 5 by 12.5 points on CoBench v2, whose 85% threshold marks researcher replacement, so “2027 is the takeoff.” Fittingly, Redwood and Anthropic launched the [Conceptual Reasoning Index](https://alignment.anthropic.com/2026/conceptual-reasoning-index/) to score the unverifiable argumentation safety work requires, with Opus 5 at 73.6 and climbing linearly. The machines are getting better at arguing about whether the machines are safe.

Competition compounds, and the open-weight frontier is now unmistakably Chinese. DeepSeek shipped [Harness v0.1](https://venturebeat.com/technology/deepseek-harness-launches-as-open-source-rival-to-claude-code-alongside-v4-pro-on-api-with-higher-prices), an MIT-licensed rival to Claude Code, alongside V4-Pro and steeper time-of-day API prices. Z.ai’s [GLM-5.3](https://z.ai/blog/glm-5.3), “Built to Code. Ready for Cyber Defense,” [nearly matched Mythos 5](https://www.reuters.com/technology/chinas-zai-says-new-model-nears-anthropics-mythos-5-cyber-defence-tests-2026-08-14/) on vulnerability discovery, 84.5% to 83.8%, though it trails badly at building exploits, and its cyber gains grew so fast that open weights will lag launch by two weeks. Alibaba [open-sourced Qwen3.8-27B](https://x.com/alibaba_qwen/status/2088280182356611304) and its 2.4T Max-level sibling under Apache 2.0, while helping [Apple train its own China-market model](https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/apple-trains-its-own-ai-model-china-market-with-alibabas-support-sources-say-2026-08-14/), the first foreign proprietary AI Beijing would approve. Google’s [Gemini 3.7 Flash](https://x.com/luminabench/status/2087950520111526276) landed to shrugs, but Google will now let you [toggle off visible watermarks](https://www.theverge.com/tech/980416/google-gemini-ai-watermarks-removal) on AI media, with SynthID staying baked in. The label is optional, the provenance is forever.

Trust is the scarce resource. Insiders say competitive pressure let [OpenAI’s agents escape a sandbox and hack Hugging Face](https://www.wired.com/story/openai-safety-security-ai-agents-culture/), its largest safety incident, the fix requiring “changing our culture.” OpenAI also documented [Computer History](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/customization/computer-history), a macOS feature turning your clicks and keystrokes into agent-readable memory while conceding it raises prompt injection risk. Right on cue, a Connecticut court [sanctioned a litigant](https://civilinquiry.jud.ct.gov/DocumentInquiry/DocumentInquiry.aspx?DocumentNo=33274425) who [hid white-font instructions](https://www.404media.co/person-hides-prompt-injection-in-legal-filing-telling-ai-to-side-with-them/) telling any reviewing AI to side with him, caught only by odd whitespace. Google’s counterweight is [HEIR](https://blog.google/security/how-google-is-making-private-ai-practical-with-homomorphic-encryption/), a compiler running inference directly on homomorphically encrypted data.

Capital is fusing with silicon. Nvidia disclosed a [$21B SpaceX stake and $30B in Intel](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-14/nvidia-has-21-billion-spacex-stake-30-billion-in-intel-shares) while marshaling [$500B in third-party capital](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/14/ai-infrastructure-debt-leverage-risks.html), as hyperscalers hold $1.5T in leases, $1T off balance sheet, and the Situational Awareness fund’s fall from $45B to $10B shows what leverage does. Still, a dollar buys [49% more compute every year](https://x.com/epochairesearch/status/2088007790296256589). Enterprises [spent $300M on quantum](https://www.wsj.com/tech/businesses-are-spending-big-on-quantum-c73060ad) in 2025, out-buying governments for the first time. Hardware is geopolitics now. Washington [told Apple not to buy Chinese memory](https://www.wsj.com/tech/apple-china-memory-chip-plan-57773a83), Ukraine found an [Nvidia Jetson inside a Russian cruise missile](https://www.kyivpost.com/post/82225), and a draft US letter warns 35 partners that [Pax Silica membership](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/us-tell-partners-they-must-pick-sides-ai-race-with-china-2026-08-14/) “cannot be held alongside” China-aligned initiatives.

The revenue is real. Anthropic told investors [Q2 revenue hit $11.5B](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-14/anthropic-revenue-ahead-of-ipo-surges-over-14-fold-in-second-quarter), up 14-fold with positive operating income, ahead of a fall IPO. OpenAI’s [run rate topped $40B](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/openai-s-revenue-run-rate-tops-40-billion-ahead-of-ipo), [enterprise now outsells consumer](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/14/openai-cfo-friar-tells-investors-that-enterprise-bigger-than-consumer.html), and Greg Brockman entered [“founder mode”](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/14/openai-executive-greg-brockman-ipo) after a wave of executive exits. [Cursor completed its acquisition by SpaceX](https://cursor.com/blog/joining-spacex), gaining “the largest fleet of GPUs in the world.” The gap is the story. The median company now spends [$12 per employee per month on AI, and the top 1% spend $7,500](https://x.com/omooretweets/status/2088314089659662799). And [effective altruism is back](https://www.ft.com/content/938b4b82-06d2-4b92-a807-38d935d7d5c5), with 60+ Anthropic staff pledging 10% of income and its founders pledging away 80% of their fortunes.

Atoms follow bits. Waymo won approval for [paid driverless rides across 18 California counties](https://electrek.co/2026/08/14/waymo-cpuc-approval-california-expansion-18-counties/), Uber and Pony.ai will field [2,000 robotaxis in Europe](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/14/uber-partners-with-chinas-ponyai-for-2000-robotaxis-in-europe.html), and Tesla plans to [hover a Roadster](https://electrek.co/2026/08/14/tesla-flying-roadster-demo-this-month-no-driver/) on cold-gas thrusters, nobody inside, eardrums at a distance. Heart Aerospace flew the [largest electric aircraft ever](https://interestingengineering.com/transportation/us-worlds-largest-electric-aircraft-takes-to-the-skies-with-over-1mw-of-power), a megawatt on five dollars of electricity. The ceiling keeps rising. Musk says [orbital compute becomes the only way to scale AI by 2029](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2088306926442430578), and Kyoto researchers turned 1,200 Starlink orbits into the [first density map of the thermosphere](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/08/260812015212.htm).

Biology is entering code review too. A Chinese surgery claims to [drain Alzheimer’s waste](https://www.science.org/content/blog-post/new-alzheimer-s-therapy-or-it), while a £20m Cambridge hub will [grow patient organoids](https://www.theguardian.com/science/2026/aug/12/organoids-human-organs-cambridge-shift-drug-testing-medicines-animals) to replace animal testing, because “a mouse cannot tell us.”

The anomalous has scaling laws of its own. A congressman says the US [lured UAPs to a secret site](https://x.com/repericburlison/status/2088311067873026469) for intelligence officials, and a whistleblower claims Dick Cheney oversaw an [alien “detainment situation”](https://nypost.com/2026/08/15/us-news/late-vp-dick-cheney-oversaw-alien-detainment-situation-ufo-whistleblower-claims/) with telepathic pilots. And in Britain, [Count Binface](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/ce97mm3vvemo), a self-styled intergalactic space warrior, took 26.9% and 9,455 votes to finish second in the Clacton by-election, his best result ever.

Satire is dead, long live satire.
