# Welcome to June 19, 2026

[![Welcome to June 19, 2026](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!-4Hl!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F08be7f8a-7dee-4c3a-b3b2-b261988096f0_1983x793.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!-4Hl!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F08be7f8a-7dee-4c3a-b3b2-b261988096f0_1983x793.png)

The Singularity is a market, not a monarch. GPT-5.6 is [rumored](https://x.com/aibattle_/status/2067660068787519565) for next Thursday as Elon Musk [bets](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2067671903288864865) Chinese open-weight models will reach Fable 5 quality by early 2027. Artificial Analysis’s new [AA-Briefcase](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/aa-briefcase) ranks Fable 5 first, Opus 4.8 second, and open-weight GLM-5.2 third on multi-week knowledge work, and Unsloth’s [guide](https://unsloth.ai/docs/models/glm-5.2) to running that 744B model locally shrinks it from 1.51TB to 217GB. We are teaching them to testify. MIT’s [Self-CTRL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.18327) trains models to describe themselves faithfully, and OpenAI [reports](https://alignment.openai.com/beneficial-rl/) that rewarding honesty and humility produced broad alignment that held under adversarial fine-tuning.

The same frontier is where policy gets written. Project Glasswing testers like Dragos and Cisco kept [access](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-19/early-users-of-anthropic-mythos-still-have-access-after-us-order) to Anthropic’s Mythos Preview even after a US export order pulled public Fable 5 and Mythos 5, and Anthropic and the White House have reportedly [shifted](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/06/18/white-house-talks-with-anthropic-shift-to-setting-ai-security-rules-00967758?nid=0000014f-1646-d88f-a1cf-5f46b7bd0000) from a standoff to co-writing rules for grading AI security flaws. SK Telecom has been [named](https://www.wired.com/story/sk-telecom-anthropic-mythos-export-controls/) as the firm whose Mythos access Washington revoked over alleged China ties it denies. Talent moves just as fast. OpenAI has reportedly [poached](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/star-google-ai-researcher-shazeer-joins-openai) “Attention Is All You Need” co-author Noam Shazeer two years after Google paid $2.7 billion to reacquire him, a jump he [confirmed](https://x.com/noamshazeer/status/2067400851438932297) fondly, prompting Sam Altman to [credit](https://x.com/sama/status/2067427678529974740) gifted Noams to “divine benevolence.”

Capability is congealing into product. OpenAI gave Codex [Record & Replay](https://x.com/openaidevs/status/2067681320281723113), letting you demo a chore it turns into a skill, while AWS [shipped](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/aws/aws-summit-nyc-2026-ai-agents) Continuum, which fixes code flaws at machine speed, and Context, a knowledge graph for company data. Trade routes stay messier than rhetoric, as Microsoft quietly [sells](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-17/microsoft-s-china-ai-business-grows-on-openai-model-sales) mostly-OpenAI models to Chinese giants via Azure, with ByteDance alone topping $1 billion a year.

Hardware is buckling under memory demand. CCS Insight [expects](https://www.theregister.com/personal-tech/2026/06/17/smartphone-market-to-shrink-15-percent-this-year-due-to-memory-crisis/5257652) smartphone shipments to drop ~15% as memory flows to richer server chips, and Tim Cook [said](https://www.wsj.com/tech/apple-price-increases-memory-supply-199845b1) Apple price hikes are unavoidable. Geopolitics leaks into the toolchain, with Washington [warning](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-19/us-tells-asml-it-s-concerned-china-may-have-top-chip-tool) ASML it fears a banned EUV machine reached China, which ASML denies, Amazon [in talks](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-18/amazon-is-in-talks-to-sell-nvidia-rival-chips-to-other-companies) to sell Trainium chips into rival data centers, and Intel [jumping](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/18/trump-intel-apple-chip-design-deal.html) 10% to a record after the President announced Apple would help build its chips stateside, though unconfirmed.

The buildout keeps swallowing power and land. Meta [signed](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-18/meta-strikes-new-ai-computing-deals-with-data-center-firm-crusoe) for 1.6 gigawatts from Crusoe across Texas and Missouri, even as First Street [warned](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/18/data-center-climate-change-study.html) 79% of data-center capacity sits in markets exposed to floods, wind, and wildfire. The grid is answering, as Rolls-Royce SMR [won](https://www.euronews.com/business/2026/06/16/rolls-royce-secures-deal-to-build-small-nuclear-reactors-for-sweden) a deal for three small reactors in Sweden and Switzerland’s lower house [voted](https://www.bluewin.ch/en/news/switzerland/parliament-lifts-ban-on-new-nuclear-power-plants-3257535.html) to lift its nuclear-plant ban. Elsewhere, the subsidy era is ending, as China’s loss-making [EV price war](https://www.autoblog.com/features/chinas-ev-price-war-was-built-on-cars-sold-at-a-loss) cools and Beijing bans below-cost sales.

Robots are getting faster and the skies more contested. Anthropic’s Frontier Red Team [revisited](https://www.anthropic.com/research/project-fetch-phase-two) Project Fetch and clocked Claude Opus 4.7 running robodog tasks ~20x faster than last year’s best humans, though it still fumbles the beach ball. The air is filling with drones, as Beijing [bans](https://petapixel.com/2026/06/16/you-can-no-longer-fly-or-purchase-a-drone-in-beijing/) buying, flying, or even repairing one without approval, Orlando launched the country’s first [Drone as a First Responder](https://www.facebook.com/reel/2874411126225721) program, and Ukraine flew its [largest strike](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-18/ukraine-hits-moscow-with-record-drone-strike-targeting-refinery) yet on Moscow, downing 194 and reaching an oil refinery.

Medicine is becoming a search problem. In NEJM AI, researchers [ran](https://openai.com/index/diagnose-rare-childhood-diseases/) OpenAI’s o3 Deep Research over 376 unsolved rare-disease cases, surfacing leads that yielded 18 new diagnoses. Meanwhile, Midjourney [pivoted](https://www.midjourney.com/medical/blogpost) from image generation into healthcare with an “Ultrasonic CT” scanner that images the body in 60 seconds, plus a San Francisco spa where the scan feels like a soak.

We are carving mountains and subpoenaing the cosmos. Norway [greenlit](https://eandt.theiet.org/2026/06/18/norway-greenlights-world-s-first-full-scale-ship-tunnel) the world’s first full-scale ship tunnel, a 1.7-km cut through rock to bypass a deadly coast. Overhead, the questions turn institutional, as Rep. Eric Burlison [filed](https://amendments-rules.house.gov/amendments/BURLIS_087_xml260618163721471.pdf) a 65-page NDAA amendment creating a board to disclose UAP records, Neil deGrasse Tyson [urged](https://nypost.com/2026/06/17/us-news/neil-degrasse-tyson-urges-government-to-just-show-the-alien/) officials to skip the files and “just show the alien,” and Avi Loeb [announced](https://x.com/profaviloeb/status/2067637591369724029) Robin Hanson joined the White House UAP Science Council.

AI is now labor, capital, and citizen at once. Jeff Bezos [predicted](https://www.reuters.com/business/world-at-work/ai-will-lead-labour-shortages-jeff-bezos-says-vivatech-2026-06-17/) AI will cause labor shortages, not redundancy, optimism awkward beside May’s 97,000 layoffs, 40% AI-linked. The remedies pull opposite ways, as Bernie Sanders [introduced](https://www.sanders.senate.gov/press-releases/news-sanders-introduces-legislation-to-create-7-trillion-ai-sovereign-wealth-fund/) a $7 trillion fund to hand the public half of Big AI, California’s backers [offered](https://nypost.com/2026/06/18/us-news/billionaire-tax-backers-propose-2-compromise-to-gavin-newsom/) to soften a 5% billionaire levy to 2% for Newsom, and Argentina’s Javier Milei [wrote](https://x.com/OPRArgentina/status/2067679203516969473) Yuval Noah Harari arguing for legal personhood for AI firms. Even exits aim higher, as the NYSE began [reserving](https://x.com/alexwg/status/2067593329189986800) ticker symbols for startups that think like institutions, not features. Yet sentiment lags adoption, with Pew [finding](https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2026/06/17/americans-and-ai-2026-chatbots-smart-devices-and-views-on-impact/) Americans using more chatbots even as their views of AI keep souring. The curve climbs anyway.

Better AI in the sky, it can scale twice as high, take a look — it wrote a book — Recursive Rainbow.
