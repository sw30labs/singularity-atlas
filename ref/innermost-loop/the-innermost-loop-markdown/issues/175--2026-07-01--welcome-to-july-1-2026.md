# Welcome to July 1, 2026

[![Welcome to July 1, 2026](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!tdqI!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F52a5a1fc-901e-4c7c-9176-e9fb7a209f53_1983x793.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!tdqI!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F52a5a1fc-901e-4c7c-9176-e9fb7a209f53_1983x793.png)

The Singularity just cleared customs. [Anthropic is redeploying Claude Fable 5 globally](https://www.anthropic.com/news/redeploying-fable-5), after Washington lifted export controls on Fable 5 and Mythos 5, pairing the return with fresh cybersecurity safeguards, a Glasswing-partner framework for scoring jailbreak severity, and [pledges to the US government](https://x.com/deredleritt3r/status/2072112364690833604) to detect risks, co-author standards for future models, and flag malicious activity. Not everyone is applauding. [Alex Stamos](https://x.com/alexstamos/status/2072182638878863777) welcomed the White House to “the AI safety club” while calling the détente a “huge own goal for the US,” betting Chinese models pull ahead on cyber within six months. The same lab kept shipping. [Claude Sonnet 5](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-sonnet-5) nears Opus 4.8 at introductory prices of $2 and $10 per million tokens, though [analysts note](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2072082171830157706) it runs cheaper by the token yet pricier by the task, burning enough extra reasoning to outbill Opus anyway. Efficiency is contagious. Stanford and Together AI proposed [“intelligence per watt,”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.07885) finding local models can already handle 88.7% of chat queries, [OpenAI](https://www.theinformation.com/newsletters/ai-agenda/openai-discovers-new-way-cut-inference-costs-half) quietly halved inference costs, [Google’s TabFM](https://research.google/blog/introducing-tabfm-a-zero-shot-foundation-model-for-tabular-data/), a zero-shot foundation model for tabular data, retired feature engineering behind a single BigQuery SQL call, and [Nano Banana 2 Lite and Gemini Omni Flash](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-omni-flash-nano-banana-2-lite/) shipped cheaper pixels and video. Meanwhile, doomerists at [MIRI](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.28694) now want a surveillance-state regime of polygraphs, prison sentences, and embedded auditors to police intelligence itself, criminalizing curiosity to keep the AI future at bay.

Science is speeding the other way. [Anthropic’s Claude Science](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-science-ai-workbench) wires 60-plus databases into one reproducible workbench, and [Basecamp’s EDEN models](https://www.genengnews.com/topics/artificial-intelligence/claude-science-is-here-antibiotics-designed-by-text-prompt-among-applications/) now let researchers text-prompt antibiotics against drug-resistant pathogens. [OpenAI’s GeneBench-Pro](https://openai.com/index/introducing-genebench-pro/), a benchmark of 129 research-level computational-biology problems, shows headroom remains, with its best model passing just 28.7%, rising to 31.5% in Pro mode. To close such gaps, [Amazon](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/30/amazon-launches-new-1-billion-fde-org-following-openai-and-anthropic/) raised a $1 billion army of forward-deployed engineers to embed agents inside every enterprise.

The bottleneck has moved from code to concrete, and concrete has politics. [South Korea’s June exports](https://asia.nikkei.com/economy/south-korean-exports-hit-monthly-high-on-booming-ai-chip-demand) topped $100 billion for the first time on record SK Hynix and Samsung shipments, while [ByteDance](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-01/tiktok-creator-bytedance-picks-brazil-for-largest-data-center-outside-china) planted a $39 billion data center in Brazil’s Ceará and [Amazon’s “Fastnet” cable](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-07-01/amazon-cable-shows-ireland-s-status-as-ai-hub-and-highlights-security-risk) surfaced in Ireland’s County Cork to feed European AI. The friction is local. [The Bitcoin Policy Institute](https://x.com/samlyman33/status/2071934009928532453) blames a China-linked Marxist group for stalling $23.6 billion in US data center projects, [Henrico County](https://www.404media.co/henrico-virginia-datacenter-energy-cost-email/) begged schools to kill the lights as data centers spiked its rates 25%, and [SpaceX](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-30/spacex-drops-starlink-costs-in-memphis-amid-data-center-pushback) halved Starlink prices in Memphis to placate neighbors of xAI’s Colossus.

Atoms are the new frontier. [Tesla’s first Optimus humanoid line](https://x.com/sawyermerritt/status/2072153222177870293) is being installed in Fremont with dozens more planned, [UBTech’s $17,650 U1](https://asia.nikkei.com/business/companies/china-s-ubtech-launches-lifelike-humanoid-robots-for-consumers) offers silicone companionship in his-and-hers models in China, and [South Korea](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/06/south-korea-plans-to-train-entire-military-as-drone-warriors/) will drill its entire military into drone operators backed by counter-drone lasers and microwaves. Overhead, [Blue Origin](https://x.com/andrewcurran_/status/2071997418087043310) will fly New Glenn again this year by switching to a hybrid pad that stacks the stages flat then tips the rocket upright, [China](https://www.china-in-space.com/p/state-owned-firms-confident-in-space) eyes a 10-kilowatt orbital datacenter by 2027, and [the DOT](https://www.forbes.com/sites/suzannerowankelleher/2026/06/30/faa-supersonic-flight-no-boom/) moved to end the 53-year ban on supersonic flight over US land. Beneath it all, [CERN](https://home.cern/cern-bids-farewell-to-the-lhc-and-enters-long-shutdown-3/) powered down the LHC to build a tenfold-brighter 2030 successor to pin down the Higgs and probe beyond the Standard Model.

The wetware is next. [Meta’s Brain2Qwerty v2](https://ai.meta.com/blog/brain2qwerty-brain-ai-human-communication/) reads sentences from magnetoencephalography recordings at 61% accuracy with no surgery and open code, while [Neuralink](https://x.com/neuralink/status/2072026718467166512) successfully threaded electrodes through the dura, the brain’s armor, without ever cutting it. Biology is shifting from discovery to design. [One startup’s virtual heart](https://x.com/mihailoxyz/status/2071980122207642075) turned a deadly pill into its safe twin in three hours on a laptop, a fix that once cost $6 billion, and [Conception](https://www.conception.bio/science-and-updates/the-first-early-human-eggs-from-stem-cells) grew the first human eggs from blood-derived stem cells.

The state is being fenced in and pried open. [The Supreme Court](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/25pdf/25-112_0am4.pdf) ruled geofence warrants demand probable cause, affirming privacy in your phone’s location trail, just as [House testimony](https://x.com/repluna/status/2071986774893548024) alleged the CIA continued Nazi human experiments after Nuremberg. Congress wants the other files opened. [Rep. Burlison](https://x.com/repericburlison/status/2071740372930400761) pushed a UAP Disclosure Act, [asking why it took 80 years](https://x.com/repericburlison/status/2072001147347308695), while [Rep. Burchett](https://x.com/uapwatchers/status/2072067619645968567) says members were briefed on five crash sites and [nonhuman “lifeforms”](https://x.com/uapjames/status/2071996668208136467) both dead and alive, plus vehicles that may have arrived without crashing at all.

Money is being rewritten. [Ukraine](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/06/29/ukraine-transfers-usd8-3-million-in-seized-crypto-amid-potential-plans-for-strategic-reserve) moved $8.3 million in seized crypto toward a strategic reserve, a first for the country, and [140 firms including Visa and BlackRock](https://www.theblock.co/post/406736/visa-stripe-coinbase-join-open-usd-stablecoin-shares-reserve-revenue) launched Open USD, a stablecoin sharing reserve yield. [SpaceX](https://www.semafor.com/article/06/29/2026/spacex-us-government-discuss-stock-donation-for-trump-accounts) may seed children’s 530A accounts with donated stock, a stab at universal basic equity. Yet the labor apocalypse keeps missing. AI-heavy firms [grew white-collar headcount 10.2%, with entry-level roles up 12%](https://www.ft.com/content/8026eac6-16ad-467d-b8c3-c48c5af684e6), and [OpenAI’s economist](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-30/ai-won-t-be-substitute-for-human-workers-openai-economist-says) insists AI won’t make workers superfluous.

Reports of labor’s death are greatly exaggerated.
