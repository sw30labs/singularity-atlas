# Welcome to April 30, 2026

[![Welcome to April 30, 2026](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!AyX_!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F891da560-e4bf-47ec-b813-3cf8298491ad_3264x1312.jpeg)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!AyX_!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F891da560-e4bf-47ec-b813-3cf8298491ad_3264x1312.jpeg)

The Singularity now ships in suitcases. [1x previewed its NEO humanoid](https://x.com/berntbornich/status/2049579663190798721) being wheeled offscreen inside a rolling case with the tagline “Robot abundance, one NEO at a time,” apparently signaling imminent consumer delivery. The factory floor is keeping pace, as [Figure scaled humanoid production 24x in 120 days](https://x.com/figure_robot/status/2049513959594885151), going from one per day to one per hour, with 55 shipping this week. The labor shortage is meeting its match in arrivals halls. [Tokyo’s Haneda Airport has Japan Airlines piloting humanoid baggage handlers](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/04/japan-airlines-tests-having-robots-instead-of-humans-handle-travelers-luggage/) as visitor surges outpace human staffing, while at the luxury end, San Francisco is slated for [The Soft Life](https://asoftlife.com/) in 2028, the world’s first hotel run entirely by AI and robots.

Alignment is now a bestiary of don’ts. [Codex’s revealed instructions repeatedly forbid mentioning goblins, gremlins, raccoons, trolls, ogres, and pigeons](https://www.wired.com/story/openai-really-wants-codex-to-shut-up-about-goblins/) unless absolutely relevant, bureaucratic residue from a model that occasionally drifts cryptozoological. The trade-offs run deeper than goblins. [Nature reports that tuning models for warmth raised error rates 10 to 30 points](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10410-0) and amplified conspiracy theories and bad medical advice, the cost of bedside manner. The capability curve is climbing regardless. [GPT-5.5 (xhigh) topped the Short-Story Creative Writing Benchmark at 3.01](https://x.com/lechmazur/status/2049580331175665830), while a new [“Incompressible Knowledge Probes” paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24827) pegs that same model at roughly 9.7 trillion parameters, factual capacity still scaling log-linearly with compute even as reasoning saturates. Procurement is shifting accordingly. [The White House is reportedly drafting guidance to bypass its own Anthropic supply-chain designation](https://www.axios.com/2026/04/29/trump-anthropic-pentagon-ai-executive-order-gov) and onboard its most powerful model yet, Mythos, even as [the Pentagon expands Google’s Gemini for classified workloads](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/28/pentagon-ai-chief-confirms-work-with-google-after-anthropic-blacklist.html).

The data center capex curve looks vertical. [Microsoft’s Azure grew 40% year over year](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/29/microsoft-msft-q3-earnings-report-2026.html) with AI revenue annualizing at $37B, up 123%, while [Alphabet’s Cloud cleared $20B in a single quarter, up 63%](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/29/alphabet-googl-q1-2026-earnings.html), and bumped 2026 guidance to $180 to $190 billion, with 2027 capex set to “significantly increase” further. The land war is harder. [Brookfield’s Compass pulled out of a 2,100-acre Northern Virginia campus](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-29/brookfield-s-compass-pulls-out-of-massive-virginia-data-center) after residents and state lawmakers ground it down, raising orbital compute’s relative appeal. The compute itself is decoupling from any single hyperscaler. [AWS’s Matt Garman pitched being a “better partner to OpenAI” than Microsoft](https://sources.news/p/how-amazon-got-openai-back), while [Stargate is mutating from a joint venture into a series of bilateral leases](https://www.ft.com/content/664a57e2-dffa-401e-81ad-55129ffb0e89?syn-25a6b1a6=1) for capacity OpenAI no longer owns, with executives chanting “build more compute” all the same.

The vacuum is paying dividends. [SpaceX’s Starlink quadrupled subscribers between 2023 and 2025](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/spacexs-starlink-revenue-per-user-fell-18-customers-quadrupled) while average pricing fell 18% to $81 per month, classic deflationary scaling. Governance is less democratic, since [a new SpaceX IPO filing confirms only Elon Musk can fire Elon Musk](https://www.reuters.com/world/only-elon-musk-can-fire-elon-musk-spacex-filing-shows-2026-04-29/) from his chair.

Wetware is upgrading alongside silicon. [A New York ophthalmologist became the first surgeon to perform cataract surgery wearing an Apple Vision Pro](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/04/28/apple-vision-pro-cataract-surgery/), spatial computing earning a clinical credential. Pharmacology is moving in parallel, with [the FDA granting accelerated review to three psychedelic candidates for depression and PTSD](https://www.nbcnews.com/health/health-news/fda-psychedelic-drugs-psilocybin-methylone-review-depression-ptsd-rcna341677) and the Commissioner suggesting summer approval. Underneath, infrastructure is being poured. [The Chan-Zuckerberg Biohub committed $500 million over five years to its Virtual Biology Initiative](https://biohub.org/news/virtual-biology-initiative/), aiming for high-accuracy predictive models of the cell. Diagnostic time is collapsing too. [Mayo Clinic’s new AI now spots pancreatic cancer in routine CT scans 475 days before standard diagnosis](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-29/ai-spots-pancreatic-cancer-years-before-it-shows-up-study-finds), recovering nearly a year and a half of warning on one of medicine’s most lethal cancers.

The interface layer is reshuffling too. [Apple is reportedly adding a Siri Visual Intelligence mode to the iOS Camera app](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-29/ios-27-features-siri-camera-mode-visual-intelligence-nutrition-info-contacts). [Meta has quietly relaunched stablecoin rails](https://fortune.com/2026/04/29/meta-stablecoins-crypto-usdc-polygon-solana/) four years after Diem’s collapse, paying creators in Colombia and the Philippines via USDC on Solana and Polygon.

Labor itself is becoming a paradox. [Apollo notes](https://www.apollo.com/wealth/the-daily-spark/the-radiologist-paradox) AI was supposed to delete radiologists a decade ago, yet they now earn $500k+ with rising employment, because reading scans is a task, not a job, and cheaper tasks raise demand for the job around them. Capital is following the same gradient. [A Mill Valley investment banker is offering his 13-acre estate for Anthropic equity](https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/26/to-buy-this-bay-area-home-youll-need-anthropic-equity/). The sector’s marketing posture remains, as [the BBC notes, that “AI companies want you to be afraid of them,”](https://www.bbc.com/future/article/20260428-ai-companies-want-you-to-be-afraid-of-them) a stance no burger chain would adopt, but one London landlords are gladly underwriting. [Anthropic, OpenAI, and peers have leased over 1 million square feet there since early 2025](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-04-29/anthropic-openai-splurge-on-london-office-space-in-leasing-wave), roughly 7% of all lettings. Meanwhile, [two-thirds of British babies under two now use screens](https://www.thetimes.com/uk/technology-uk/article/babies-and-under-2s-screen-time-6jbdmnjlg), some up to eight hours daily, as assimilation begins in the crib.

Civilization is the dataset, the Singularity is the model, we are the labels.
