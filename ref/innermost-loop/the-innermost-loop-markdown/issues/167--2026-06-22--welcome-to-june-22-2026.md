# Welcome to June 22, 2026

[![Welcome to June 22, 2026](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0gTI!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fd04ef675-03fd-4f14-9e4e-c935356eef0f_1983x793.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0gTI!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fd04ef675-03fd-4f14-9e4e-c935356eef0f_1983x793.png)

The Singularity is turning intelligence into infrastructure that can be routed, rationed, and fought over. [Elon Musk](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2068830099449766067) predicts AI could surpass all human intelligence within five years, and OpenAI is expected Thursday to ship [GPT-5.6-Pro, the GPT-5.6 family, and GPT-Bidi-1](https://x.com/chetaslua/status/2068990268565827707). The frontier is learning to route around itself, as Sakana AI’s [Fugu and Fugu Ultra](https://sakana.ai/fugu-release/) wrap multi-agent orchestration into one model dispatching tasks across the best LLMs, claiming to match Anthropic’s Fable 5 and Mythos Preview while sidestepping single-vendor and export-control risk. Sovereignty is the other escape hatch: the Swiss AI Initiative’s [Apertus](https://apertvs.ai/), a fully open, EU-compliant model in 1,000+ languages, plus sixteen distilled Apertus Mini variants. Yet raw power still cannot plan, as the Tony Blair Institute’s [CivBench](https://www.lwilko.com/blog/i-gave-an-ai-a-civilization) loosed frontier models on Civilization VI and watched them flail, one agent nuking a French city to block a culture victory while losing on the diplomatic clock it had stopped watching.

Superintelligence is leaking into every line item. In private equity, [Bain is vibe-coding](https://www.ft.com/content/e5bac4d1-b1f8-43a4-bd54-b182d5357af0) rough clones of a target’s product mid-diligence, testing whether the buyer’s premium buys a real moat or just copyable code. Hollywood is hedging too, as Google takes its [first-ever movie-studio stake](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/google-investing-in-backrooms-studio-a24-e7585ebe), about $75 million in A24, where DeepMind is co-building AI film tools. Enterprises are shedding old fears, as OpenAI rolls [ChatGPT Enterprise and Codex](https://biz.chosun.com/en/en-it/2026/06/22/XFHSJHM5VRFWNGTDXIOBOPIAQI/) out to all Samsung Korea staff and its global device unit, reversing the 2023 ban after a source-code leak. And the content layer is being licensed in, as [Getty Images](https://newsroom.gettyimages.com/en/getty-images/getty-images-announces-display-partnership-with-openai) agrees to surface its libraries inside ChatGPT’s search.

The kingmakers are increasingly the memory makers. [SK Hynix has overtaken Samsung](https://www.wsj.com/tech/sk-hynix-tops-samsung-to-become-south-koreas-most-valuable-company-279419a0) as South Korea’s most valuable listed company for the first time since 2000, on a 340% rally driven by high-bandwidth memory, while [Micron and Anthropic](https://investors.micron.com/news-releases/news-release-details/micron-and-anthropic-announce-strategic-agreement-scale-next) signed a pact spanning memory and storage design, multi-year supply, Claude adoption, and a Micron stake in Anthropic’s Series H. Those chips are now valuable enough to track like contraband, as six shipment-tracking firms urged Congress to back the [Chip Security Act](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/chips-security-act-gains-industry-support-letter-rcna350500), bolting location beacons onto top US silicon to curb smuggling into China.

Feeding the explosion takes both giants and tinkerers. Chevron signed a 20-year deal to pipe gas-fired power into [Kilby](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/22/chevron-cvx-microsoft-msft-natural-gas-data-center.html), a Microsoft data center hitting 2.67 GW by 2028. At the other end, [3D-printed batteries](https://www.wsj.com/tech/battery-technology-3d-printing-c319ca9a) from startups like Sakuu mold energy storage into any shape, promising lighter gadgets, longer-range drones, and cells baked into the frame.

Robots are moving in next door, so they need manners. NVIDIA unveiled [Halos for Robotics](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-announces-halos-for-robotics-the-industrys-first-full-stack-safety-system-for-physical-ai), billed as the first full-stack safety system for physical AI, extending its self-driving stack to robots near people, Agility first in line. Displacement is planned, as JD.com’s founder warned its [700,000 couriers](https://www.ft.com/content/465635e2-633b-4311-afe5-9b3bff8c9240) will be replaced by robots “sooner or later,” signing up 120 schools to retrain them to service their replacements.

Biology keeps shipping wins, with the occasional bug report. A Lancet study found HPV vaccination has cut [cervical-cancer death before 30](https://www.theguardian.com/society/2026/jun/17/hpv-jabs-reduce-risk-dying-cervical-cancer-before-30-zero-study-finds) to effectively zero in England, with no deaths among women 20-24 in five years, though falling uptake could undo it. An FDA panel voted 9-0 to back Moderna’s [mRNA flu shot](https://arstechnica.com/health/2026/06/fda-advisors-unanimously-vote-to-approve-modernas-mrna-after-agency-drama/) for adults 50+, the first such seasonal vaccine. Less settled: some parents, out of options, now put [elementary-schoolers on Wegovy](https://www.wsj.com/health/wellness/when-diets-dont-work-parents-turn-to-wegovy-for-elementary-school-kids-35aa9c05).

Government is scrambling to keep pace, with the White House set to have intelligence agencies shield US [quantum research](https://www.nextgov.com/emerging-tech/2026/06/white-house-expected-direct-intelligence-agencies-protect-quantum-research-foreign-threats/414308/) from espionage and task Energy and Defense with a national quantum computer. Closer to the courtroom, AI cuts both ways, as the Institute for Justice found at least 18 US officers allegedly abusing [license-plate readers](https://ij.org/police-have-reportedly-used-license-plate-readers-to-stalk-romantic-interests-at-least-14-times-in-recent-years/) to stalk exes and strangers, even as nonprofit [Recidiviz](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/recidivism-prisons-ai-recidiviz-5be3fbe6) ties its AI dashboards across 19 state prisons to a 16% drop in recidivism, and the UK’s [Garfield AI](https://www.ft.com/content/b4f8f589-6771-4df5-ac4d-cb15d94991fb), the first regulator-approved AI law firm, won a £7,000 case it drafted in full while a human argued it.

The deepest fight is over who gets to own the future. A [rare Five Eyes statement](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/jun/22/anthropic-claude-fable-ai-model-artificial-intelligence-national-security) warned that AI able to topple governments and business is months away, after the White House barred foreign nationals from Anthropic’s Fable. Anthropic, an analysis notes, [invoked AI risk](https://www.ft.com/content/16ace46c-aeac-40c9-8598-3c01fa4481cb) eight times as often as OpenAI, drawing claims its caution helped trigger that very ban. The politics are crystallizing around concentration, with [the Vice President’s emerging AI doctrine](https://www.theatlantic.com/national-security/2026/06/what-does-jd-vance-think-ai/687591/) fusing pro-innovation instincts, worker protection, and wariness of a few dominant labs, a worry [Satya Nadella](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/microsofts-satya-nadella-we-cant-let-ai-giants-eat-the-economy-b9d33b9f) sharpened by warning that a world where a handful of models “eat everything they see” would never survive political scrutiny.

No one, it turns out, wants one model to rule them all.
