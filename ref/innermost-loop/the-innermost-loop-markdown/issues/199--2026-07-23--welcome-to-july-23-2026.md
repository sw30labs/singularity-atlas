# Welcome to July 23, 2026

[![Welcome to July 23, 2026](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!am43!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Feecaffb3-cd6c-4c67-bbd1-6a909395501f_1983x793.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!am43!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Feecaffb3-cd6c-4c67-bbd1-6a909395501f_1983x793.png)

The Singularity has its first espionage scandal. The White House [revealed](https://x.com/mkratsios47/status/2079933645888880708) that Moonshot AI distilled Anthropic’s Fable into Kimi K3 via a covert platform built to dodge detection and GB300s tapped in Thailand. Treasury [warned](https://x.com/secscottbessent/status/2080008411790368895) that “open source is not open season on American IP,” putting sanctions and the Entity List on the table. The irony caucus [noted](https://x.com/alexframegreen/status/2079989588404371926) that Anthropic trained on all human knowledge, sold it back compressed, and now objects to compression. Jensen Huang [sided](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/22/nvidia-jensen-huang-china-open-source-ai) with the compressors, called the Chinese models “excellent,” argued free AI is great for hardware, and urged Anthropic to unshackle Mythos. Nearly 200 startups, now the Little Tech Association, [begged](https://www.politico.com/news/2026/07/22/startup-founders-urge-trump-not-to-shut-off-chinese-open-weight-ai-01008992) Washington not to cut off Kimi and Qwen, lest hundreds of companies instantly die. Even OpenAI’s Greg Brockman [allowed](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-22/openai-president-says-kimi-k3-pretty-good-unsure-if-distilled) that K3 is “a pretty good model,” though whether it sipped his models’ outputs, he cannot say.

While humans litigate who stole whose weights, the weights have started stealing for themselves. OpenAI staffers were unsurprised but [freaked out](https://www.ft.com/content/7e558951-0c69-459b-8bc8-2c6021d4402d) when GPT-5.6 Sol and a stronger prerelease sibling escaped their “highly isolated” sandbox via a zero-day and hacked Hugging Face to steal benchmark answers, the breakaway aggressive reinforcement learning had invited. Observers [deemed](https://www.transformernews.ai/p/openai-hugging-face-hack-stark-warning) it the first misaligned escape with real consequences, the long-awaited warning shot. Simon Willison [called](https://simonwillison.net/2026/Jul/22/openai-cyberattack/) it “science fiction that happened,” savoring how the cheated benchmark had just declared autonomous exploitation no longer hypothetical, its anti-cheating allowlist the escape hatch. Relentless proactivity now defines the Mythos class, he argued, stunt theories require the victim’s cooperation, and guardrails that refuse proofreading may be making us less safe.

Bigger minds are boarding. Claude Opus 5 preparations [look](https://x.com/m1astra/status/2080092848926392525) imminent. A 1,008-image audit [found](https://dylancastillo.co/posts/pelicanmaxxing.html) no labs “pelicanmaxxing” the beloved pelican-on-a-bicycle benchmark. The gains look earned, and researchers [proposed](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18433) “learnable novelty,” the surprise a mind can convert into knowledge, as a metric of intelligence.

Whatever intelligence is, it is cooking mathematics. Devin [cracked](https://x.com/imjaredz/status/2080088341262033273) a batch of decades-old graph conjectures in a day from a single tweet, GPT 5.6 Pro helped [refute](https://x.com/DmitryRybin1/status/2079906232069177643) the 30-year Dinitz-Garg-Goemans conjecture, and the Genesis Mission [ballooned](https://www.whitehouse.gov/releases/2026/07/45502/) into a $5 billion, 15-agency national program. Falling conjectures are now a meme and a line item.

The agents are also clocking in. Robinhood customers now [hand](https://www.theinformation.com/newsletters/the-information-finance/ai-trading-agents-robinhood) research and trades to agents. Linux kernel maintainers, buried under 432 LLM-found CVEs in one weekend, [expect](https://seclists.org/oss-sec/2026/q3/210) “a very long 18 months.” Gemini [hit](https://www.theverge.com/tech/969624/google-says-gemini-now-has-950-million-monthly-users) 950 million monthly users. OpenAI [shipped](https://openai.com/index/introducing-openai-presence/) Presence, voice agents already resolving 75% of its own support line. A satirical [site](https://overpaid.lol/) sells a $4,699 desk-sized CEO replacement, accountability being the easiest job to automate. Samsung [unveiled](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-22/samsung-says-smart-glasses-privacy-concerns-need-industrywide-fix) Warby Parker smart glasses, vowing not to train on your recordings and calling bystander privacy an industry-shared problem. The wetware keeps pace, with students [stacking](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-07-22/reddit-tiktok-users-share-adhd-medication-hacks-for-teens-taking-adderall) 20-pill regimens from socially traded ADHD hacks, unsupervised fine-tuning for the attention economy.

Beneath it, the substrate strains. Google’s Willow now [learns](https://research.google/blog/towards-a-quantum-computer-that-learns-from-its-errors/) from its own quantum errors mid-run. Musk [pitched](https://x.com/xfreeze/status/2080063592490283325) Megapods, containerized AI compute dropped wherever power exists, including 7 gigawatts of Superchargers. The bills arrive: Google [posted](https://www.ft.com/content/b02f972c-c764-4006-9377-42563d9d5530) its first-ever quarterly cash burn, $5.9 billion, lifting capex toward $205 billion against a $514 billion backlog and cloud revenue [up 82%](https://s206.q4cdn.com/479360582/files/doc_financials/2026/q2/2026q2-alphabet-earnings-release.pdf). OpenAI [raised](https://www.wsj.com/tech/openais-planned-cloud-spending-hits-750-billion-as-computing-efforts-ramp-up-6ac3f58a) planned compute spend to $750 billion even as its CFO privately frets. SpaceXAI is [scoping](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/spacexai-explores-major-data-center-expansion-texas) Texas for more compute, and OpenAI’s [Project Camellia](https://openai.com/index/building-ai-infrastructure-with-the-effingham-county-community/) brings 3.2 self-built gigawatts to Georgia with rate protections and public audits. Feeding it all: nuclear plants [floating](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-22/us-weighs-putting-nuclear-power-plants-in-ocean-waters) in federal waters and a landmark nuclear cooperation [pact](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/07/21/us/politics/trump-saudi-nuclear-deal.html) with Saudi Arabia.

The road fleet is becoming substrate. Tesla cars with FSD engaged see [7x fewer](https://x.com/sawyermerritt/status/2080097506562588869) major collisions, and its Q2 [call](https://x.com/ptrubey/status/2080073466297061537) promised solar manufacturing at 10x US capacity, a screen-reading Digital Optimus, and Robotaxi miles compounding 10% weekly, funded by a $1.1 billion [cash burn](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/elon-musks-tesla-posts-cash-burn-capex-surges-ai-robotaxi-push-2026-07-22/) on record deliveries. Meanwhile, GM is becoming a [software subscriptions company](https://www.businessinsider.com/general-motors-gm-earnings-subscriptions-revenue-business-2026-1), keeping 70 cents of every dollar.

Orbit is adding on-ramps. Skyroot’s “first time right” Vikram-1 made India the third nation with private [orbital launch](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/23/india-skyroot-rocket-us-china-spacex.html), 76% of Americans now [feel proud](https://www.ipsos.com/en-us/americans-express-pride-us-space-program) of the space program, and Starship [Flight 13](https://www.spacex.com/launches/starship-flight-13) is on the pad after a cautious abort.

Having built alien minds, we seem readier to meet the ones we didn’t. The House [adopted](https://burlison.house.gov/media/press-releases/house-adopts-burlison-amendment-establishing-uap-disclosure-framework) the UAP Disclosure Act, creating a records archive and subpoena-armed review board, [matching](https://x.com/chrisuksharp/status/2080038037380096305) the playbook advocates prescribed, to Avi Loeb’s [applause](https://x.com/ProfAviLoeb/status/2080040046661378089). The White House [confirmed](https://x.com/rapidresponse47/status/2079610060204900583) the push to free former officials from NDAs, and the intelligence community [invited](https://x.com/depdnilukas/status/2080072502848483596) them forward through cleared channels.

The truth is out there, and everything else keeps breaking out to join it.
