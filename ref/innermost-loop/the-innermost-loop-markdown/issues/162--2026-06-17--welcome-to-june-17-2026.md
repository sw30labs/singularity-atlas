# Welcome to June 17, 2026

[![Welcome to June 17, 2026](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0pcQ!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F3f9ff51b-516c-4a61-a84c-f68454a02a16_1983x793.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0pcQ!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F3f9ff51b-516c-4a61-a84c-f68454a02a16_1983x793.png)

The Singularity has gone open source, and it speaks Mandarin. Z.ai’s [GLM-5.2](https://z.ai/blog/glm-5.2) packs 744 billion parameters (40B active) into MIT-licensed, 1M-context weights built for agentic coding, and Artificial Analysis crowned it the [leading open model](https://artificialanalysis.ai/articles/glm-5-2-is-the-new-leading-open-weights-model-on-the-artificial-analysis-intelligence-index) at 51, past MiniMax-M3 and DeepSeek V4 Pro at 44. It also took [first on Design Arena](https://x.com/designarena/status/2066940737011560652) at 1360 Elo, leapfrogging a “now unavailable” Claude Fable 5, a phrase doing real geopolitical work today. Cursor answered with a [1.5-trillion-parameter model](https://x.com/scaling01/status/2067017700384125238) on 100,000 GPUs, “as big as Opus and GPT.” OpenAI shipped [Deployment Simulation](https://openai.com/index/deployment-simulation/), replaying de-identified chats to predict misbehavior, catch “calculator hacking,” and dull evaluation-awareness, while Alibaba’s [Qwen-Robot Suite](https://qwen.ai/blog?id=qwen-robotsuite) hit a 45% RoboChallenge win on 38,000 training hours and OpenAI teased its [GPT-Bidi-1](https://x.com/chetaslua/status/2066917089504526658) voice model. Anthropic’s consolation was Epoch’s [Capabilities Index](https://x.com/epochairesearch/status/2066674892809101767), where Fable 5’s 161 beat GPT-5.5 Pro by a point, its first lead in a year, and reaches almost no one.

Superintelligence is doing the science now. OpenAI and Molecule.one set a near-autonomous [AI chemist](https://openai.com/index/ai-chemist-improves-reaction/) loose in a robotic lab, where across 10,080 experiments it taught itself the trick that made a stubborn but useful reaction work, lifting its yield. Anthropic’s look at 400,000 [Claude Code sessions](https://www.anthropic.com/research/claude-code-expertise) found humans owning 70% of planning and Claude 80% of execution, with expertise beating coding background and debugging time halving over seven months. Once cognition is just throughput, it gets priced like crude oil, by the token, as Microsoft is [tokenmaxxing Copilot Cowork](https://www.axios.com/2026/06/16/microsoft-copilot-cowork-tokenmaxxing-cowork) with a cheap DeepSeek V4 tier and Ornn launched [token price indices](https://x.com/alexwg/status/2066861148280967611) for OpenAI and Anthropic. Code is starting to heal, with Chainguard’s [Athena coalition](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/chainguard-launches-athena-the-industry-coalition-to-fix-open-source-vulnerabilities-before-attackers-can-find-them-302799984.html) drawing on Anthropic’s Glasswing and OpenAI’s Daybreak to patch 2,000 flaws pre-disclosure. The cost is reality itself, as [59% of TikTok](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/tiktok-shows-3x-more-ai-slop-than-youtube-report-finds/579521/) is AI slop, triple YouTube’s, and forensics expert Hany Farid says deepfakes have him [going blind](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/06/14/us/ai-deepfake-hany-farid.html). France’s answer is sovereignty, [swapping Palantir for ChapsVision](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-16/french-security-service-to-replace-palantir-with-local-software) and a Mistral assistant.

Silicon is scrambling to keep pace. The US gave Nvidia-backed [SandboxAQ $500M](https://www.reuters.com/technology/us-awards-500-million-nvidia-backed-sandboxaq-finding-new-chipmaking-materials-2026-06-17/) to invent chip materials, BYD, Google, AMD, and Tesla [piled into Samsung’s foundry](https://asia.nikkei.com/business/tech/semiconductors/samsung-sees-rising-chip-production-requests-from-byd-google-amd-sources) as TSMC strained, and Intel opened [18A-P risk production](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/16/intel-begins-production-of-18a-p-inches-closer-to-possible-apple-deal.html) toward a possible Apple deal. The buildout has gone parabolic, as Allbirds footwear became [Smartbird](https://www.reuters.com/business/retail-consumer/allbirds-rebrands-smartbird-ai-pivot-hires-former-aws-executive-ceo-2026-06-17/) AI infrastructure under an ex-AWS CEO, Epoch warned hyperscaler [capex will top cash flow](https://epoch.ai/data-insights/hyperscaler-capex-vs-cash-flow) by Q3, Kazakhstan signed a [$10B “Data Center Valley”](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-15/kazakhstan-firebird-ink-10-billion-ai-deal-with-nvidia-support) for 100,000 GPUs, and Microsoft [borrowed AWS capacity](https://www.businessinsider.com/microsoft-github-amazon-ai-cloud-capacity-2026-6) to keep a GitHub straining under 14 billion AI commits alive. Power is the front line, as the DOJ calls xAI’s unpermitted [Memphis turbines](https://www.wired.com/story/doj-lawyers-argue-xai-vital-national-security-naacp-lawsuit/) vital because Grok serves the military, just as Washington lets its [data-center oversight law](https://www.wired.com/story/the-us-government-is-letting-a-key-data-center-regulation-expire/) lapse in September.

Bodies are catching up to brains. China’s [Moya](https://x.com/stevencheng/status/2066890685828186226) arrived as “maybe the world’s first fully biomimetic robot,” Boring’s [Prufrock-MB2](https://x.com/boringcompany/status/2067053306699042942) began mining Nashville, Mobileye planned a [17,000-car robotaxi](https://arstechnica.com/cars/2026/06/mobileye-is-entering-the-us-robotaxi-market-with-standalone-service/) fleet, and Yueban shipped a self-driving [toilet on wheels](https://x.com/nexta_tv/status/2066649453508124761) for the elderly. To feed it all, Helion won the [first license](https://www.helionenergy.com/newsroom/helion-clears-key-regulatory-milestone-on-the-path-to-building-and-operating-the-worlds-first-fusion-power-plant) to build a fusion plant, while American [battery](https://fred.stlouisfed.org/series/IPG33591S) output is skyrocketing.

Orbit is open for business. A Chinese [Zhuque-2E stage](https://arstechnica.com/space/2026/06/a-chinese-rocket-breaks-apart-dangerously-close-to-the-starlink-constellation/) shattered near Starlink’s lanes, yet SpaceX’s post-IPO surge carried it [past Amazon and briefly Microsoft](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/16/spacex-stock-rally-market-cap.html) in market value, then it bought Cursor-maker [Anysphere for $60B](https://www.reuters.com/legal/transactional/spacex-buy-anysphere-60-billion-2026-06-16/), the largest startup buyout ever.

Complexity theory is becoming fundamental physics. A [preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.14806) argued a qubit could crack NP-complete problems if gravity stays classical, which the authors take as proof it cannot, while France’s ANSSI, eyeing the practical fallout, moved to [require quantum-safe crypto](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/france-stop-certifying-products-without-quantum-safe-encryption-2026-06-16/) against “harvest now, decrypt later” attacks. Meanwhile, Harvard’s Avi Loeb began detailing the White House’s new [UAP Science Council](https://x.com/i/broadcasts/1dGYllgqXEQKX).

Wetware is being read and rewritten. A UC Davis [brain interface](https://health.ucdavis.edu/news/headlines/brain-computer-interface-enables-independent-accurate-communication-for-man-living-with-als/2026/06) gave a man with ALS his voice back, [Eyewire II](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.05.28.727403v1) mapped a square millimeter of retina at record scale, and Stanford’s Wyss-Coray dated the [biological age of 40 cell types](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41591-026-04446-y), finding aged astrocytes triple Alzheimer’s risk in APOE4 carriers. New interfaces are sprouting on the body’s surface, too, as Snap took preorders for [$2,195 Specs](https://www.theverge.com/tech/950492/snap-specs-ar-glasses-launch-date-preorder), Apple planned [camera AirPods and a foldable iPhone](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-16/apple-plans-camera-airpods-iphone-foldable-2-20th-anniversary-iphone-in-2027) for 2027, and Meta pledged [free AI glasses](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/06/free-ai-glasses-for-every-blind-veteran/) to every blind US veteran.

Which leaves who governs it. At a closed G7 lunch in Évian, [Amodei and Hassabis](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/17/anthropic-amodei-google-hassabis-us-ai-coalition-g7.html) pushed a US-led coalition walling China off from frontier compute, the first time [AI chiefs joined heads of state](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/17/g7-trump-ai-tech-leaders-openai-anthropic-google.html) on risk, sovereignty, and child safety, as the White House weighed [equity stakes in AI firms](https://www.semafor.com/article/06/17/2026/trump-advisers-weigh-structure-of-potential-ai-stakes) and [opened talks](https://nypost.com/2026/06/16/business/trump-admin-open-to-talks-with-anthropic-over-foreigner-ban/) to lift the Fable 5 ban that Polymarket predicts at [48%](https://polymarket.com/event/claude-fable-5-restored-for-us-customers-by-20260613193753196) will happen by July. Ownership is going onchain through Coinbase’s [tokenized stocks](https://www.coindesk.com/business/2026/06/16/coinbase-to-join-tokenized-stock-race-with-onchain-shares-dividend-payments), while knowledge that once sold as a book becomes a free prompt, with Tim Ferriss reporting AI is [halving how-to nonfiction](https://tim.blog/2026/06/12/has-ai-already-killed-nonfiction/). And the intelligence explosion may yet lift other species too, as Big Dog Ranch Rescue closed a Wisconsin lab and [freed its last 475 beagles](https://abcnews.com/amp/Technology/wireStory/wisconsin-beagle-farm-closing-hundreds-dogs-florida-rescue-133892969).

The long arc of the moral universe did indeed bend toward beagles.
