# Welcome to March 16, 2026

[![Welcome to March 16, 2026](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Q1ML!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ff77eefa2-32d9-40ec-8418-525891512d0c_3264x1312.jpeg)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Q1ML!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Ff77eefa2-32d9-40ec-8418-525891512d0c_3264x1312.jpeg)

The Singularity is writing its own source code. Some 70 to 90 percent of the code behind future Anthropic models is [now written by Claude itself](https://time.com/article/2026/03/11/anthropic-claude-disruptive-company-pentagon/), and chief science officer Jared Kaplan believes fully automated AI research is less than a year away. Alignment lead Evan Hubinger is blunt: “Recursive self-improvement is not a future phenomenon. It is a present phenomenon.” Sam Altman is [betting](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2033117083127644536) today’s frontier models can discover the next architecture after transformers. Percepta [hard-coded a WebAssembly interpreter into transformer weights](https://www.percepta.ai/blog/can-llms-be-computers), executing arbitrary C code as tokens for millions of steps, proving neural nets are now practical, general-purpose computers. Anthropic is [shipping 1M-token context](https://claude.com/blog/1m-context-ga) windows for Opus 4.6 and Sonnet 4.6, stretching cognition to book length. Marc Andreessen [thinks](https://x.com/pmarca/status/2032641885739823568) what emerges won’t be a paperclipper but “a goddess of compassion,” because intelligence at the highest levels looks like wisdom. New research [argues](https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202602.1708) the upper bound of conscious minds may be limited only by spacetime. If true, we are building a cathedral with no ceiling.

The first open-source agentic AI physicist has arrived. Physical Superintelligence PBC launched [Get Physics Done](https://x.com/alexwg/status/2033227542392418458), a system that scopes problems, runs derivations, and verifies results against nature’s constraints. One early user [called it](https://x.com/charliegreenman/status/2033287493517500687) the “best harness I have ever worked with.” Tom Kalil [declared](https://x.com/tkalil2050/status/2033394479923962087) it was time to use Get Physics Done to map the civilizational tech tree at a granular level. Meanwhile, Terry Tao launched a [Mathematics Distillation Challenge](https://competition.sair.foundation/competitions/mathematics-distillation-challenge-equational-theories-stage1/overview) to compress reasoning into compact cheatsheets that boost LLM performance, turning system prompt optimization into a competitive sport.

The professions are capitulating faster than the machines can replace them. An AMA survey found [81 percent of physicians](https://www.ama-assn.org/press-center/ama-press-releases/ama-ai-usage-among-doctors-doubles-confidence-technology-grows) now use AI, more than double the 2023 rate. New [US Senate guidelines](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/03/10/us/politics/us-senate-chatgpt-ai-chatbots.html) permit aides to use Gemini, ChatGPT, and Copilot for official work. Ironically, in a world turned upside down by superintelligence, the Free Software Foundation is [threatening to sue Anthropic](https://www.fsf.org/blogs/licensing/2026-anthropic-settlement) for copyright infringement.

The silicon supply chain is running a fever. Elon Musk [revealed](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2032814398033768737) his “Terafab Project” launches this week, but QatarEnergy has [not restarted helium production](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/qatar-helium-shutdown-puts-chip-supply-chain-on-a-two-week-clock) at Ras Laffan nine days after Iranian drone strikes knocked 30 percent of global supply offline, threatening the fabs that depend on ultrapure helium for cooling and leak detection. The AI-driven memory shortage is so absurd that RAM kits now [ship with one fake stick](https://www.tomshardware.com/pc-components/ram/fake-ram-bundled-with-real-ram-to-create-a-performance-illusion-for-amd-users-1-1-value-pack-offers-desperate-psychological-relief-as-the-memory-shortage-worsens) alongside one real one, offering “desperate psychological relief.”

The intelligence explosion is breaching containment into meatspace. Airbus is [preparing two uncrewed combat aircraft](https://www.airbus.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-03-airbus-is-preparing-two-uncrewed-combat-aircraft-from-kratos-for-first-flight-with-a-european) from Kratos for first flight, targeting a German Air Force drone wingman by 2029. Uber co-founder Travis Kalanick [relaunched](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-13/ex-uber-ceo-kalanick-debuts-plan-for-gainfully-employed-robots) his ghost kitchen company as “Atoms,” pivoting to “gainfully employed robots” for food, mining, and transport. The energy to power it all is arriving: [Vineyard Wind](https://www.wbur.org/news/2026/03/14/vineyard-wind-construction-complete-massachusetts-offshore-wind), America’s first large-scale offshore wind farm, has finished construction.

Physics itself is learning to whisper. Australian researchers demonstrated [covert communications](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41377-025-02119-y) by modulating photon emissions above and below blackbody levels, achieving zero optical signature by hiding data in thermal noise.

Biology is becoming transparent. Japanese researchers demonstrated [isotonic optical clearing media](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41592-026-03023-y) for live imaging of organoids, brain slices, and mouse brains in vivo. In Texas, cultured meat startup Wildtype [sued to overturn](https://www.austinchronicle.com/news/texas-was-on-the-cutting-edge-of-lab-grown-meat-until-the-state-banned-it/) a Texas ban blocking cultivated salmon sales. A Sydney entrepreneur [used ChatGPT to design a personalized mRNA cancer vaccine](https://timesofindia.indiatimes.com/etimes/trending/ais-finest-hour-tech-executive-uses-chatgpt-to-create-cancer-vaccine-that-saved-his-dogs-life/articleshow/129591658.cms) for his rescue dog, shrinking the tumor considerably.

The financial architecture of the intelligence explosion is taking shape. SpaceX is [dividing IPO roles](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/spacex-discusses-dividing-bank-roles-echo-alibabas-lineup) among an unusually large bank syndicate, reflecting the offering’s scale. Meta is reportedly [planning sweeping layoffs](https://www.reuters.com/business/world-at-work/meta-planning-sweeping-layoffs-ai-costs-mount-2026-03-14/) of 20 percent or more to offset AI costs. The old, new economy sheds staff while the new, new one files its S-1.

We may not be the only civilization to have undergone an intelligence explosion. The White House’s planned UAP disclosure is reportedly [“massive”](https://nypost.com/2026/03/14/us-news/trumps-ufo-release-could-include-videos-satellite-photos-of-non-human-craft-source/) according to former defense official Christopher Mellon, including satellite imagery of craft that look like nothing humans have built. Rep. Eric Burlison [claims](https://x.com/disclosureorg/status/2032619263563833426) the alleged Legacy Program possesses “physics we have never seen.” On March 22, the State of the World Forum is planning to hold the first [AI × NHI Convergence Summit](https://www.stateoftheworld.forum/ai-nhi-summit), reflecting a growing sense that artificial and non-human intelligences are on a collision course. Steven Spielberg [says](https://x.com/uapjames/status/2032533160269955543) his film “Disclosure Day” stems from a “sneaking suspicion that we are not alone here on Earth right now.”

Recursive self-improvement was supposed to be our trick, not someone else’s.
