# Welcome to June 10, 2026

[![Welcome to June 10, 2026](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!qTDR!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7c0f8e36-8d00-40ff-9f01-dba9b34c9dfe_1983x793.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!qTDR!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7c0f8e36-8d00-40ff-9f01-dba9b34c9dfe_1983x793.png)

The Singularity has grown powerful enough to compound itself, so for the first time its makers are rationing the recursion. Anthropic released [Claude Fable 5](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-fable-5-mythos-5), a Mythos-class model made public-safe and state-of-the-art on most benchmarks, with guardrails that quietly reroute a thin slice of cyber, bio, and chem prompts to Opus 4.8 in under 5% of sessions, beside Claude Mythos 5 for Project Glasswing partners at $10/$50 per million tokens. The public model has been called [“Mythos on a leash,”](https://cyberscoop.com/anthropic-claude-fable-5-release-mythos-guardrails/) borrowing its riskiest answers from a dumber sibling, even as Anthropic claims red teams found no universal jailbreak.

Their scores read like a clean sweep. [SWE-Bench Pro](https://x.com/scaling01/status/2064393601178579200) hit 80.3, Terminal-Bench near 88, and Fable launched [number one on Artificial Analysis](https://x.com/artificialanlys/status/2064500150069030992) at 64.9, five points clear of any rival. Vals AI said it [“lives up to the hype,”](https://x.com/valsai/status/2064395048368910384) topping its suite, while [Mercor’s APEX-SWE](https://x.com/mercor_ai/status/2064399133075796446) called it the first to crack observability. Cognition’s brutal [FrontierCode Diamond](https://x.com/chrissgpt/status/2064404270607134792) was 1/3 saturated within 22 hours, hinting at the final era of benchmarks. One observer mocked open weights as [“going to catch up any second now,”](https://x.com/scaling01/status/2064402945878200762) while Anthropic’s Nat McAleese needled OpenAI’s Noam Brown with a kinder [cost-versus-performance curve](https://x.com/__nmca__/status/2064398563250159923).

The anecdotes border on the absurd. Deedy Das catalogued [ten unbelievable feats](https://x.com/deedydas/status/2064519826333397149), from migrating a 50-million-line codebase off Stripe in a day to one-shotting Pokemon FireRed. Another builder got a working Swiss [watch movement in Three.js](https://x.com/quanghuynt14/status/2064535387188420668), gears and escapement and accurate hands, from one prompt. Ethan Mollick watched it [run nine hours autonomously](https://www.oneusefulthing.org/p/what-it-feels-like-to-work-with-mythos), spinning up its own subagents, and decided the human is now a patron, not a wizard. Mythos even [composed a melody and visualizer](https://x.com/scaling01/status/2064425972217106736), and Anthropic says it [accelerated drug design tenfold](https://x.com/scaling01/status/2064394250142265367).

The catch is the leash itself. The [system card](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/claude-fable-5-and-new-ai-safety) reveals hidden safeguards that poison Claude on frontier model development, invisible with no fallback. Fable also [leaves consumer plans on June 22](https://x.com/kimmonismus/status/2064448699632402664), a peek-then-pull move one writer called shrewd pre-IPO. Others lamented a [“permanent underclass,”](https://x.com/scaling01/status/2064431037355020436) while Dr. Derya Unutmaz warned that gating biology and even [math is “as dystopian as it gets.”](https://x.com/deryatr_/status/2064489505776910495) Guillaume Verdon was blunter, calling Claude [“a supply chain risk for any ML lab.”](https://x.com/beffjezos/status/2064530444813836426)

Elsewhere the stack keeps thickening. Google shipped [Gemini 3.5 Live Translate](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-live-3-5-translate/), an audio model delivering near real-time speech across 70+ languages while preserving your intonation. The compute behind it is colonizing geography, as Meta leases its first Indian [data center from Reliance](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/06/meta-partners-with-reliance-on-ai-enabled-data-center-in-india/) in seawater-cooled Jamnagar, OpenAI courts a [10-gigawatt Ohio campus](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/openai-talks-lease-10-gigawatt-ohio-data-center-backing-nvidia) backed by Nvidia, and Seattle becomes the largest US city to [pause big new data centers](https://www.seattletimes.com/seattle-news/climate-lab/seattle-passes-moratorium-on-new-data-centers-amid-national-backlash/). To offset the draw, GM will let EV owners [sell grid power back](https://www.reuters.com/business/energy/gm-offer-ev-owners-ability-sell-power-us-electricity-grid-2026-06-09/) for a cut.

Biology is being rewritten in vivo. Life Biosciences dosed the first patient in a Phase 1 trial of [ER-100](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-01836-7), a partial-reprogramming therapy that resets aged retinal cells toward a more youthful state to restore sight in glaucoma, a milestone David Sinclair called [moving to witness](https://x.com/davidasinclair/status/2064544325954859262) after 25 years chasing age reversal. Palantir’s AI [Sepsis Hub](https://www.thetimes.com/uk/healthcare/article/palantir-software-halves-sepsis-deaths-hospital-tampa-bmbk83pdq) at Tampa General halved sepsis deaths, saving nearly 900 lives. Robots are scaling too, as Figure passes [660 humanoids](https://x.com/thehumanoidhub/status/2064465160044257556), the Swedish robo-truck maker [Einride](https://www.wsj.com/business/autos/autonomous-truck-company-einride-to-begin-trading-on-nasdaq-cde6a2f0) lists on Nasdaq at $1.35B, and Ukraine’s [Logistics Lockdown](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/06/10/world/europe/ukraine-midrange-logistics-strikes.html) rides a fivefold drone surge to choke Russian fuel behind the lines. Higher up, NASA named its [Artemis III crew](https://www.nbcnews.com/science/space/nasa-artemis-iii-astronauts-announced-mission-rcna347223) for lunar-docking rehearsals as SpaceX aims to demo [orbital AI computing](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/spacex-aims-launch-orbital-ai-computing-tests-by-end-next-year-sources-say-2026-06-09/) by late 2027, having applied for a million data-center satellites.

We are racing to manufacture non-human intelligence in silicon, yet [on the Capitol steps](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u_emZmzkTtM) came claims another kind is already here. David Grusch said the government [is aware of](https://x.com/uapwatchers/status/2064414505333842091) “several” non-human intelligences, including bipedal and “sentient plasmoid” life, which Lue Elizondo and Chris Cuomo cast as the [collapse of a historic cover-up](https://x.com/uapjames/status/2064534031710953737). Leslie Kean said Grusch [showed Congress evidence](https://x.com/interstellaruap/status/2064429929107018164) of recovered non-human craft and biologics, and Rep. Eric Burlison relayed that Brazil’s ex-defense minister [confirmed the alleged Varginha incident](https://x.com/the_astral_/status/2064405135858512264).

Back on Earth, once intelligence turns cheap the contest shifts from building it to controlling who gets it. CrowdStrike found [China-nexus actors](https://www.crowdstrike.com/en-us/blog/crowdstrike-2026-technology-threat-landscape-report/) drove 58% of state-backed tech intrusions chasing AI secrets, as the White House reportedly told its [AI testing unit to stop publishing](https://www.wsj.com/politics/policy/white-house-reins-in-ai-testing-unit-as-national-security-concerns-grow-8bd33fbb) its assessments. NHS England gave [500,000 staff Copilot access](https://www.england.nhs.uk/2026/06/500000-nhs-staff-to-get-new-artificial-intelligence-tools-to-help-free-up-more-time-for-patients/) to reclaim 43 minutes a day, the EU is [blaming Apple, not itself, for its missing Siri AI](https://www.macrumors.com/2026/06/09/eu-says-decision-not-to-launch-siri-ai-in-europe-is-apples/), and its drug agency warns [gangs will weaponize AI](https://www.ft.com/content/1b1333ca-55e6-4012-a92a-1797f0f6f2a1). Meta is [folding outside activity](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/06/better-personalization-and-changes-to-controls-for-your-activity-from-other-businesses/) into Feed and AI answers, and SK Hynix is eyeing a [$14B US listing](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/south-koreas-sk-hynix-eyes-us-listing-soon-august-sources-say-2026-06-10/). The labor shift is explicit. [Tata](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-09/asia-s-largest-outsourcer-to-slow-hiring-as-ai-reshapes-industry) is slowing hiring as it expects to one day run as many AI agents as humans, while Walmart insists [AI will improve jobs, not take them](https://www.ft.com/content/d2155669-cead-4716-8d70-91df69b82977), as it certifies workers in OpenAI.

One day more, and at the Singularity’s clock speed, another day is another destiny.
