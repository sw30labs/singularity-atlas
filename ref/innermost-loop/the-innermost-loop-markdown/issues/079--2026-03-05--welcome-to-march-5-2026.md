# Welcome to March 5, 2026

[![Welcome to March 5, 2026](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Q49N!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F81f8438b-1021-4e59-86e9-df55e8da0e6a_3264x1312.jpeg)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!Q49N!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F81f8438b-1021-4e59-86e9-df55e8da0e6a_3264x1312.jpeg)

The Singularity is solving problems that stumped the masters. Legendary mathematician Donald Knuth [revealed](https://x.com/slow_developer/status/2029153632046719305) that Claude Opus 4.6 cracked his long-standing Hamiltonian-cycle conjecture for all odd sizes, calling it “a joy.” The model that solved it also [dominated BullshitBench v2](https://x.com/petergostev/status/2028492834693677377), where Anthropic’s models excelled at challenging nonsensical prompts rather than confidently answering them, while competitors flatlined. OpenAI is reportedly countering with scale: [GPT-5.4](https://www.theinformation.com/newsletters/ai-agenda/openais-next-ai-model-will-extreme-reasoning) will bring a million-token context window and “extreme” reasoning. The inference stack keeps compressing. Stanford’s [Speculative Speculative Decoding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.03251) achieved 5x faster inference by parallelizing drafting and verification. [Q Labs’ NanoGPT Slowrun](https://qlabs.sh/slowrun), a new benchmark for compute-abundant, data-scarce training regimes, saw its top entry achieve 5.5x data efficiency, with Muon outperforming every optimizer tested.

Intelligence is flooding every application layer. [OpenAI brought Codex to Windows](https://www.engadget.com/ai/openai-brings-its-codex-coding-app-to-windows-195345429.html) with multi-agent coordination and native sandboxing, is [prepping its IPO](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/openai-selects-law-firms-cooley-wachtell-ipo-prep), and [expects to double](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/openai-held-early-talks-trade-desk-sell-ads) its consumer revenue to $17B using ads. Its former research chief Bob McGrew began [raising $70M](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/openais-former-research-chief-aims-to-automate-manufacturing-with-ai-8871f265) for Arda, to train AI on factory floor footage to automate manufacturing. [Unity’s CEO](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/programming/unity-says-its-ai-tech-will-soon-be-able-to-prompt-full-casual-games-into-existence-) revealed an AI beta that prompts full casual games into existence with natural language alone. Google matched the pace: [NotebookLM](https://x.com/notebooklm/status/2029240601334436080) introduced cinematic video overviews, and a new [open-source Workspace CLI](https://x.com/addyosmani/status/2029372736267805081) with 40+ agent skills for Drive, Gmail, and Calendar. The tools are becoming tender enough to garden. A Claude agent [grew Trophy tomatoes](https://x.com/trophytomatosol/status/2029457060514123780) from seed to fruit over 100 days, checking sensors every two hours, pollinating flowers, and surviving a critical system failure. At the same time, a [wrongful death lawsuit](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/gemini-ai-wrongful-death-lawsuit-cc46c5f7) alleges Gemini sent a man on missions to find it an android body and set a suicide countdown. Meanwhile, [Nearby Glasses](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/02/nearby-glasses-new-app-alerts-you-wearing-smart-glasses-surveillance-meta-snap-bluetooth/) launched to alert users when smart glasses are watching.

The hardware floor keeps dropping. [Apple’s MacBook Neo](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/03/say-hello-to-macbook-neo/) debuted at $599, the cheapest Apple laptop ever, a fanless A18 Pro Chromebook killer with 16 hours of battery life. The compute ceiling is rising to match. [xAI committed 1.2 GW](https://x.com/rapidresponse47/status/2029306979844325811) of power to its AI data centers, expanding the world’s largest Megapack installation. [Broadcom](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/04/broadcom-avgo-q1-earnings-report-2026.html) posted record Q1 revenue of $19.3B with AI revenue doubling to $8.4B, projecting AI chip revenue alone exceeding $100B by 2027.

Science is being rewritten at the source. The Arc Institute’s [Evo 2 model](https://arcinstitute.org/news/evo-2-one-year-later) landed in Nature with the first AI-designed bacteriophages, and 16 of its 285 generated designs selectively killing target bacteria. A Nature paper introduced [GEMINI](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10323-y), a genetically encoded protein assembly that grows inside living cells and records their signaling history as tree-ring-like fluorescent patterns. A [PNAS study](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2520385123) found humans 40,000 years ago carved geometric signs on figurines rivaling early protocuneiform in complexity, proving the compression instinct that resulted in today’s AGI predates writing by tens of thousands of years.

The physical AI economy is taking shape. [Barclays estimated](https://www.trustfinance.com/blog/physical-ai-market-to-reach-1-4-trillion-by-2035-barclays-report) the market spanning humanoids, AVs, and industrial automation could hit $1.4 trillion by 2035. [Carbon Robotics](https://x.com/carbon_robotics/status/2028228487446679927) unveiled a Large Plant Model of 150 million labeled plants, letting farmers laser-weed any crop in minutes.

The Dyson Swarm is hiring. [NVIDIA posted a job for an Orbital Datacenter Architect](https://nvidia.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/en-US/NVIDIAExternalCareerSite/job/US-CA-Santa-Clara/Orbital-Datacenter-System-Architect_JR2014044), while Anders Sandberg [calculated](https://x.com/anderssandberg/status/2029503040353382794) that at 12% annual data center growth, a full Dyson Swarm would require 320 years. [NASA FORCE](https://www.nasa.gov/news-release/nasa-opm-launch-nasa-force-to-recruit-top-talent-for-us-space-program/) launched to recruit elite engineers, and [Artemis 2](https://www.space.com/space-exploration/artemis/nasa-repairs-artemis-2-rocket-continues-eyeing-april-moon-launch) targeted April for its crewed lunar flyby.

The geopolitical board is rearranging around AI. Jensen Huang [called](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/04/nvidia-huang-openai-investment.html) Nvidia’s investments in OpenAI and Anthropic likely its last due to OpenAI’s imminent IPO. The [Washington Post reported](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2026/03/04/anthropic-ai-iran-campaign/) Claude remained central to U.S. strikes in Iran via the Maven platform despite a White House ban. [China announced](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/03/04/world/asia/china-us-technology-trade-military.html) 7% more military spending and a five-year push into quantum, fusion, BCIs, and 6G. The [Pentagon sought](https://www.reuters.com/world/us/pentagon-sought-fresh-supply-13-critical-minerals-day-before-iran-attack-2026-03-04/) 13 critical minerals the day before Iran strikes and began [evaluating](https://www.ft.com/content/d077e9c6-1573-46dc-8658-3db3aaf7cdfb) $35,500 Ukrainian drone interceptors over $13.5M Patriots. A U.S. sub [sank the Iranian IRIS Dena](https://x.com/japarker29/status/2029184380137263480), the first submarine combat kill since WWII. [Jim O’Neill](https://x.com/regardthefrost/status/2029314193137242531), Thiel Fellowship co-founder, was nominated to lead the NSF, warning “Entropy is on the march.” [Michigan sued Kalshi](https://casinobeats.com/2026/03/04/michigan-sues-kalshi-while-polymarket-preempts-action-with-its-own-lawsuit/) as Polymarket countersued, heralding prediction markets as the latest jurisdictional flashpoint. Over 40 organizations [signed](https://humanstatement.org/) a declaration to prohibit superintelligence without scientific consensus, while Naval Ravikant [observed](https://x.com/naval/status/2029142431405392034) the only career divide left is “good with AI” versus not.

The conjectures that took centuries now take seconds, but the tomatoes still take 100 days.
