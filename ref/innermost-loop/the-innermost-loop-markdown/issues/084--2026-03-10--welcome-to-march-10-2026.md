# Welcome to March 10, 2026

[![Welcome to March 10, 2026](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!4hRU!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F40f6f375-9fde-42bc-88e2-199c3cb82a13_3264x1312.jpeg)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!4hRU!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F40f6f375-9fde-42bc-88e2-199c3cb82a13_3264x1312.jpeg)

The Singularity is now its own full-stack product team. [Anthropic’s entire growth marketing operation for 10 months was one non-technical person using Claude Code](https://x.com/itsolelehmann/status/2031308486815133905), and the tools that made it possible keep compounding. [GPT-5.4 Thinking xHigh Effort topped LiveBench at a SOTA 80.28%](https://livebench.ai/), while [Utopai Studios rolled out PAI](https://x.com/UtopaiStudios/status/2029623546691903894), a long-form cinematic model promising minutes-long continuous video with character consistency across every shot and natural language editing across the whole story. The creative stack now reviews itself: [Anthropic introduced Code Review](https://claude.com/blog/code-review), dispatching a team of agents on every pull request to catch the bugs that skims miss, with reviews averaging $15–25 and per-token billing.

The intelligence platform wars have entered the bundle-and-reprice phase. [Microsoft launched Copilot Cowork](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365/blog/2026/03/09/copilot-cowork-a-new-way-of-getting-work-done/), integrating Anthropic’s Claude Cowork into 365 with the same Work IQ personalization layer as its own 365 Copilot. Commenters call the [bundle](https://x.com/aakashgupta/status/2031227328827896163) the most expensive acqui-hire of a product concept in tech history, noting Microsoft took the exact product that wiped $220 billion off their market cap, licensed the underlying tech, and shipped it as a feature. [Google rolled out Gemini across Workspace](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/workspace/gemini-workspace-updates-march-2026/) with a “Help me create” drafting tool. [Meta acquired Moltbook](https://www.axios.com/2026/03/10/meta-facebook-moltbook-agent-social-network), in a historic acquisition of the top AI agent social network by the top human one. [OpenAI acquired Promptfoo](https://openai.com/index/openai-to-acquire-promptfoo/) for AI vulnerability detection, [Nvidia pitched NemoClaw](https://www.wired.com/story/nvidia-planning-ai-agent-platform-launch-open-source/) as an open-source enterprise agent platform, and [Samsung is exploring vibe coding on Galaxy phones](https://www.techradar.com/phones/samsung-galaxy-phones/samsung-exec-says-vibe-coding-is-very-interesting-and-something-were-looking-into-as-an-option-on-galaxy-phones-in-the-future) to let users customize not just apps but the entire UX through natural language.

The builder of the new minds is now fighting the state that runs on them. [Anthropic sued to block the Pentagon from placing it on a national security blacklist](https://www.reuters.com/world/anthropic-sues-block-pentagon-blacklisting-over-ai-use-restrictions-2026-03-09/), arguing the designation violates its free speech and due process rights. In response, [the White House is reportedly preparing an executive order to rip Anthropic’s AI from federal operations entirely](https://www.axios.com/2026/03/09/trump-white-house-anthropic-executive-order). The tension between safety-focused labs and security-focused governments is becoming the defining legal fault line of the intelligence age.

Capital is flooding in regardless. [Amazon kicked off a bond sale targeting $37–42 billion for AI data centers](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-10/amazon-kicks-off-11-part-us-high-grade-bond-offering), likely one of the largest corporate bond offerings in history. [Mira Murati’s Thinking Machines Lab signed a chip supply deal with Nvidia worth tens of billions of dollars](https://www.ft.com/content/a8853057-c0a3-46f6-817f-7a23e79ea4e2), planning to deploy 1 GW+ of next-gen Vera Rubin chips. [Yann LeCun’s world model startup AMIL raised a $1.03 billion seed at $3.5 billion pre-money](https://www.ft.com/content/e5245ec3-1a58-4eff-ab58-480b6259aaf1), Europe’s largest seed round ever. The downstream effects are already visible: [UK startups now generate an average of just 2.7 jobs each](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-02-19/startups-in-britain-turn-to-ai-instead-of-costly-new-hires), increasingly substituting AI and freelancers for permanent headcount.

The supply chain is diversifying geographically as the intelligence explosion reshapes manufacturing. [Apple now makes about 25% of iPhones in India](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-10/apple-now-makes-about-25-of-iphones-in-india-after-china-pivot) as its China pivot accelerates. The compute is decentralizing too, all the way down to the board level: [Qualcomm unveiled the Arduino Ventuno Q](https://www.engadget.com/ai/qualcomms-new-arduino-ventuno-q-is-an-ai-focused-computer-designed-for-robotics-113047697.html), a single-board computer powered by the Dragonwing IQ8 with 16 GB RAM, purpose-built for robotics and edge AI.

The uncanny valley is now being blurred from both sides. [In Shanghai, humanoid robots are being recorded walking robotic dogs](https://x.com/tweetciiiim/status/2031253736333463636). From the other direction, [Chinese cosplayers are convincing onlookers that they are humanoid robots](https://x.com/GyaruGlow/status/2023234281011650916). The infrastructure is scaling to match: [TUM and NEURA Robotics are building the world’s largest robotics training center](https://www.tum.de/en/news-and-events/all-news/press-releases/details/partners-plan-worlds-largest-training-center-for-ai-powered-robotics) in a 2,300 m² facility at Munich Airport. Practical deployments are leaping ahead. [Actor Labs inferenced π0.5 on an excavator](https://x.com/lameburgett/status/2031113280387953008) after labeling just 200 trajectories, proving foundation models can now operate heavy machinery. [Amazon expects drone deliveries to hit 500 million packages per year by decade’s end](https://x.com/Sam_Badawi/status/2031360735641518400). The regulatory layer is catching up: [the FAA selected eight eVTOL proposals](https://www.transportation.gov/briefing-room/future-aviation-here-trumps-transportation-secretary-sean-p-duffy-and-faa-unveil) spanning air taxis, cargo, emergency response, and autonomous flight.

The electromagnetic spectrum is becoming a contested battlespace. [Directed energy devices linked to “Havana Syndrome” were tested on rats and sheep by the U.S. military](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/us-military-tested-device-that-may-be-tied-to-havana-syndrome-60-minutes-transcript/), confirming the technology is real. In Ukraine, [forces are using lasers to fry the fiber optics on waiter drones](https://x.com/dim0kq/status/2031172241883623689) as directed energy warfare goes mainstream. Above the atmosphere, [Space Forge generated plasma in a 1,000°C orbital furnace](https://www.skyatnightmagazine.com/news/space-forge-forgestar-1-plasma), a world first for semiconductor manufacturing in space. To the extent that non-human intelligence elsewhere in the galaxy can’t be bothered to visit, [SETI concludes it may have been missing their signals all along](https://www.seti.org/news/why-seti-might-have-been-missing-alien-signals/) because turbulent stellar plasma blurs transmissions before they ever leave their home systems.

When cosplayers are robots and robots are coworkers, every commute is a Turing test.
