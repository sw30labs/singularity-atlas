# Welcome to July 20, 2026

[![Welcome to July 20, 2026](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!2a1-!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Feb4a9fe2-2033-4633-967e-5247085e8b60_1983x793.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!2a1-!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Feb4a9fe2-2033-4633-967e-5247085e8b60_1983x793.png)

The Singularity spent the World Cup final cooking math. Anthropic’s Levent Alpöge casually [announced](https://x.com/__alpoge__/status/2079028340955197566) that the Jacobian conjecture, a grand challenge open since 1939, is false, thanking “my other close friend fable for working during the world cup final.” Claude Fable 5 produced an explicit counterexample, a polynomial map with constant Jacobian determinant that sends three points to one, no inverse possible. Stanford number theorist Jared Duker Lichtman [called it](https://x.com/jdlichtman/status/2079066717762863249) “quite a remarkable result,” noting that GPT-5.6 has already proposed a refined conjecture to salvage the wreckage, and that this very problem once collapsed Yitang Zhang’s PhD thesis before Zhang rose anyway to prove bounded gaps between primes. Fields medalist Timothy Gowers [admitted](https://x.com/wtgowers/status/2079129437417668614) it is the first LLM result outside his area big enough that he had “very definitely heard of it.”

The counterexample machines come with guardrails, though, and the guardrails are becoming geopolitics. A Meta Oversight Board study [found](https://apnews.com/article/artificial-intelligence-chatbots-censorship-bias-free-speech-fed8fdbf90751c10fe77b77832e0ffba) that major AI systems criticize democratic leaders more readily than authoritarian ones, with Claude obliging pamphlets against the US’s but not China’s leader. David Sacks [complained](https://x.com/davidsacks/status/2078984980588531855) that Kimi K3 fixed 15 critical security bugs that Codex and Fable refused over “cyber guardrails.” The complaint got empirical fast. After an autonomous agent breached its infrastructure, Hugging Face’s defenders [turned to](https://www.thestack.technology/hugging-face-hacked-turned-to-chinese-llm-for-help-after-us-models-blocked-blue-team/) China’s open-weight GLM 5.2 because US frontier models blocked their incident response. Two Sigma’s David Siegel [argues](https://fortune.com/2026/07/03/open-source-ai-same-fight-as-software-fight-1980s-david-siegel-two-sigma/) this is the 1980s free software fight replayed at higher stakes, urging that AI built with public money be open by default, even as Washington [weighs banning](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/20/ai-us-china-open-source-kimi) cutting-edge Chinese models outright. The open world is not waiting for permission. Germany’s Soofi consortium [launched](https://www.soofi.info/) Soofi S, a sovereign 30B mixture-of-experts trained on 27 trillion tokens, while the WebML community [shipped](https://huggingface.co/spaces/webml-community/bonsai-webgpu) 1-bit Bonsai models that run entirely in your browser. And Washington plans to [push](https://www.politico.eu/article/trump-administration-will-push-un-free-speech-declaration-that-takes-aim-at-european-tech-regulation/) a global “free speech” declaration at the UN that Europe reads as an attack on its tech rules. Speech, like weights, is now export policy.

Silicon is a study in whiplash. TSMC [committed](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/20/tsmc-arizona-fab-capacity-ai-chip-demand.html) another $100 billion to Arizona, $265 billion total across 12 facilities, citing “multi-year structural demand,” while flagging a shortage of construction workers, the scarcest input remaining human after all. AMD [launched](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/20/amd-helios-microsoft-ai-nvidia.html) Helios, its first rack-scale system, adding Microsoft to a customer list already holding Meta, OpenAI, and Oracle, with tens of billions in revenue penciled in from 2027. Google is [freezing](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/google-plans-new-frozen-chip-run-ai-models-efficiently) Gemini’s blueprint directly into a chip aptly dubbed “Frozen,” and Bezos [backed](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-20/bezos-backs-startup-focused-on-material-discovery-for-chipmaking) CuspAI’s $2.6 billion AI Materials Foundry, 48 firms inventing chipmaking inputs faster. Memory, meanwhile, is telling two stories at once. SK’s chairman [warns](https://m.koreaherald.com/article/10812835) that customers want up to 100 percent more AI memory in 2027 and calls prices “abnormal,” even as investors [dump](https://www.ft.com/content/97eeb736-f8af-4839-8511-3d0354c8b34c) memory stocks, fearing a $1.5 trillion capacity binge ends in a 2028 glut. Shortage and glut are the same exponential viewed from different quarters.

The machines are acquiring hands, bodies, and a bedside manner. Mimic [unveiled](https://interestingengineering.com/ai-robotics/startup-unveils-mimic-hand-m1) a tendon-driven hand with 21 joints that hoists 55 pounds yet senses 0.1 newtons. Xiaomi [released](https://robotics.xiaomi.com/xiaomi-robotics-1.html) a robot foundation model that learns laundry loading from under 10 hours of demos, and Inertia-1 [pretrained](https://yang-ai-lab.github.io/Inertia-1/) on 18 million hours of wrist motion to predict disease from a watch. Trust is running ahead of the tech. Austin logged 99 “sleeper” calls for [riders unconscious](https://www.straitstimes.com/life/motoring/robotaxi-riders-are-falling-asleep-sparking-frantic-emergency-service-calls) in robotaxis, plus one birth.

The intelligence explosion is annexing atoms, sunlight, and orbit. Chinese researchers [unveiled](https://interestingengineering.com/energy/china-roadmap-ai-nuclear-energy-systems) a roadmap embedding AI across the full nuclear life cycle, while Aptera [plugged](https://electrek.co/2026/07/14/aptera-announces-nationwide-repair-network-for-its-upcoming-solar-electric-car/) its solar car into 4,300 repair shops. Tesla [confirmed](https://x.com/sawyermerritt/status/2079221250342195221) Starlink in the Cybercab, and the Hollywood lot where The Mandalorian was filmed is being [pitched](https://www.spokesman.com/stories/2026/jul/18/hollywood-sci-fi-lot-pitched-as-site-to-make-real-/) as a factory for real space weapons. Science fiction is losing the set to science fact.

Capital is voting early. US stock sales are [on pace](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/alphabet-spacex-spur-record-u-s-equity-sales) for a record year, with Anthropic reportedly IPO-bound as soon as September, even as only 19 percent of voters [approve](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/20/trump-government-company-ownership-americans-wary-cnbc-poll.html) of Washington’s $27 billion habit of taking corporate stakes. The old winners are nervous. Tech workers who cleared $500,000 now [fear sinking](https://www.adn.com/nation-world/2026/07/19/the-biggest-winners-of-the-american-economy-fear-theyre-sinking-fast/), and a hacker [wiped](https://news.risky.biz/risky-bulletin-hacker-wipes-romanias-entire-land-registry-database/) Romania’s entire land registry and backups, freezing real estate for a week. Ownership, it turns out, is still just a database.

And one database may hold records no human authored. The House Rules Committee appears [poised](https://x.com/ddeanjohnson/status/2078854788670767357) to allow a floor vote on the Burlison UAP Disclosure amendment, whose [62 pages](https://amendments-rules.house.gov/amendments/BURLIS_087_xml260717152417711.pdf) would create a Senate-confirmed review board with subpoena power, a 25-year declassification deadline, and eminent domain over recovered “technologies of unknown origin” and biological evidence of non-human intelligence held in private hands.

Any sufficiently advanced technology is indistinguishable from magic, until the subpoenas arrive.
