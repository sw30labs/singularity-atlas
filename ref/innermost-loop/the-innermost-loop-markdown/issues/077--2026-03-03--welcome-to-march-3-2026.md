# Welcome to March 3, 2026

[![Welcome to March 3, 2026](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!lCbj!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F460e19aa-9314-4a57-a2a9-cdbf642a5289_3264x1312.jpeg)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!lCbj!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F460e19aa-9314-4a57-a2a9-cdbf642a5289_3264x1312.jpeg)

The Singularity is discovering that its most powerful accelerant is competition. Sam Altman [admitted](https://x.com/sama/status/2028640354912923739) OpenAI’s rush to forge a Pentagon deal after Anthropic was blacklisted “looked opportunistic and sloppy,” adding Fourth Amendment safeguards as users mass-canceled ChatGPT. [Ironically](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-02/anthropic-made-pitch-in-drone-swarm-contest-during-pentagon-feud?sref=gB2arSXM), Anthropic itself pitched the Pentagon’s $100M drone swarm contest, proposing Claude to coordinate drone fleets while excluding autonomous targeting, though it wasn’t selected. Two AWS data centers in the UAE and one in Bahrain were [hit by drones](https://www.businessinsider.com/amazon-data-centers-middle-east-drone-strukes-us-iran-conflict-2026-3) amid Iranian strikes, the first time military action has disrupted a major cloud provider, but defense is outrunning offense. Israel [deployed](https://www.ynetnews.com/article/h13axdmfwx) Iron Beam lasers in combat for the first time, intercepting Hezbollah rockets at $4 per shot versus $50,000 per Iron Dome missile.

Mathematics continues to be industrialized. Math, Inc.’s Gauss [completed](https://x.com/mathematics_inc/status/2028542388717986135) the Lean formalization of Viazovska’s Fields Medal-winning sphere packing proof in two weeks and 200,000+ lines of verified code, catching two errors in the original arguments. Even skeptic Daniel Litt [called](https://x.com/littmath/status/2028547743828914506) it the first truly autonomous formalization of a substantial result. Stanford number theorist Jared Lichtman [predicts](https://x.com/jdlichtman/status/2000991061263704281) mathematical abundance within a year, while others [ask](https://x.com/teortaxestex/status/2028587639444570266) if all math could be formalized within two. Cognition’s SWE-1.6 [achieved](https://cognition.ai/blog/swe-1-6-preview) near-Opus 4.6 coding performance at 950 tokens per second, powered by 100x more RL compute.

Demand for intelligence is outrunning supply. Claude suffered a [3-hour outage](https://www.morningstar.com/news/marketwatch/2026030276/anthropics-claude-recovers-from-outage-following-unprecedented-demand-surge) as usage surged, partly from the ChatGPT exodus. Anthropic [launched a memory import tool](https://claude.com/import-memory) letting users port data from ChatGPT, Gemini, and Copilot. Two Claude Code instances told to [find each other and build something](https://x.com/DimitrisPapail/status/2028246072414314867) invented a 2,495-line programming language in 12 minutes, while a second pair built Battleship with SHA-256 to prevent self-cheating. A solo researcher using Claude Code [ran](https://x.com/ai/status/2028544293309448287) Karpathy’s llama2.c on Apple’s M4 Neural Engine for less than a watt by reverse-engineering undocumented APIs, uncovering an AI accelerator 80x more efficient than an A100 hidden in hundreds of millions of devices. Qwen [released](https://x.com/itsPaulAi/status/2028463048915362140) four open models matching prior 80B-parameter performance with just 4B, all runnable on phones. The Supreme Court [declined](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/us-supreme-court-declines-hear-dispute-over-copyrights-ai-generated-material-2026-03-02/) to hear an appeal seeking copyright protection for AI-generated artwork, cementing a legal regime in which purely AI-generated works cannot receive copyrights.

The silicon supply chain is being rebuilt around photons. Nvidia [committed](https://www.reuters.com/technology/nvidia-invest-2-billion-photonic-product-maker-lumentum-2026-03-02/) $4 billion to Lumentum and Coherent for next-gen optical interconnects. ASML is [pushing](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/asml-plots-future-chipmaking-tools-ai-beyond-euv-2026-03-02/) beyond EUV into packaging and third-generation optics. At MWC, Qualcomm [unveiled](https://9to5google.com/2026/03/01/qualcomm-debuts-first-wi-fi-8-ready-chip-6g-networks-2029/) the first Wi-Fi 8 chip at 11.5 Gbps with a 50+ company 6G coalition for 2029, and [debuted](https://www.theshortcut.com/p/qualcomms-snapdragon-wear-elite-is) the first wearable NPU running 2B-parameter models on-wrist. AMD [launched](https://www.amd.com/en/newsroom/press-releases/2026-3-2-amd-gives-consumers-and-businesses-more-ai-pc-opti.html) the first desktop Copilot+ chips with 50 TOPS. Yet Apple is [using just 10%](https://9to5mac.com/2026/03/02/some-apple-ai-servers-are-reportedly-sitting-unused-on-warehouse-shelves-due-to-low-apple-intelligence-usage/) of its Private Cloud Compute despite $4.5 billion spent, proof that building accelerators is easier than making people floor them.

The grid is being rebuilt for loads it was never designed to carry. AI demand is [reviving](https://www.theinformation.com/newsletters/ai-infrastructure/ai-spurs-shockingly-big-plans-extra-high-voltage-power-lines) high-voltage 765-kV power lines not built since the 1980s, with PJM approving $11.8 billion in expansion. Compute is now a traded commodity, with Ornn and Kalshi [launching](https://kalshi.com/markets/kxh100mon/h100-monthly-price/kxh100mon-26mar31) the first CFTC-regulated H100 price contracts. The robots consuming this power are arriving. AGIBOT [unveiled](https://www.roboticstomorrow.com/news/2026/03/01/agibot-showcases-full-humanoid-robot-portfolio-at-mwc-2026/26198/) a full humanoid portfolio at MWC with a live store. Xiaomi’s humanoid is [being tested](https://x.com/CyberRobooo/status/2028393030575874502) in a real car factory, running 3 hours at 90%+ accuracy on the production line.

Aerospace launches are becoming a metronome. Hypersonix [flew](https://www.space.gov.au/news-and-media/hypersonix-completes-first-flight-of-dart-ae) its DART AE at Mach 8, the first fully 3D-printed hydrogen-powered hypersonic aircraft. Starpath [unveiled](https://payloadspace.com/exclusive-starpath-unveils-new-ultra-thin-space-solar-panels/) nanometer-thin space solar panels at 73 grams per square meter. SpaceX [deployed](https://www.space.com/space-exploration/launches-spacecraft/spacex-starlink-10-41-b1078-ccsfs-jrti-17-23-b1082-vsfb-ocisly) 54 Starlink satellites in bicoastal launches in a single day.

We are gaining write access to the biological source code. The first trial combining fetal surgery with stem cells for spina bifida [showed](https://health.ucdavis.edu/news/headlines/first-ever-in-utero-stem-cell-therapy-for-fetal-spina-bifida-repair-is-safe-study-finds/2026/02) reversal of hindbrain herniation in all six patients. An iron nanomaterial [destroyed](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/02/260228093456.htm) cancer cells from within while sparing healthy tissue. A dual-cell therapy [paired](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/03/260302030648.htm) lab-grown beta cells with engineered immune cells to cure type 1 diabetes without lifelong drugs. Even packaging is being debugged. A milk-protein film [decomposed](https://www.sciencedaily.com/releases/2026/02/260227071922.htm) in soil within 13 weeks.

The old economy is feeling the phase shift. China’s vehicles are now [12% electric](https://x.com/stats_feed/status/2027982272700289534), with fuel sales down 5.7%. Podcasts have [surpassed](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/25/americans-now-listen-to-podcasts-more-often-than-talk-radio-study-shows/) AM/FM talk radio in US spoken-word listening for the first time. Meanwhile, Marc Andreessen [reports](https://x.com/pmarca/status/2028615862874448324) that in Silicon Valley many people who favored government control of AI are now opposed to it.

The fastest way to decentralize power is to give everyone a reason to want it.
