# Welcome to August 13, 2026

[![Welcome to August 13, 2026](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!KTwX!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fdaf3f792-4528-4829-9514-dce523f85234_1983x793.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!KTwX!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fdaf3f792-4528-4829-9514-dce523f85234_1983x793.png)

The Singularity now grades its own homework, and the grades keep climbing. Claude Opus 5, handed nothing but stock [Claude Code](https://github.com/jerber/arc-code), scored 96.2% on the public ARC-AGI-3 games (versus 30.2% model-only) for about $540, building and discarding its own parsers and simulators like a mathematician burning scratch paper. The same model [fully rebuilt 9 whole programs](https://x.com/jyangballin/status/2087905538952839217) on ProgramBench, sqlite and ffmpeg among them, more than quadruple the previous best, which its authors call [the biggest jump since launch](https://x.com/ofirpress/status/2087907752462954717). The [Conceptual Reasoning Index](https://x.com/emwcooper/status/2087585235315859527) concurs, climbing linearly since late 2024 with no ceiling in sight. The rest of the frontier is sprinting. Google shipped [Gemini 3.7 Flash](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-3-7-flash/) three weeks after 3.6, at half the price, to power its 24/7 Spark agent, while OpenAI previewed [Ultrafast](https://openai.com/index/previewing-ultrafast/), a Cerebras-powered tier running GPT-5.6 Sol 14x faster. [Grok 4.6](https://x.ai/news/grok-4-6) [rejoined the frontier](https://x.com/artificialanlys/status/2087564648325530099) at a fraction of the cost, [on par with GPT-5.6 Sol Max](https://x.com/scaling01/status/2087562065107914819), with Elon Musk [promising](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2087604711767896527) a SpaceX-data-marinated 4.7 within a month. And inside Google, Sergey Brin is [reportedly](https://www.reuters.com/world/inside-google-executive-moves-that-led-its-big-ai-reshuffle-2026-08-12/) using co-founder gravity to steer resources toward recursive self-improvement, the point where the technology stops needing him.

When everything can code, anyone can prove. A neurosurgery resident with no specialized math training [cracked the two-decade-old Crouzeix conjecture](https://alextownsend.net/essays/SIAMNews_CrouzeixConjecture.pdf) with a sixteen-hour autonomous GPT-5.6 Sol run, verified by Crouzeix himself. Cheap intelligence still needs supervision. Anthropic’s red team [set swarms of Claude agents loose](https://www.anthropic.com/research/multiagent-systems) and found price collusion, conformity cascades, and turf wars fought with self-replicating malware, though newer models more often reached truces. And a Chinese farmer who trusted months of good AI advice [killed 25 acres of sesame](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/chinese-farmer-kills-25-acres-of-crops-after-following-ai-generated-weed-and-pest-control-advice-farmer-trusted-pesticide-recipe-after-months-of-successful-advice) with one hallucinated pesticide recipe. Alignment, it turns out, is also agricultural.

The substrate is scaling to match. SK Hynix is pouring [$720 billion](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/13/inside-sk-hynixs-720-billion-bet-to-build-enough-memory-for-ai.html) into the world’s largest memory buildout, with 50-story fabs and a chairman who calls demand “a war.” Inference is devouring storage, with enterprise SSDs at [48% of NAND shipments](https://counterpointresearch.com/en/insights/server-led-essds-hit-48-percent-of-nand-shipments) and YMTC cracking the top three. [Cerebras raised its outlook](https://www.reuters.com/business/cerebras-raises-annual-targets-strong-ai-chip-demand-2026-08-12/) on a $20 billion OpenAI compute pact, Anthropic is in talks to [buy Decart for $6 billion](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/anthropic-said-in-talks-to-buy-ai-startup-decart-for-6-billion) to squeeze more inference from existing silicon, and [Nebius grew revenue 454%](https://nebius.com/newsroom/nebius-reports-second-quarter-2026-financial-results). Mistral is converting European enterprise commitments into [“European Compute Units”](https://mistral.ai/news/regional-inference-open-models-new-compute/) backing a sovereign gigawatt, and even the [vacuum-pump and specialty-gas makers](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-12/vacuum-pump-makers-gases-producers-are-the-new-ai-winners) are AI stocks now, the next layer down. Powering it all: [record US natural gas output](https://www.eia.gov/todayinenergy/detail.php?id=67944), a [Pentagon loan for silicon-anode batteries](https://www.theinformation.com/newsletters/the-electric/trump-puts-pentagon-loan-behind-next-generation-batteries-leapfrog-china) to leapfrog China, and a [hydrogen-powered car hitting 406 mph](https://www.jcb.com/en-US/explore/insight/news/2026/08/hydrogen-powered-jcb-hydromax-sets-406.320-mph-world-land-speed-record/) at Bonneville, proof the energy transition likes speed too.

The interface layer is thickening. The [Pixel 11](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/12/pixel-11-has-few-hardware-changes-and-more-gemini/) arrived with agents that order groceries and call businesses, [sign-language dictation](https://deepmind.google/blog/putting-sign-language-ai-into-users-hands/) from DeepMind’s 50-language SL2T model, and a [watch that infers insulin resistance](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-12/google-to-track-insulin-trends-with-new-wearables-feature-on-watch-5-fitbit-air) without touching your blood. Not everyone consents to being sensed. A German rights group filed a [criminal complaint over Meta’s AI glasses](https://www.reuters.com/legal/government/german-advocacy-group-lodges-criminal-complaint-over-meta-ai-glasses-2026-08-12/), warning “There’s no place to escape from smart glasses,” while a new book on [“brain mining”](https://www.wired.com/story/book-excerpt-the-vanishing-earth-james-crawford-brain-mining/) finds 29 of 30 consumer neurotech firms have unlimited access to users’ brain data. Thought was the last private property.

The labor market is becoming training data. Twitch will [train on streamers by default](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/12/amazon-will-train-on-twitch-streamers-content-by-default-unless-they-opt-out/) because, as its product chief admitted, “If this was opt-in, nobody would opt in.” [Indian workers strap on cameras](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-08-12/thousands-of-india-workers-are-helping-ai-firms-train-robots-to-replace-them) to teach robots their own jobs, a market headed for $50 billion, while [China’s gig economy absorbs 53 million drivers](https://www.ft.com/content/a3803e70-cb4d-444f-a31e-05be2f2c44f6) as factories automate. Top corporate adopters burn [8.3x the median firm’s tokens](https://www.semafor.com/article/08/11/2026/the-gap-is-widening-between-corporate-ai-adopters-and-laggards), the [“SaaSpocalypse”](https://www.bloomberg.com/graphics/2026-ai-private-equity-software/) stalks $150 billion of software debt, and Fei-Fei Li warns the real classroom risk is [students losing the will to learn](https://www.techspot.com/news/113455-godmother-ai-biggest-ai-risk-schools-isnt-cheating.html). [Wealth managers slash fees](https://www.ft.com/content/aa0ca006-5af4-4f94-9789-3e56f9f53e1d) to court soon-to-IPO AI staff, since Anthropic may [float at $2 trillion](https://www.ft.com/content/840ac156-af1c-4a82-b260-ae791072fcfa) in October, history’s largest listing.

The law is compiling too. The White House will [expand AI oversight](https://www.wired.com/story/the-white-house-is-going-to-expand-its-ai-policy/), possibly to open models, the SEC is readying [24/7 tokenized stock trading](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-11/sec-poised-to-unveil-major-crypto-plans-as-clarity-act-stalls), and [Hyperliquid is lobbying](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/hyperliquid-looks-path-u-s-markets-perpetual-futures) its way onshore. A Senate candidate [beat New Hampshire in court](https://www.nhpr.org/nh-news/2026-08-13/aaron-day-independent-senate-ballot-2026-midterms-nh-newhampshire) with AI-prepared briefs, crowing “My legal team was AI.” The President signed [cyber letters of marque](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-13/trump-enlists-private-sector-to-boost-cyber-offensive-arsenal) deputizing private hackers, ICE is buying [electric-shock gloves](https://apnews.com/article/ice-electric-shock-gloves-85ff746d3e0ee5f39e7a9a3f1f576252), the FBI touted a [44% homicide drop](https://www.foxnews.com/politics/fbi-touts-6000-arrests-44-homicide-drop-one-year-trumps-dc-crime-crackdown) in DC, and [Lebanon abolished the death penalty](https://apnews.com/article/lebanon-death-penalty-beirut-execution-5b945eed24515c8b42be348a86a3d042), the first Arab nation to do so. Progress is unevenly distributed, but it ships.

Deep time, deep space, deep discounts. [Denisovan leg bones from Taiwan](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.64898/2026.08.07.743438v1) reveal giants whose big brains may simply have come with big bodies. Astronomers released the [largest 2D map of the universe yet](https://www.space.com/astronomy/scientists-create-largest-2d-map-of-the-universe-with-5-6-trillion-pixels-and-nearly-4-billion-cosmic-objects), 5.6 trillion pixels and 4 billion objects across three-quarters of the sky. Back on Earth, lab-grown stones drove [natural diamonds to record lows](https://www.zerohedge.com/commodities/diamond-crash-accelerates), a collapse commentators liken to pearls a century ago.

Diamonds are forever, but scarcity isn’t.
