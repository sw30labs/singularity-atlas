# Welcome to June 24, 2026

[![Welcome to June 24, 2026](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!4yfi!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F8c65da6c-d5f2-445a-8458-30261863717e_1983x793.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!4yfi!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F8c65da6c-d5f2-445a-8458-30261863717e_1983x793.png)

The Singularity has started bulk-solving software bugs. OpenAI expanded its [Daybreak program](https://openai.com/index/daybreak-securing-the-world/) with a new Codex Security plugin and the full GPT-5.5-Cyber, which hit a SOTA 85.6% on CyberGym, edging out Mythos 5, and shifted the mission from finding vulnerabilities to autonomously patching them through a “Patch the Planet” open-source effort with Trail of Bits. Codex’s [Thibault Sottiaux](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2069152290326630518) hailed “a day of celebration for cyber defense acceleration,” and Sam Altman [pledged](https://x.com/sama/status/2069121360744550796) to help companies “solve security problems instead of just finding them.”

The frontier’s lead is thinning from below. Skeptical of benchmarks, [Cline pitted GLM-5.2 against Opus 4.8](https://x.com/cline/status/2069171146994729078) on a real repo bug and found GLM half the cost and tidier, while Opus finished faster but left build-breaking type errors. And it’s not just code, as ByteDance unveiled [Seedance 2.5](https://www.theinformation.com/briefings/bytedance-unveils-seedance-2-5-video-model), stretching video to 30-second clips with up to 50 reference inputs. Meanwhile [Grok Build](https://x.com/grok/status/2069095154691272933) added a “/goal” command for autonomous subagents, [Mistral’s OCR 4](https://mistral.ai/news/ocr-4/) reads 170 languages from one container, and [Qwen-AgentWorld](https://qwen.ai/blog?id=qwen-agentworld) shipped as the first “native language world model” simulating agents across seven domains. The real scarcity, though, is talent, as [Alphabet had its worst day in over a year](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/22/alphabet-goog-stock-ai-departures.html), losing $250 billion after Noam Shazeer and Nobel laureate John Jumper defected to OpenAI and Anthropic, while [the White House is pressing Meta](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/06/23/business/meta-ai-government-reviews-security.html), the last holdout, to submit models for federal safety review.

Superintelligence is dissolving into the workplace. Anthropic’s new [Claude Tag](https://www.anthropic.com/news/introducing-claude-tag) lets teams delegate by tagging Claude in Slack, where it works asynchronously on Opus 4.8, which [Andrej Karpathy](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2069547676849557725) called the third redesign of LLM UX, a “persistent, asynchronous entity” that joins the team like a colleague. Skills now self-author too, via Nous Research’s [“/learn”](https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/docs/user-guide/features/skills#learning-a-skill-from-sources-learn) command that turns any doc or workflow into a reusable skill, and Madison Avenue is next, as [OpenAI debuted at Cannes Lions](https://www.ft.com/content/9717a042-fd09-4d08-972d-29b68f7985a4), pitching ChatGPT ads and Codex to marketers while racing Anthropic, now the most valuable private lab, toward a trillion-dollar IPO.

Underneath, the physical layer is straining. [Nvidia’s banned chips more than doubled in price on China’s black market](https://www.ft.com/content/57fcd3ce-464f-4dc2-8ea2-5712d4972c69), with DGX B300 servers topping $1.1 million, while [Cerebras posted 92% growth](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/23/cerebras-cbrs-q1-earnings-report-2026.html) in its first results since IPO. From here, progress is poured into concrete and copper, as [SpaceX inked a $6.3 billion deal](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/22/spacex-ai-colossus-data-center-reflection.html) to rent GB300s to startup Reflection, [Nvidia’s Rubin racks went fully liquid-cooled](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/liquid-cooling-ai-factories/?ncid=so-twit-996123-vt21) at 45°C, and one analysis argues [the real bottleneck is grid hookups](https://worksinprogress.co/issue/why-american-data-centers-cant-plug-in/), not electricity. Horsepower shifted east as [China’s LineShine became the world’s most powerful supercomputer](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/06/23/technology/china-supercomputer-crown-us.html) at 2.198 exaflops on CPUs alone. The next substrate is already funded, with the President signing [two quantum executive orders](https://www.wsj.com/politics/policy/trump-seeks-to-boost-quantum-computing-with-new-executive-orders-b2200634) targeting a useful machine by 2028, and [Chicago betting $500 million](https://www.wsj.com/tech/chicago-missed-the-tech-boom-quantum-computing-gives-it-a-second-chance-0b34d116) on a quantum park. Powering it all, [Canada declared a “civilian nuclear renaissance”](https://www.cbc.ca/news/politics/federal-nuclear-strategy-9.7244509) of up to 10 reactors, mirrored by a US [$17.5 billion loan](https://www.energy.gov/articles/department-energy-announces-american-nuclear-supply-chain-loans) for ten AP1000s.

The machines are moving in, sometimes ahead of the workers. [GM installed 50 robots](https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/06/gm-installs-robots-at-flagship-ev-factory-after-laying-off-1300-workers/) at its flagship Detroit plant while 1,300 laid-off staff still awaited recall, though robotics showed a friendlier face as a [ProRL humanoid](https://x.com/_ProRL/status/2069032563151094242) played soccer with kids in Boston. AI also climbed onto your face, as Meta and EssilorLuxottica launched [Meta Glasses](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/06/meta-essilorluxottica-partner-launch-meta-glasses/), [26 styles from $299](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/23/meta-glasses-are-new-smart-glasses-starting-at-299.html) with Muse Spark built in, [ditching the Ray-Ban name](https://www.wired.com/story/meta-new-smart-glasses-are-cheaper-colorful-and-meta-branded/) for cheaper, brighter frames.

Autonomy is going orbital and martial. [NASA’s Roman Space Telescope](https://spaceflightnow.com/2026/06/22/nasas-nancy-grace-roman-space-telescope-arrives-in-florida/) reached Kennedy for an August launch, [Musk trademarked “STARMIND”](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2069563583382630894) for SpaceX’s Dyson Swarm, and the Secretary of War declared the first [Golden Dome test](https://x.com/secwar/status/2069596091234857455) a “full mission success,” with directed energy autonomously downing drones and cruise missiles. Meanwhile, Rep. Eric Burlison says [FFRDCs may be hiding UAP records](https://x.com/repericburlison/status/2069166978367889867) outside FOIA, and an F-15 pilot [downed over Iran in April](https://www.cnn.com/2026/06/23/politics/iran-drones-f-15-pilot-intelligence) reportedly described a jellyfish-like swarm of objects moving as one before ejecting.

Biology is compiling faster. [Nabla Bio’s JAM-2](https://x.com/nablabio/status/2069405121084281260) pushed zero-shot drug design beyond binding into multifunctional antibodies, including KRAS-targeting multispecifics built and tested in roughly six weeks, [Void-X](https://www.pnas.org/doi/10.1073/pnas.2607035123) predicted atomic protein packing from sequence alone, and Eli Lilly is building [an “App Store” for scientists](https://www.ft.com/content/d524396a-4986-46e9-9a93-a982e330d157) on its own Blackwell cluster.

Work, trust, and money are all being rewritten. [Virtual staging](https://www.theverge.com/report/953888/ai-virtual-staging-real-estate-apartment-listings) now lets brokers cram impossible furniture into tiny rooms, [Oracle cut 21,000 jobs](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-22/oracle-layoffs-fueled-by-ai-reduces-workforce-by-21-000) blaming AI, Google launched a [Xoogler incubator](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-23/google-taps-employee-alumni-network-with-ai-startup-incubator) for alumni founders, and Meta [paused a keystroke-harvesting program](https://www.wired.com/story/meta-accidentally-let-employees-access-each-others-keystroke-data/) after the data leaked internally. As [Alphabet replaces Verizon in the Dow](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/23/alphabet-verizon-dow-djia.html), money itself is going programmable, with Europe backing a [digital euro](https://www.euronews.com/business/2026/06/23/european-parliament-backs-long-awaited-digital-euro-to-reduce-us-dominance-in-payments) by 2029 to escape US payment rails and Meta building [a prediction-markets app, “Arena,”](https://www.nytimes.com/2026/06/23/technology/meta-prediction-markets-app.html) to let users forecast politics and sports for points.

The best way to predict the future is to invent a market for it.
