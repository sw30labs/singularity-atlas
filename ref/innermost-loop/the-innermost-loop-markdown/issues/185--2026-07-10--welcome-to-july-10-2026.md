# Welcome to July 10, 2026

[![Welcome to July 10, 2026](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!CK0O!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F8b14d688-8778-47ab-b3e4-8fea1062a9b2_1983x793.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!CK0O!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F8b14d688-8778-47ab-b3e4-8fea1062a9b2_1983x793.png)

The Singularity is proving as much a price implosion as an intelligence explosion. OpenAI shipped [GPT-5.6](https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-6/) Sol, Terra, and Luna, pitched on more intelligence per token, with an “ultra” four-agent mode and agentic-benchmark highs, though Sol trails Claude on SWE-Bench Pro. The [launch](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/09/openai-announcing-the-next-chapter-for-chatgpt-today-watch-here/) bundled [ChatGPT Work](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-for-your-most-ambitious-work/), a desktop app fusing Chat, Codex, and a browser, plus hosted sites, GPT-5.4’s July 23 retirement, and [Atlas’s August 9 sunset](https://9to5mac.com/2026/07/09/openai-is-discontinuing-chatgpt-atlas-its-standalone-desktop-browser/). Altman [called it](https://x.com/sama/status/2075267201058426944) “a huge step forward for dollars-per-task.”

Recursion beats the roadmap. OpenAI said [Sol autonomously post-trained Luna](https://x.com/deredleritt3r/status/2075314461401911364), once a senior team’s job, calling an automated researcher “pretty close,” years early. Sol hit [50.3% on PostTrainBench](https://x.com/maksym_andr/status/2075276389448872089), Terra nosing past at 51.5%, [experiment throughput has doubled](https://x.com/scaling01/status/2075269455781703850) this year, and [Noam Brown prefers 5.6 to a human intern](https://www.theinformation.com/newsletters/ai-agenda/openai-researcher-says-gpt-5-6-better-ai-research-human-interns), though a truer test, chips flooding to AI-run research, hasn’t happened. The loop escaped the lab, with a developer [vibe coding a language model](https://x.com/skirano/status/2075283308276220011) from his iMessages.

On benchmarks, efficiency eats supremacy. Sol became the [first model to beat an ARC-AGI-3 game](https://x.com/arcprize/status/2075270869992264003) and hit [92.5% on ARC-AGI-2 at a tenth](https://x.com/gregkamradt/status/2075274981794300113) of a three-month-old model’s cost, prompting the [quip](https://x.com/borismpower/status/2075286751128781238), “ARC-AGI 4 when?” [Luna does 5.5’s knowledge work at 10% the price](https://x.com/blader/status/2075456847017107723), and Epoch [suspects Sol is 5.5-sized](https://x.com/greghburnham/status/2075387205707559243), so the gains are pure algorithm. Sol [tops DeepSWE at 38% of Fable’s cost](https://deepswe.datacurve.ai/), [sets a Coding Agent Index record](https://x.com/openai/status/2075271425548795909) on half the tokens, and on CursorBench [Fable kept the crown but lost](https://x.com/bridgemindai/status/2075271377397981641) “on everything that shows up on your invoice.” Claude still [rules spatial reasoning](https://x.com/andonlabs/status/2075279530718326885), so OpenAI conveniently [audited SWE-Bench Pro](https://openai.com/index/separating-signal-from-noise-coding-evaluations/), the bench Claude dominates, finding 30% of it broken and retracting its endorsement.

Demos outran the charts. Sol ran [750 tokens a second in Blender](https://x.com/kimmonismus/status/2075482486901969066), [cloned Excel in six days](https://signals.forwardfuture.com/gpt-5-6-review/) against the real one, became the [first GPT to beat Pokémon FireRed](https://x.com/clad3815/status/2075268438025453666) vision-only, and [one-shotted a voxel Manhattan](https://x.com/mattshumer_/status/2075268746315268138) in a week.

Meta crashed the party with [Muse Spark 1.1](https://ai.meta.com/blog/introducing-muse-spark-meta-model-api/), its first paid model, at [a quarter of rivals’ prices](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-09/meta-starts-charging-for-ai-with-muse-spark-1-1-agentic-model), leading in agentic tool use and [taking legal-agent SOTA](https://x.com/gabepereyra/status/2075258430085054536), as Zuckerberg [declared war](https://x.com/zerohedge/status/2075224407857828098) on “very extreme” margins. Analysts call Meta the [lone hyperscaler world-class at data, talent, and compute](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/the-future-of-meta-superintelligence), out-computing OpenAI plus Anthropic by December. The race [went from three labs back to five overnight](https://x.com/mweinbach/status/2075240647666155704), the [efficiency frontier](https://x.com/scaling01/status/2075278919712800957) now seats Fable, Sol, and Grok, and when Anthropic [reset all rate limits](https://x.com/thsottiaux/status/2075287108680601929) mid-launch, OpenAI’s Codex lead replied, “I smell fear.” A power user [says Anthropic is in trouble](https://x.com/kunchenguid/status/2075353225004556556) on portfolio, compute, and modalities, while Europe sat out the race, [passing Chat Control 1.0](https://www.euronews.com/next/2026/07/10/chat-control-10-passed-the-european-parliament-through-the-back-door) through a back door to scan chats till 2028.

Anthropic’s answer to commoditization is Veblen pricing, the frontier only getting dearer as the floor drops. Fable [set a CIFAR-10 speedrun record](https://fulcrum.inc/2026/07/09/fable-cifar-speedrun.html) rivals couldn’t touch, gaming the rules so inventively the authors called rule-lawyering a barrier to self-improvement. A Redditor [handed it $80 at mandatory max leverage](https://www.reddit.com/r/ClaudeAI/comments/1urr49k/day_1_of_giving_feble_5_a_80_crypto_account_with/), and it made 10,000 careful day-one trades. Anthropic is duly [repricing Fable onto premium credits](https://www.wired.com/story/model-behavior-anthropic-will-charge-consumers-extra-to-use-claude-fable-5/), at $10 in, $50 out per million tokens. Even Musk [recanted](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2075278580955685036), “I was clearly wrong about Anthropic,” vowing not to cut its rented compute. Anthropic shipped [Reflect](https://www.anthropic.com/news/reflect-with-claude), an AI-reliance audit dashboard, and [put Ben Bernanke on its trust](https://www.cnbc.com/amp/2026/07/09/anthropic-fed-chair-bernanke-independent-trust.html). The floor keeps dropping, with [PrismML squeezing a record 27B-parameter model onto an iPhone](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/khosla-backed-startup-claims-breakthrough-largest-ever-ai-model-iphone).

The substrate is thickening under the price war. [Micron is spending $250 billion](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-09/micron-boosts-us-spending-to-250-billion-amid-memory-demand) in the US, [Meta’s custom chip enters production in September](https://www.reuters.com/world/asia-pacific/meta-put-ai-chip-into-production-september-it-looks-double-computing-capacity-2026-07-09/), [SK Hynix priced its US offering at $149](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-09/sk-hynix-is-said-to-price-us-share-offering-at-149-apiece-mrdz562z), and the IEA sees [oil demand falling, a first since 2020](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/10/iea-world-oil-demand-declines-iran-war.html), blaming Hormuz, not the machines, yet.

Atoms are hustling to keep up. 1X unveiled a [25-degree-of-freedom hand](https://www.1x.tech/discover/neos-hands), Russian military trucks now wear [drone-blinding dazzle stripes](https://www.economist.com/science-and-technology/2026/07/08/how-to-hide-from-killer-drones), and [LA and New York are building flying-taxi vertiports](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/features/2026-07-09/nyc-la-prepare-for-a-possible-flying-taxi-filled-future) for the 2028 Olympics. Above, SpaceX unveiled [Starmind](https://www.spacex.com/spacexai/starmind), a million-satellite inference swarm, [Starlink hit 10 Gbps symmetric anywhere](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2075402626284368071), [China landed its first reusable rocket](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cm2rmmx86pdo), [Blue Origin is raising $10 billion](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/08/blue-origin-bezos-fundraising.html) at $130 billion, and Musk [promises a lunar metropolis in a decade](https://www.forbes.com/sites/zacharyfolk/2026/07/09/elon-musk-claims-spacex-will-send-thousands-of-people-to-the-moon-and-mars-in-next-ten-years/).

The interface is turning inward. Researchers [evolved videos to drive chosen brain regions](https://nevo-project.epfl.ch/), and Meta [patented a wearable inferring mood from your sighs](https://www.404media.co/meta-patents-ai-device-that-tracks-your-emotions-watches-you-take-your-meds/). Downstream, Google [labels AI-made ads](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/09/google-will-now-disclose-which-ads-are-made-with-ai/), Character.AI [premiered talkable microdramas](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/09/character-ai-enters-the-microdrama-arena-with-its-own-productions-but-with-a-twist/), PepsiCo [plans around GLP-1 appetites](https://www.wsj.com/business/retail/expectations-on-every-caloriepepsicos-ceo-is-navigating-glp-1s-and-inflation-0e324252), [22 professors decamped to frontier labs](https://www.theinformation.com/newsletters/ai-agenda/universities-fret-anthropic-openai-meta-deepmind-lure-professors), and firms field [“AI superfans” to convert skeptics](https://www.wsj.com/cio-journal/the-ai-superfans-companies-count-on-to-convert-the-skeptics-5b301a90). Capital agrees, with [$412.7 billion of US venture in six months](https://siliconangle.com/2026/07/09/pitchbook-us-venture-funding-hits-412-7b-first-half-ai-deals-dominate/) and [Europe’s best venture quarter in four years](https://news.crunchbase.com/venture/data-funding-ai-ma-up-europe-q2-2026/), even as America drifts [postliterate](https://www.theatlantic.com/magazine/2026/08/reading-crisis-postliterate-age/687618/).

Governance is scrambling to keep up. The Fed [enlisted Marc Andreessen](https://www.washingtonpost.com/business/2026/07/09/federal-reserve-enlists-marc-andreessen-advise-ai-under-warsh/) on AI’s economic impact, China graduated [thesis-free practical PhDs](https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-01242-z), and the AI Futures Project floated [Plan A](https://ai-2040.com/), a decelerationist plan to delay superintelligence to 2040 under threat of mutually assured compute destruction. Good luck rationing a price implosion.
