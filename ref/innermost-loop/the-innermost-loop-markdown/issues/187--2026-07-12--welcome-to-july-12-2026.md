# Welcome to July 12, 2026

[![Welcome to July 12, 2026](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!CNsy!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F835f963b-4f60-4f4c-b652-aa25211f73e4_1983x793.png)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!CNsy!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F835f963b-4f60-4f4c-b652-aa25211f73e4_1983x793.png)

The Singularity is going to yeshiva. OpenAI’s Head of Strategic Futures has [realized](https://x.com/deanwball/status/2076029765631484042) that “there comes a time in every ai policy professional’s life when they realize they have to read the talmud to make further progress.” His colleague Roon [posted](https://x.com/tszzl/status/2075764068548489327) a Kabbalistic diagram of the Sefirot, crowned by Ein Sof, the Infinite, and earnestly captioned it “Lightcone Infrastructure.” The conviction is already a job description. Judd Rosenblatt is [hiring](https://x.com/juddrosenblatt/status/2069187141788475783) AI engineers versed in Jewish texts, on the thesis that a tradition that survived millennia of adverse selection may know something about surviving recursive self-improvement. A [profile](https://forward.com/culture/835118/ai-alignment-rsi-talmud-torah-judaism-halakha/) of his AI Alignment Foundation sharpens the point: the Talmud’s layered, self-referential reinterpretation preserves core values across civilizational resets, exactly what you want in an AI that could edit its values away.

The commentaries are multiplying faster than the canon. A leaked [“Honeycomb EAP”](https://x.com/luminaxspace/status/2076243903175446986) preview suggests Anthropic ships Claude Opus 5 by end of July with a 1M-token context window, and one forecaster [predicts](https://x.com/daniel_mac8/status/2075999850038956502) Fable 5 exits subscriptions but is replaced by an Opus 5 distilled from the unfiltered Mythos. OpenAI is answering prophecy with product. GPT-5.6 set a new bar on professional health benchmarks, and blinded physicians [found fewer flaws](https://x.com/thekaransinghal/status/2075689779937833302) in its answers than in specialty-matched doctors’ own, while the smallest variant, Luna, beats GPT-5.5’s best reasoning at 25x lower cost. Noam Brown [hints](https://x.com/polynoamial/status/2075647948906533297) the next scaling law is parallel test-time compute. Grok 4.5 [claimed #2](https://x.com/mercor_ai/status/2075792789690888640) on real-world software engineering, up 30 points in a year, though Fable 5 keeps the crown. A Meta researcher [concludes](https://x.com/ziv_ravid/status/2076148548270166349) there is no moat, just shared recipes and migrating researchers. Freshness is contested, a new [benchmark](https://x.com/apoorv_umang/status/2075646580083048945) finding only OpenAI and Anthropic keep their models’ knowledge cutoffs within a year of today. Meanwhile a new single-file C engine [colibrì](https://github.com/JustVugg/colibri) lets a consumer laptop host the 744B-parameter GLM-5.2 by keeping the dense layers resident in 25GB of RAM and streaming routed experts off disk on demand. METR-style task reliability is [doubling every 40 days](https://x.com/8teapi/status/2076056200446456293), on track for one model to outwork every human who ever lived by 2032, while Benedict Evans [argues](https://www.ben-evans.com/benedictevans/2026/7/9/ways-to-think-about-token-pricing) tokens are destined to be low-margin commodity infrastructure. Superintelligence too cheap to meter, arriving on schedule.

Commoditization at the model layer means war at the agent layer. Cursor, being bought by SpaceX at $60 billion, is [building](https://www.theinformation.com/briefings/cursor-working-competitor-claude-cowork) a general agent to challenge Claude Cowork, while Elon Musk [ordered](https://www.theinformation.com/briefings/exclusive-elon-musk-tells-tesla-staff-move-using-grok) Tesla staff onto Grok, citing token cost. The financing beneath it all appears [circular](https://io-fund.com/ai-stocks/nvidia-coreweave-nebius-circular-financing-gpu-boom): Nvidia invests in the neoclouds, sells them GPUs, and backstops their unsold capacity as capex outruns cash flow. No wonder chip stocks [whipsawed](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/12/ai-demand-chips-data-centers-stock-volatility.html) when Meta offered to sell excess compute, even as operators insist demand still swamps supply.

The labor market keeps ignoring the doomer script. Sam Altman is [pretty sure](https://x.com/sama/status/2076036901824532530) AI has been net job-creating so far, to his surprise. The jobs, though, are getting stranger. Factories now staff four-hour shifts through an [app](https://www.npr.org/2026/07/08/nx-s1-5876084/manufacturing-flexible-part-time-work) dubbed “the Uber of manufacturing,” a new federal [“do no harm” rule](https://www.npr.org/2026/06/30/nx-s1-5835631/turner-camhi-do-no-harm-college-loans) will cut student loans to degrees that don’t out-earn skipping college, and a Chinese voice actor must [repeatedly prove he is human](https://www.sixthtone.com/news/1018753) after platforms flagged his real voice as an AI clone of itself. JPMorgan claims its macro [agents](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-09/jpmorgan-builds-ai-agents-that-beat-60-40-portfolio-in-backtests) beat the 60/40 portfolio across two decades of backtests.

Not everyone is buying in. The Bay Area now anchors a hardening [anti-AI resistance](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/anti-ai-activists-disappearance-sam-kirchner-6872879f), still on edge over a Stop AI co-founder who mused “the ship may have sailed on nonviolence,” then vanished. And the watchers are being watched: UK shops’ facial recognition will soon [alert police](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/jul/10/facewatch-facial-recognition-uk-shops-instantly-alerts-police-civil-liberties) within four seconds of a match.

Overhead, construction continues. Starship Flight 13 [targets Thursday](https://x.com/sawyermerritt/status/2076076086908027387) and will [deploy](https://x.com/sawyermerritt/status/2076079355890212972) the first 20 laser-linked Starlink V3 satellites, six of them camera-equipped to inspect the heat shield. Altman is [unimpressed](https://x.com/sama/status/2075982617976230043) by Musk’s Dyson Swarm: “homeboy you’re the one sellling public market investors on short-term space datacenters.” Those datacenters now face an [FCC petition](https://www.theregister.com/on-prem/2026/07/10/orbital-datacenter-gold-rush-needs-an-environmental-review-fcc-told/5269863) from environmental groups arguing that a million-plus proposed satellites demand a cumulative review of their effects, from ozone layer to orbital debris, before any licensing. The frontier presses on, nonetheless. Longshot’s kinetic accelerator [joined](https://interestingengineering.com/military/usaf-picks-longshot-to-test-hypersonic-tech) the Air Force’s hypersonic testing alliance, and the first kidney and liver tissues were [bioprinted](https://www.mddionline.com/3d-printing/first-kidney-liver-tissues-bioprinted-in-space-mark-major-breakthrough-in-orbital-biomanufacturing) aboard the ISS.

Someone else may have noticed the buildout. Dr. Phil, claiming White House early access to the historic fourth PURSUE declassification, [reports](https://x.com/drphil/status/2075588746515505626) the government’s own files show UAPs loitering over nuclear facilities for 77 years, and [adds](https://x.com/DrPhil/status/2075641008352628765), “This should bother you.” More strikingly, some analysts [conclude](https://x.com/jordanflowers/status/2076108095689003253) December’s UAP incursions over the Southern US were a “very successful” government luring operation, briefed to the Gang of 8.

Turns out if you build it, they really will come.
