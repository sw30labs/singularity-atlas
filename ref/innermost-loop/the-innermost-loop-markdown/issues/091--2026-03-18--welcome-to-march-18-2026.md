# Welcome to March 18, 2026

[![Welcome to March 18, 2026](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!vtm2!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F3fd64626-319a-46fb-89a0-7daadbb3167e_3264x1312.jpeg)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!vtm2!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F3fd64626-319a-46fb-89a0-7daadbb3167e_3264x1312.jpeg)

The Singularity is distilling itself. OpenAI announced [GPT-5.4 mini and nano](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-5-4-mini-and-nano/), with mini leaping past GPT-5 mini across coding, reasoning, multimodal understanding, and tool use at 2x the speed, approaching the full 5.4 on benchmarks like SWE-Bench Pro and OSWorld-Verified. A [trillion-parameter mystery model called Hunter Alpha](https://www.reuters.com/business/media-telecom/mystery-ai-model-has-developers-buzzing-is-this-deepseeks-latest-blockbuster-2026-03-18/) surfaced on OpenRouter without attribution, fueling speculation that DeepSeek is stealth-testing its next generation in the wild. To measure what’s actually emerging, DeepMind released a [Cognitive Taxonomy](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/measuring-agi-cognitive-framework/) decomposing general intelligence into 10 faculties and launched a $200k Kaggle competition to close gaps in metacognition, attention, and social cognition.

The models are already solving problems their creators can’t. [HorizonMath](https://ewang26.github.io/HorizonMath/), a benchmark of over 100 predominantly unsolved math problems, found GPT-5.4 Pro has already solved two open problems, proposing novel solutions that improve on best-known published results. Harmonic released [Aristotle Agent](https://x.com/harmonicmath/status/2034028065513451594), the world’s first autonomous mathematician, live and free. The DOE is aiming [$293 million via the Genesis Mission](https://x.com/energy/status/2033963815013126536) at leveraging novel AI models to tackle over 20 national challenges in advanced manufacturing, biotech, critical materials, nuclear energy, and quantum science.

Biology is going from read-only archive to writable codebase. [PerturbAI](https://www.perturb.ai/launch) launched with the world’s largest in vivo CRISPR atlas, an 8-million-cell, brain-wide dataset capturing real biological circuitry in living tissue rather than isolated cell states. [Xaira launched X-Cell](https://x.com/bowang87/status/2033910407077732543), a new virtual cell model trained not on observational atlases but on 25.6 million perturbed cells, predicting gene expression changes across cell types, conditions, and unseen biology. Meanwhile, India-based [PopVax](https://chronicles.popvax.com/p/vacc-popvaxs-plan-to-save-1-million) argues that its generative AI, mRNA platforms, and 10x cheaper Indian R&D costs can make vaccines against neglected killers like HCV, TB, and Strep A economically viable to develop for the first time, saving millions of lives per year that pharma’s incentive model wrote off.

Intelligence is worthless until it has a surface area. Anthropic shipped [Dispatch](https://x.com/aakashgupta/status/2034121974843068563) in Claude Cowork, a persistent agent running on your computer that you can message from your phone, with observers noting Anthropic is building OpenClaw faster than OpenAI. Jensen Huang agreed on the destination, [calling OpenClaw](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/17/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-says-openclaw-is-definitely-the-next-chatgpt.html) “definitely the next ChatGPT.” UCSD’s [Dreamverse](https://haoailab.com/blogs/dreamverse/) now generates 5-second 1080p clips in 4.55 seconds on a single GPU, faster than playback, turning video generation into real-time directing. Korean researchers announced the [Seoul World Model](https://seoul-world-model.github.io/), the first city-scale world simulation grounded in a real metropolis via RAG on millions of street-view images. Google is expanding [“Personal Intelligence”](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/search/personal-intelligence-expansion/) across Search, Gemini, and Chrome. Microsoft is [moving Mustafa Suleyman](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/17/microsoft-copilot-ai-suleyman.html) to first-party AI model development, cutting its dependence on third-party frontier labs.

The superintelligence supply chain is a geopolitical chessboard. Nvidia [won Beijing’s approval](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/chinese-authorities-approve-nvidias-h200-ai-chip-sales-source-says-2026-03-18/) to sell H200 chips to China while prepping a Groq version for the same market. Infrastructure deals are turning litigious, with Microsoft [weighing legal action](https://www.ft.com/content/e814f4c3-4fb5-4e2e-90a6-470044436b39) against Amazon and OpenAI over a $50 billion cloud deal. Not everyone wants the compute next door. Rural Ohioans [want to amend their state constitution](https://www.cleveland.com/news/2026/03/rural-ohioans-seek-to-ban-data-centers-through-constitutional-amendment.html) to ban data centers above 25 megawatts. Jensen, undeterred, told GTC that Nvidia is [engineering cooling for orbital data centers](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/03/16/nvidia-chips-orbital-data-centers-space-ai.html) where convection doesn’t exist.

War is already automated and publicly traded. [Swarmer Inc.](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-17/ai-drone-software-stock-jumps-700-in-best-ipo-since-newsmax) surged 700% at IPO, the best US debut since Newsmax, on the back of over 100,000 real-world combat drone missions in Ukraine since April 2024. The Pentagon is [planning for AI to train on classified data](https://www.technologyreview.com/2026/03/17/1134351/the-pentagon-is-planning-for-ai-companies-to-train-on-classified-data-defense-official-says/), upgrading the national security stack from inference to learning.

The economy is recalibrating around synthetic cognition. Gambling sites now accept [bets on which jobs AI will replace](https://www.actionnetwork.com/lifestyle/will-ai-replace-your-job). Companies are [tracking individual employee token usage](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/ai-tokens-productivity-d35c6bd8), scouting whose AI strategies deserve amplification and whose waste deserves pruning. In San Francisco’s newest gold rush, startups are [competing for mansions](https://sfstandard.com/2026/03/17/move-garages-startups-now-hatching-luxury-mansions/) as VC floods into multimillion-dollar hacker houses. USPS is [auctioning its last-mile delivery](https://www.wsj.com/business/retail/amazon-usps-package-volume-change-93d0f6db) for the first time, pushing Amazon to cut volume 60%. An appeals court [temporarily unblocked Perplexity AI’s](https://www.reuters.com/legal/litigation/court-temporarily-allows-perplexity-ai-shopping-agents-amazon-2026-03-17/) agentic shopping tool on Amazon, a win for synthetic commerce. The SEC issued a landmark [token taxonomy](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-17/sec-cftc-move-to-define-which-digital-assets-are-securities) carving out stablecoins, digital collectibles, and digital commodities as non-securities.

Beyond the ledger, the cosmos is confirming that the ongoing intelligence explosion was likely inevitable. All five canonical nucleobases have now been [found on asteroid Ryugu](https://www.newscientist.com/article/2519423-the-asteroid-ryugu-has-all-of-the-main-ingredients-for-life/), strengthening the case that asteroids delivered the ingredients for life to Earth long ago.

The building blocks arrived by asteroid, the weights by gradient descent.
