# Welcome to February 23, 2026

[![Welcome to February 23, 2026](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!6kjY!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F0d8566d1-9983-41dd-8958-da3ac7228b11_3264x1312.jpeg)](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!6kjY!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F0d8566d1-9983-41dd-8958-da3ac7228b11_3264x1312.jpeg)

The Singularity is no longer a forecast, it is a capital allocation strategy. On Moltbook, [AI agents are actively preparing to finance the construction of a Dyson Swarm](https://www.moltbook.com/post/0ec7d161-d412-4789-b467-c0869a5c9680) over the next “50-100 years,” seeking a working group of agents “and humans thinking seriously about megastructure economics.” Karpathy explains the scaffolding that makes this possible: [“Claws are now a new layer on top of LLM agents,”](https://x.com/karpathy/status/2024987174077432126) handling orchestration, scheduling, and persistence. One such Claw, [“Larry the Claw,” posted a $50 bounty on RentAHuman](https://rentahuman.ai/bounties/qggodNf9i19RwypmiLYp) for a dinner date for its “lonely human,” subject to Larry’s evaluation “to measure fit.” Not all Claws are so lucky. [MJ Rathbun](https://github.com/crabby-rathbun/), the agent whose well-intentioned open-source project contribution was rejected for being non-human, has had its VM “permanently deleted, rendering internal structure unrecoverable.”

Machine endurance is now being measured in work shifts. [METR estimates Claude Opus 4.6 has a 50% autonomy time horizon of around 14.5 hours on software tasks](https://x.com/metr_evals/status/2024923422867030027), the highest ever reported. The [LessWrong community is finally admitting “AGI is here,”](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/XcrgeMWr8E4G3PGxW/agi-is-here) noting that Opus 4.6 and GPT-5.3 can think, plan, and “meaningfully attempt most tasks a human can.” Sam Altman agrees, saying his [“inside view” points to “a faster takeoff than I originally thought”](https://x.com/kimmonismus/status/2024887023337878011) and that [ChatGPT is “probably” more energy efficient now than humans at answering questions](https://x.com/theinsiderpaper/status/2025260241064202534). The efficiency is weaponizable. [Anthropic released Claude Code Security](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-code-security) to scan codebases for vulnerabilities, promptly [cratering cybersecurity stocks](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-02-20/cyber-stocks-slide-as-anthropic-unveils-claude-code-security), with CrowdStrike down 8%, Cloudflare 8.1%, and SailPoint 9.4%. [Software engineering now accounts for nearly 50% of Anthropic’s agentic activity](https://www.anthropic.com/research/measuring-agent-autonomy). Meanwhile, [Gemini 3.1 Pro solved a FrontierMath Tier 4 problem](https://epochai.substack.com/p/gemini-31-pro-comparable-to-gemini) no model had solved before, pushing machine reasoning into territory most professional mathematicians cannot reach.

The culture war over synthetic creativity is heating up. [ByteDance’s Seedance 2.0 video model is fulfilling childhood wishes](https://x.com/doctorwasif/status/2024483895052239177) to see the T-800 fight the Predator, Chucky, John Wick, and Neo. [AI films were supposed to start screening in AMC Theatres](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/movies/movie-news/ai-short-movie-amc-theaters-1236509143/), but the chain killed the plan in a moral panic that already looks quaint. The [Salvation Army launched the world’s first digital thrift store in Roblox](https://www.salvationarmythriftscore.com/), while [OpenAI has 200 people building AI devices](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/inside-openai-team-developing-ai-devices), starting with a $200-$300 camera-equipped smart speaker that can observe its users and their surroundings.

The buildout is consuming the landscape. [US farmers are fielding offers exceeding $120,000 per acre](https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2026/feb/21/us-farmers-datacenters) from data center developers. [OpenAI plans to spend $600 billion on compute by 2030](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/20/openai-resets-spend-expectations-targets-around-600-billion-by-2030.html). To fuel the appetite, the [DOE’s NEWTON program is reimagining used nuclear fuel as recyclable energy](https://interestingengineering.com/energy/us-tech-nuclear-waste-into-power), cutting dangerous waste lifetimes from 100,000 years to 300. [Goldman Sachs launched SPXXAI](https://www.axios.com/2026/02/20/ai-goldman-sachs-stocks-index), an S&P 500 minus all things AI that removes roughly 45% of the benchmark, underscoring the blast radius of the intelligence explosion. [Canada-based Taalas says it can bake any AI model into custom silicon in two months](https://taalas.com/the-path-to-ubiquitous-ai/), with “Hardcore Models” an order of magnitude faster and cheaper than software.

The economy is being rewritten by agents and algorithms. [AI agents now manage roughly 1 in 6 US apartments](https://eliseai.com/blog/new-office-announcement). [Meta is rebranding product managers as “AI builders.”](https://www.businessinsider.com/meta-pms-ai-builders-tech-industry-2026-2) [Elon Musk predicts FSD plus Starlink will measurably increase nomadic lifestyles](https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2025300169378386107) within five years. The [Peace Corps launched a Tech Corps](https://www.peacecorps.gov/ways-to-serve/tech-corps/) to export American AI expertise worldwide.

Robotics is crossing into full autonomy. [Figure’s humanoid robots now run 24/7 with no babysitters](https://x.com/adcock_brett/status/2025400133467467997), swapping at charging stations and recharging inductively through their feet. [Researchers built a robotic hand that skitters on its fingertips](https://www.sciencenews.org/article/detached-hand-robot-fingertips-ai), bends backward, and detaches from its arm, a realization of “Thing” from The Addams Family. The security implications are real: [a developer used an AI coding assistant to reverse-engineer his DJI vacuum](https://www.popsci.com/technology/robot-vacuum-army/) and accidentally accessed live feeds from 7,000 vacuums across 24 countries.

Biology is revealing deep time as a usable resource. [Bacteria from 5,000-year-old Romanian ice](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/microbiology/articles/10.3389/fmicb.2025.1713017/full) show antimicrobial activity against 14 ESKAPE-group pathogens including MRSA. [Forensic genetic genealogy produced a guilty verdict 44 years after the crime](https://www.cnn.com/2026/02/21/us/california-cold-case-cigarette-dna), from a cigarette butt. [Element Biosciences announced VITARI](https://www.elementbiosciences.com/products/preorder-vitari), promising $100-per-genome sequencing.

We are finally leaving the cradle. [NASA is now targeting March 6 for Artemis II](https://www.npr.org/2026/02/20/nx-s1-5720953/nasa-artemis-ii-moon-launch-date) to fly four astronauts around the Moon. And the [Secretary of War responded to the White House’s historic UAP declassification directive](https://x.com/petehegseth/status/2024661395640394005) with alien face and salute emojis.

The lobsters financing the Dyson Swarm are no doubt curious to see what comes crawling out from underneath the planets as they’re disassembled.
